### INTRODUCTION 引言

2.1. From the time of the first discovery of the large amounts of energy released in nuclear reactions to the time of the discovery of uranium fission, the idea of atomic power or even atomic bombs was discussed off and on in scientific circles. The discovery of fission made this talk seem much less speculative, but realization of atomic power still seemed in the distant future and there was an instinctive feeling among many scientists that it might not, in fact, ever be realized. During 1939 and 1940 many public statements, some of them by responsible scientists, called attention to the enormous energy available in uranium for explosives and for controlled power, so that U-235 became a familiar byword indicating great things to come. The possible military importance of uranium fission was called to the attention of the government (see [Chapter III](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_iii.html)), and in a conference with representatives of the Navy Department in March 1939 Fermi suggested the possibility of achieving a controllable reaction using slow neutrons or a reaction of an explosive character using fast neutrons. He pointed out, however, that the data then available might be insufficient for accurate predictions.

从首次发现核反应释放大量能量之时起，直至发现铀裂变之时止，科学界一直时断时续地讨论着原子能乃至原子弹的设想。裂变的发现使这类谈论显得远不那么空泛，但原子能的实现似乎仍遥遥无期，许多科学家本能地感到，它也许实际上永远无法实现。1939年和1940年间，许多公开声明——其中一些出自负有责任的科学家——提请人们注意，铀中蕴藏着可供爆炸物和受控动力使用的巨大能量，于是铀-235成为一个家喻户晓的代名词，预示着重大事物的到来。铀裂变可能的军事重要性被提请政府注意（见[第三章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_iii.html)），并且在1939年3月与海军部代表的会议上，费米提出，有可能利用慢中子实现可控反应，或利用快中子实现爆炸性反应。然而他指出，当时可获得的数据或许不足以作出准确的预测。

2.2. By the summer of 1940 it was possible to formulate the problem fairly clearly, although it was still far from possible to answer the various questions involved or even to decide whether a chain reaction ever could be obtained. In this chapter we shall give a statement of the problem in its entirety. For purposes of clarification we may make use of some knowledge which actually was not acquired until a later date.

到1940年夏，已经有可能相当清晰地陈述这一问题，尽管仍远不能回答其中涉及的各项问题，甚至不能断定究竟能否获得链式反应。本章将完整地陈述这一问题。为便于说明，我们可能使用一些实际上直到较晚时期才获得的知识。

### THE CHAIN-REACTION PROBLEM 链式反应问题

2.3. The principle of operation of an atomic bomb or power plant utilizing uranium fission is simple enough. If one neutron causes a fission that produces more than one new neutron, the number of fissions may increase tremendously with the release of enormous amounts of energy. It is a question of probabilities. Neutrons produced in the fission process may escape entirely from the uranium, may be captured by uranium in a process not resulting in fission, or may be captured by an impurity. Thus the question of whether a chain reaction does or does not go depends on the result of a competition among four processes:

利用铀裂变的原子弹或动力装置，其运行原理相当简单。如果一个中子引起一次裂变并产生一个以上的新中子，那么裂变次数就可能剧增，同时释放出巨大的能量。这是一个概率问题。裂变过程中产生的中子可能完全逃逸出铀，可能被铀以不导致裂变的过程俘获，也可能被杂质俘获。因此，链式反应是否能够持续，取决于四种过程之间竞争的结果：

> (1) escape,
> (2) non-fission capture by uranium,
> (3) non-fission capture by impurities,
> (4) fission capture.

> (1) 逃逸，
> (2) 被铀的非裂变俘获，
> (3) 被杂质的非裂变俘获，
> (4) 裂变俘获。

If the loss of neutrons by the first three processes is less than the surplus produced by the fourth, the chain reaction occurs; otherwise it does not. Evidently any one of the first three processes may have such a high probability in a given arrangement that the extra neutrons created by fission will be insufficient to keep the reaction going. For example, should it turn out that process (2) non-fission capture by uranium - has a much higher probability than fission capture, there would presumably be no possibility of achieving a chain reaction.

如果前三种过程造成的中子损失小于第四种过程产生的盈余，链式反应就会发生；否则就不会发生。显然，在特定布置中，前三种过程中的任何一种都可能具有如此高的概率，以致裂变产生的额外中子不足以维持反应继续进行。例如，倘若结果表明过程(2)——被铀的非裂变俘获——的概率远高于裂变俘获，那么大概就不存在实现链式反应的可能性。

2.4. An additional complication is that natural uranium contains three isotopes: U-234, U-235, and U-238, present to the extent of approximately 0.006, 0.7, and 99.3 per cent, respectively. We have already seen that the probabilities of processes (2) and (4) are different for different isotopes. We have also seen that the probabilities are different for neutrons of different energies.

还有一个额外的复杂因素：天然铀含有三种同位素，即铀-234、铀-235和铀-238，其丰度分别约为0.006%、0.7%和99.3%。我们已经看到，过程(2)和过程(4)的概率因同位素不同而异。我们也已经看到，这些概率还因中子能量不同而异。

2.5. We shall now consider the limitations imposed by the first three processes and how their effects can be minimized.

下面我们将讨论前三种过程所施加的限制，以及如何将其影响降至最低。

### NEUTRON ESCAPE; CRITICAL SIZE 中子逃逸；临界尺寸

2.6. The relative number of neutrons which escape from a quantity of uranium can be minimized by changing the size and shape. In a sphere any surface effect is proportional to square of the radius, and any volume effect is proportional to the cube of the radius. Now the escape of neutrons from a quantity of uranium is a surface effect depending on the area of the surface, but fission capture occurs throughout the material and is therefore a volume effect. Consequently the greater the amount of uranium, the less probable it is that neutron escape will predominate over fission capture and prevent a chain reaction. Loss of neutrons by non-fission capture is a volume effect like neutron production by fission capture, so that increase in size makes no change in its relative importance.

通过改变尺寸和形状，可以使一定量铀中逃逸中子的相对数量降至最低。在球体中，任何表面效应与半径的平方成正比，任何体积效应与半径的立方成正比。中子的逃逸是一种表面效应，取决于表面积；而裂变俘获在整个材料中发生，因此是一种体积效应。所以，铀的量越大，中子逃逸相对于裂变俘获占优势并阻止链式反应的可能性就越小。非裂变俘获造成的中子损失与裂变俘获产生的中子一样，都是体积效应，因此增大尺寸不会改变其相对重要性。

2.7. The critical size of a device containing uranium is defined as the size for which the production of free neutrons by fission is just equal to their loss by escape and by non-fission capture. In other words, if the size is smaller than critical, then by definition no chain reaction will sustain itself. In principle it was possible in 1940 to calculate the critical size, but in practice the uncertainty of the constants involved was so great that the various estimates differed widely. It seemed not improbable that the critical size might be too large for practical purposes. Even now estimates for untried arrangements vary somewhat from time to time as new information becomes available.

含铀装置的临界尺寸定义为：裂变产生的自由中子恰好等于因逃逸和非裂变俘获而损失的中子时的尺寸。换言之，若尺寸小于临界尺寸，则按定义没有任何链式反应能够自行维持。原则上，1940年就有可能计算临界尺寸，但在实践中，所涉常数的不确定性极大，以致各种估算结果相去甚远。当时看来，临界尺寸有可能大到不切实际的程度，这并非不可能。即使在今天，对于未曾试验过的布置，估算值仍会随着新资料的获得而时有变化。

### USE OF A MODERATOR TO REDUCE NON-FISSION CAPTURE 利用慢化剂减少非裂变俘获

2.8. In Chapter I we said that thermal neutrons have the highest probability of producing fission of U-235 but we also said that the neutrons emitted in the process of fission had high speeds. Evidently it was an oversimplification to say that the chain reaction might maintain itself if more neutrons were created by fission than were absorbed. For the probability both of fission capture and of non-fission capture depends on the speed of the neutrons. Unfortunately, the speed at which non-fission capture is most probable is intermediate between the average speed of neutrons emitted in the fission process and the speed at which fission capture is most probable.

第一章中我们说过，热中子引起铀-235裂变的概率最高；但我们也说过，裂变过程中发射的中子速度很高。显然，说只要裂变产生的中子多于被吸收的中子链式反应就能自行维持，这是一种过度简化。因为裂变俘获和非裂变俘获的概率都取决于中子的速度。不幸的是，非裂变俘获概率最高的速度，介于裂变过程中发射的中子的平均速度与裂变俘获概率最高的速度之间。

2.9. For some years before the discovery of fission, the customary way of slowing down neutrons was to cause them to pass through material of low atomic weight, such as hydrogenous material. The process of slowing down or moderation is simply one of elastic collisions between high-speed particles and particles practically at rest. The more nearly identical the masses of neutron and struck particle the greater the loss of kinetic energy by the neutron. Therefore the light elements are most effective as ”moderators,” i.e., slowing down agents, for neutrons.

在发现裂变之前的若干年里，减慢中子速度的惯常做法是让中子穿过低原子量的物质，例如含氢物质。减慢或慢化的过程，不过是高速粒子与实际上静止的粒子之间发生弹性碰撞的过程。中子与被撞击粒子的质量越接近，中子损失的动能就越大。因此，轻元素作为中子的“慢化剂”（即减速剂）最为有效。

2.10. It occurred to a number of physicists that it might be possible to mix uranium with a moderator in such a way that the high-speed fission neutrons, after being ejected from uranium and before re-encountering uranium nuclei, would have their speeds reduced below the speeds for which non-fission capture is highly probable. Evidently the characteristics of a good moderator are that it should be of low atomic weight and that it should have little or no tendency to absorb neutrons. Lithium and boron are excluded on the latter count. Helium is difficult to use because it is a gas and forms no compounds. The choice of moderator therefore lay among hydrogen, deuterium, beryllium, and carbon. Even now no one of these substances can be excluded from the list of practical possibilities. It was E. Fermi and L. Szilard who proposed the use of graphite as a moderator for a chain reaction.

许多物理学家想到，或许可以将铀与慢化剂混合，使高速裂变中子在逸出铀之后、再次遇到铀核之前，其速度降至非裂变俘获概率很高的速度以下。显然，良好慢化剂的特征是原子量低，并且吸收中子的倾向很小或没有。锂和硼因后一条件而被排除。氦难以使用，因为它是气体，且不形成化合物。因此，慢化剂的选择范围限于氢、氘、铍和碳。即使在今天，也不能将这些物质中的任何一种排除在实际可能性之外。正是E.费米（E. Fermi）和L.西拉德（L. Szilard）提出用石墨作为链式反应的慢化剂。

### USE OF A LATTICE TO REDUCE NON-FISSION CAPTURE 利用栅格减少非裂变俘获

2.11. The general scheme of using a moderator mixed with the uranium was pretty obvious. A specific manner of using a moderator was first suggested in this country, so far as we can discover, by Fermi and Szilard. The idea was to use lumps of uranium of considerable size imbedded in a matrix of moderator material. Such a lattice can be shown to have real advantages over a homogeneous mixture. As the constants were more accurately determined, it became possible to calculate theoretically the type of lattice that would be most effective.

将慢化剂与铀混合使用的大体方案是相当明显的。据我们所知，在美国，费米和西拉德最先提出了使用慢化剂的具体方式。其设想是使用相当尺寸的铀块，将其嵌入慢化剂材料的基体之中。可以证明，这种栅格比均匀混合物具有实际优势。随着常数被更准确地测定，就有可能从理论上计算出最有效的栅格类型。

### REDUCTION OF NON-FISSION CAPTURE BY ISOTOPE SEPARATION 通过同位素分离减少非裂变俘获

2.12. In [Chapter I](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_i-a.html) it was stated that for neutrons of certain intermediate speeds (corresponding to energies of a few electron volts) U-238 has a large capture cross section for the production of U-239 but not for fission. There is also a considerable probability of inelastic (i.e., non-capture-producing) collisions between high-speed neutrons and U-238 nuclei. Thus the presence of the U-238 tends both to reduce the speed of the fast neutrons and to effect the capture of those of moderate speed. Although there may be some non-fission capture by U-235, it is evident that if we can separate the U-235 from the U-238 and discard the U-238, we can reduce non-fission capture and can thus promote the chain reaction. In fact, the probability of fission of U-235 by high-speed neutrons may be great enough to make the use of a moderator unnecessary once the U-238 has been removed. Unfortunately, U-235 is present in natural uranium only to the extent of about one part in 140. Also, the relatively small difference in mass between the two isotopes makes separation difficult. In fact, in 1940 no large-scale separation of isotopes had ever been achieved except for hydrogen, whose two isotopes differ in mass by a factor of two. Nevertheless, the possibility of separating U-235 was recognized early as being of the greatest importance, and such separation has, in fact, been one of the two major lines of Project effort during the past five years.

第一章中曾指出，对于某些中等速度的中子（相应能量为几个电子伏特），铀-238具有很大的俘获截面，可生成铀-239，但裂变截面不大。高速中子与铀-238核之间发生非弹性（即不产生俘获的）碰撞也有相当大的概率。因此，铀-238的存在既会降低快中子的速度，又会促成对中等速度中子的俘获。虽然铀-235也可能发生某些非裂变俘获，但显然，如果我们能把铀-235与铀-238分离并弃去铀-238，就能减少非裂变俘获，从而促进链式反应。事实上，铀-235被高速中子裂变的概率可能高到足以在去除铀-238之后无需使用慢化剂。不幸的是，铀-235在天然铀中仅占约一百四十分之一。此外，两种同位素之间相对较小的质量差异使分离十分困难。事实上，1940年时，除氢以外（氢的两种同位素质量相差一倍），从未实现过任何同位素的大规模分离。尽管如此，人们早就认识到分离铀-235的可能性具有极大重要性，而事实上，这种分离一直是过去五年中本工程两条主要工作路线之一。

### PRODUCTION AND PURIFICATION OF MATERIALS 材料的生产与提纯

2.13. It has been stated above that the cross section for capture of neutrons varies greatly among different materials. In some it is very high compared to the maximum fission cross section of uranium. If, then, we are to hope to achieve a chain reaction, we must reduce effect (3) non-fission capture by impurities - to the point where it is not serious. This means very careful purification of the uranium metal and very careful purification !of the moderator. Calculations show that the maximum permissible concentrations of many impurity elements are a few parts per millionQ in either the uranium or the moderator. When it is recalled that up to 1940 the total amount of uranium metal produced in this country was not more than a few grams and even this was of doubtful purity, that the total amount of metallic beryllium produced in this country was not more than a few pounds, that the total amount of concentrated deuterium produced was not more than a few pounds, and that carbon had never been produced in quantity with anything like the purity required of a moderator, it is clear that the problem of producing and purifying materials was a major one.

上文已经指出，不同材料俘获中子的截面差异很大。在某些材料中，这一截面相对于铀的最大裂变截面非常高。因此，如果我们希望实现链式反应，就必须把效应(3)——杂质引起的非裂变俘获——降低到不再严重的程度。这意味着要对铀金属和慢化剂进行非常仔细的提纯。计算表明，无论对铀还是对慢化剂，许多杂质元素的最大允许浓度均为百万分之几。回想一下，直到1940年，我国生产的铀金属总量不超过几克，而且即使这几克的纯度也值得怀疑；我国生产的金属铍总量不超过几磅；生产的浓缩氘总量不超过几磅；而碳从未以慢化剂所要求的纯度大量生产过。由此可见，材料的生产与提纯是一个重大问题。

### CONTROL OF THE CHAIN REACTION 链式反应的控制

2.14. The problems that have been discussed so far have to do merely with the realization of the chain reaction. If such a reaction is going to be of use, we must be able to control it. The problem of control is different depending on whether we are interested in steady production of power or in an explosion. In general, the steady production of atomic power requires a slow neutron-induced fission chain reaction occurring in a mixture or lattice of uranium and moderator, while an atomic bomb requires a fast-neutron-induced fission chain reaction in U-235 or Pu-239, although both slow- and fast-neutron fission may contribute in each case. It seemed likely, even in 1940, that by using neutron absorbers a power chain reaction could be controlled. It was also considered likely, though not certain, that such a chain reaction would be self-limiting by virtue of the lower probability of fission-producing capture when a higher temperature was reached. Nevertheless, there was a possibility that a chain-reacting system might get out of control, and it therefore seemed necessary to perform the chain-reaction experiment in an uninhabited location.

迄今所讨论的问题仅涉及链式反应的实现。如果这种反应要派上用场，我们必须能够控制它。控制问题因我们关注的是稳定的动力生产还是爆炸而有所不同。一般而言，稳定的原子动力生产需要在铀与慢化剂的混合物或栅格中发生由慢中子引起的裂变链式反应，而原子弹则需要铀-235或钚-239中由快中子引起的裂变链式反应，尽管在每种情况下慢中子和快中子裂变都可能有所贡献。即使在1940年，看来也有可能通过使用中子吸收剂来控制动力链式反应。当时还认为（尽管并不确定），这种链式反应会因温度升高时产生裂变的俘获概率降低而自行限制。然而，链式反应系统存在失控的可能性，因此似乎有必要在人迹罕至的地点进行链式反应实验。

### PRACTICAL APPLICATION OF THE CHAIN REACTION 链式反应的实际应用

2.15. Up to this point we have been discussing how to produce and control a nuclear chain reaction but not how to make use of it. The technological gap between producing a controlled chain reaction and using it as a large-scale power source or a explosive is comparable to the gap between the discovery of fire and the manufacture of a steam locomotive.

至此我们一直在讨论如何产生和控制核链式反应，而未讨论如何利用它。从产生受控链式反应到将其用作大规模动力源或爆炸物之间的技术差距，堪比从发现火到制造蒸汽机车之间的差距。

2.16. Although production of power has never been the principal object of this project, enough attention has been given to the matter to reveal the major difficulty: the attainment of high-temperature operation. An effective heat engine must not only develop heat but must develop heat at a high temperature. To run a chain-reacting system at a high temperature and to convert the heat generated to useful work is very much more difficult than to run a chain-reacting system at a low temperature.

尽管动力生产从来不是本工程的主要目标，但对这一问题的关注已足以揭示其主要困难：实现高温运行。一台有效的热机不仅要产生热量，而且要在高温下产生热量。使链式反应系统在高温下运行，并将产生的热量转化为有用功，要比使链式反应系统在低温下运行困难得多。

2.17. Of course, the proof that a chain reaction is possible does not itself insure that nuclear energy can be effective in a bomb. To have an effective explosion it is necessary that the chain reaction build up extremely rapidly; otherwise only a small amount of the nuclear energy will be utilized before the bomb flies apart and the reaction stops. It is also necessary that no premature explosion occur. This entire ”detonation” problem was and still remains one of the most difficult problems in designing a high-efficiency atomic bomb.

当然，链式反应可行的证明本身并不能保证核能可以有效地用于炸弹。要产生有效的爆炸，链式反应必须极其迅速地增长；否则，在炸弹飞散、反应停止之前，只能利用一小部分核能。此外，还必须不发生过早爆炸。整个“起爆”问题过去是、现在仍然是设计高效原子弹时最困难的问题之一。

### POSSIBILITY OF USING PLUTONIUM 使用钚的可能性

2.18. So far, all our discussion has been primarily concerned with the use of uranium itself. We have already mentioned the suggestion that the element of atomic number 94 and mass 239, commonly referred to as plutonium, might be very effective. Actually, we now believe it to be of value comparable to pure U-235. We have mentioned the difficulty of separating U-235 from the more abundant isotope U-238. These two isotopes are, of course, chemically identical. But plutonium, although produced from U-238, is a different chemical element. Therefore, if a process could be worked out for converting some of the U-238 to plutonium, a chemical separation of the plutonium from uranium might prove more practicable than the isotopic separation of U-235 from U-238.

到目前为止，我们的讨论主要涉及铀本身的使用。我们已经提到过这样一种设想：原子序数为94、质量为239的元素（通常称为钚）可能非常有效。事实上，我们现在认为其价值可与纯铀-235相比。我们已经提到过将铀-235与更丰富的同位素铀-238分离的困难。这两种同位素在化学上当然是相同的。但是，钚虽然由铀-238生成，却是一种不同的化学元素。因此，如果能研究出一种将部分铀-238转化为钚的方法，那么用化学方法将钚与铀分离，可能比将铀-235与铀-238进行同位素分离更为可行。

2.19. Suppose that we have set up a controllable chain reaction in a lattice of natural uranium and a moderator, say carbon, in the form of graphite. Then as the chain reaction proceeds, neutrons are emitted in the process of fission of the U-235 and many of these neutrons are absorbed by U-238. This produces U-239, each atom of which then emits a beta particle, becoming neptunium (Np-239). Neptunium, in turn, emits another beta particle, becoming plutonium (Pu-239), which emits an alpha particle, decaying again to U-235, but so slowly that in effect it is a stable element. If, after the reaction has been allowed to proceed for a considerable time, the mixture of metals is removed, it may be possible to extract the plutonium by chemical methods and purify it for use in a subsequent fission chain reaction of an explosive nature.

假设我们在天然铀与慢化剂（例如以石墨形式存在的碳）构成的栅格中建立了可控链式反应。那么随着链式反应的进行，铀-235裂变过程中会发射中子，其中许多中子被铀-238吸收。这就生成铀-239，其每个原子随后发射一个β粒子，变成镎（Np-239）。镎又发射一个β粒子，变成钚（Pu-239），钚发射一个α粒子，又衰变为铀-235，但衰变极其缓慢，实际上可视为一种稳定元素。如果让反应进行相当长的时间之后取出金属混合物，就有可能用化学方法提取钚，并加以提纯，以供随后用于爆炸性的裂变链式反应。

### COMBINED EFFECTS AND ENRICHED PILES 综合效应与富集堆

2.20. Three ways of increasing the likelihood of a chain reaction have been mentioned: use of a moderator; attainment of high purity of materials; use of special material, either U-235 or Pu. The three procedures are not mutually exclusive, and many schemes have been proposed for using small amounts of separated U-235 or PU-239 in a lattice composed primarily of ordinary uranium or uranium oxide and of a moderator or two different moderators. Such proposed arrangements are usually called ”enriched piles.”

已经提到三种提高链式反应可能性的途径：使用慢化剂；使材料达到高纯度；使用特殊材料，即铀-235或钚。这三种办法并不互相排斥，人们提出了许多方案，在主要以普通铀或氧化铀以及一种或两种不同慢化剂构成的栅格中使用少量分离出的铀-235或钚-239。这类拟议的布置通常称为“富集堆”。

### USE OF THORIUM OR PROTOACTINIUM OR OTHER MATERIAL 钍、镤或其他材料的使用

2.21. All our previous discussion has centered on the direct or indirect use of uranium, but it was known that both thorium and protoactinium also underwent fission when bombarded by high-speed neutrons. The great advantage of uranium, at least for preliminary work, was its susceptibility to slow neutrons. There was not very much consideration given to the other two substances. Protoactinium can be eliminated because of its scarcity in nature. Thorium is relatively plentiful but has no apparent advantage over uranium.

我们此前的全部讨论都集中于铀的直接或间接使用，但人们已经知道，钍和镤在受到高速中子轰击时也会发生裂变。铀的巨大优势（至少对初期工作而言）在于它对慢中子敏感。对其他两种物质的考虑并不多。镤可因其在自然界中稀少而被排除。钍相对丰富，但与铀相比没有明显优势。

2.22. It is not to be forgotten that theoretically many nuclear reactions might be used to release energy. At present we see no way of initiating or controlling reactions other than those involving fission, but some such synthesis as has already been mentioned as a source of solar energy may eventually be produced in the laboratory.

不应忘记，理论上许多核反应都可用来释放能量。目前我们看不到除裂变反应以外引发或控制其他反应的办法，但上文已提及的某些作为太阳能来源的合成过程，最终或许能在实验室中实现。

### AMOUNTS OF MATERIALS NEEDED 所需材料数量

2.23. Obviously it was impossible in the summer of 1940 to make more than guesses as to what amounts of materials would be needed to produce:

显然，在1940年夏，对于产生下列各项需要多少材料，只能作出猜测：

> (1) a chain reaction with use of a moderator:
> (2) a chain-reaction bomb in pure, or at least enriched, U-235 or plutonium.

> (1) 使用慢化剂的链式反应；
> (2) 在纯铀-235或钚（或至少是富集铀-235或钚）中的链式反应炸弹。

A figure of one to one hundred kilograms of U-235 was commonly given at this time for the critical size of a bomb. This would, of course, have to be separated from at least 140 times as much natural uranium. For a slow-neutron chain reaction using a moderator and unseparated uranium it was almost certain that tons of metal and of moderator would be required.

当时通常给出的炸弹临界尺寸数字为一至一百公斤铀-235。当然，这些铀-235必须从至少一百四十倍的天然铀中分离出来。对于使用慢化剂和未分离铀的慢中子链式反应，几乎可以肯定需要数吨金属和数吨慢化剂。

### AVAILABILITY OF MATERIALS 材料的可得性

2.24. Estimates of the composition of the earth’s crust show uranium and thorium both present in considerable quantities (about 4 parts per million of uranium and 12 parts per million of thorium in the earth’s crust). Deposits of uranium ore are known to exist in Colorado, in the Great Bear Lake region of northern Canada, in Joachimstal in Czechoslovakia, and in the Belgian Congo. Many other deposits of uranium ore are known, but their extent is in many cases unexplored. Uranium is always found with radium although in much larger quantity. Both are often found with vanadium ores. Small quantities of uranium Oxide have been used for many years in the ceramics industry.

对地壳成分的估算表明，铀和钍都以相当数量存在（地壳中约含百万分之四的铀和百万分之十二的钍）。已知铀矿矿床存在于科罗拉多、加拿大北部的大熊湖地区、捷克斯洛伐克的约阿希姆斯塔尔以及比属刚果。还已知有许多其他铀矿矿床，但其规模在许多情况下尚未探明。铀总是与镭共生，尽管其数量要大得多。两者常与钒矿共生。多年来，少量氧化铀一直用于陶瓷工业。

2.25. Thorium is also rather widely distributed, occurring as thorium oxide in fairly high concentration in monazite sands. Such sands are found to some extent in this country but particularly in Brazil and in India.

钍的分布也相当广泛，以氧化钍的形式存在于独居石砂中，浓度相当高。此类砂矿在我国有一定储量，但主要见于巴西和印度。

2.26. Early rough estimates, which are probably optimistic, were that the nuclear energy available in known deposits of uranium was adequate to supply the total power needs of this country for 200 years (assuming utilization of U-238 as well as U-235).

早期粗略的估算（可能偏于乐观）认为，已知铀矿床中可获得的核能足以满足我国200年的总动力需求（假定铀-238与铀-235都能得到利用）。

2.27. As has already been mentioned, little or no uranium metal had been produced up to 1940 and information was so scant that even the melting point was not known. (For example, the Handbook of Physics and Chemistry for 1943-1944 says only that the melting point is below 1850 deg C. whereas we now know it to be in the neighborhood of 1150 deg.) Evidently, as far as uranium was concerned, there was no insurmountable difficulty as regards obtaining raw materials or producing the metal, but there were very grave questions as to how long it would take and how much it would cost to produce the necessary quantities of pure metal

如前所述，直到1940年，铀金属几乎未曾生产，资料极其贫乏，甚至连熔点都不知道。（例如，1943—1944年版《物理与化学手册》只说明熔点低于1850摄氏度，而我们现在知道它约为1150度。）显然，就铀而言，在获取原料或生产金属方面不存在不可克服的困难，但在生产所需数量的纯金属需要多长时间、花费多少成本方面，存在非常严峻的问题

2.28. Of the materials mentioned above as being suitable for moderators, deuterium had the most obvious advantages. It is present in ordinary hydrogen to the extent of about one part in 5,000. By 1940 a number of different methods for separating it from hydrogen had been developed, and a few liters had been produced in this country for experimental purposes. The only large-scale production had been in a Norwegian plant, from which several hundred liters of heavy water (D<sub>2</sub>0, deuterium oxide) had come. As in the case of uranium, the problem was one of cost and time.

上文提到的适于用作慢化剂的材料中，氘具有最明显的优势。它在普通氢中约占五千分之一。到1940年，已开发出若干种将其与氢分离的方法，我国已生产出几升供实验之用。唯一的大规模生产是在一家挪威工厂进行的，从那里获得了数百升重水（D<sub>2</sub>0，即氧化氘）。与铀的情况一样，问题在于成本和时间。

2.29. Beryllium in the form of beryllium silicates is widely found but only in small quantities of ore. Its use as an alloying agent has become general in the last few years; for such use, however, it is not necessary to produce the beryllium in metallic form. In 1940 only 700 pounds of the metal were produced in this country.

铍以硅酸铍的形式广泛存在，但矿石数量很少。在过去几年中，铍作为合金剂的使用已很普遍；然而这种用途并不需要以金属形式生产铍。1940年，我国仅生产了700磅金属铍。

2.30. As far as carbon was concerned, the situation was obviously quite different. There were many hundreds of tons of graphite produced every year in this country. This was one of the reasons why graphite looked very desirable as a moderator. The difficulties lay in obtaining sufficient quantities of graphite of the required purity, particularly in view of the expanding needs of war industry

就碳而言，情况显然大不相同。我国每年生产数百吨石墨。这是石墨作为慢化剂看来非常可取的原因之一。困难在于获得足够数量、纯度符合要求的石墨，尤其是考虑到战时工业不断增长的需求

### TIME AND COST ESTIMATES 时间与成本估算

2.31. Requirements of time and money depended not only on many unknown scientific and technological factors but also on policy decisions. Evidently years of time and millions of dollars might be required to achieve the ultimate objective. About all that was attempted at this time was the making of estimates as to how long it would take and how much it would cost to clarify the scientific and technological prospects. It looked as if it would not be a very great undertaking to carry along the development of the thermal-neutron chain reaction in a graphite-uranium lattice to the point of finding out whether the reaction would in fact go. Estimates made at the time were that approximately a year and \$100,000 would be required to get an answer. These estimates applied to a chain-reacting system of very low power without a cooling system or any means for using the energy released.

时间和资金方面的需求不仅取决于许多未知的科学和技术因素，还取决于政策决定。显然，实现最终目标可能需要数年时间和数百万美元。当时所尝试的，主要是估算澄清科学和技术前景需要多长时间、花费多少资金。看来，将石墨-铀栅格中热中子链式反应的开发推进到查明该反应是否真能持续的程度，并非一项十分艰巨的任务。当时的估算认为，大约需要一年时间和\$100,000才能得到答案。这些估算针对的是功率极低、没有冷却系统、也没有任何利用所释放能量手段的链式反应系统。

### HEALTH HAZARDS 健康危害

2.32. It had been known for a long time that radioactive materials were dangerous. They give off very penetrating radiations gamma rays which are much like X-rays in their physiological effects. They also give off beta and alpha rays which, although less penetrating, can still be dangerous. The amounts of radium used in hospitals and in ordinary physical measurements usually comprise but a few milligrams. The amounts of radioactive material produced bj the fission of uranium in a relatively small chain-reacting system may be equivalent to hundreds or thousands of grams of radium. A chain-reacting system also gives off intense neutron radiation known to be comparable to gamma rays as regards health hazards. Quite apart from its radioactive properties, uranium is poisonous chemically. Thus, nearly all work in this field is hazardous particularly work on chain reactions and the resulting radioactive products.

长期以来人们就知道放射性物质是危险的。它们放出穿透力很强的辐射——伽马射线，其生理效应与X射线很相似。它们还放出β射线和α射线，这些射线虽然穿透力较弱，但仍然可能造成危险。医院和普通物理测量中使用的镭的数量通常只有几毫克。相对较小的链式反应系统中铀裂变产生的放射性物质，其数量可能相当于数百或数千克镭。链式反应系统还会放出强烈的中子辐射，已知其在健康危害方面与伽马射线相当。除放射性之外，铀在化学上也有毒性。因此，这一领域几乎一切工作都具有危险性，尤其是链式反应及由此产生的放射性产物方面的工作。

### METHOD OF APPROACH TO THE PROBLEM 解决问题的途径

2.33. There were two ways of attacking the problem. One was to conduct elaborate series of accurate physical measurements on absorption cross sections of various materials for various neutron induced processes and various neutron energies. Once such data were available, calculations as to what might be done in the way of a chain reaction could be made with fair accuracy. The other approach was the purely empirical one of mixing uranium or uranium compounds in various ways with various moderators and observing what happened. Similar extremes of method were possible in the case of the isotope-separation problem. Actually an intermediate or compromise approach was adopted in both cases.

解决这一问题有两种途径。一种是对各种材料在各种中子诱发过程和各种中子能量下的吸收截面进行一系列精细的精确物理测量。一旦获得这类数据，就能相当准确地计算在链式反应方面可以做些什么。另一种途径是纯粹经验性的：将铀或铀化合物以各种方式与各种慢化剂混合，并观察所发生的情况。在同位素分离问题上，也可能采取类似的两种极端方法。实际上，两个问题都采取了折中或中间途径。

### POWER VS. BOMB 动力与炸弹之比较

2.34. The expected military advantages of uranium bombs were far more spectacular than those of a uranium power plant. It was conceivable that a few uranium bombs might be decisive in winning the war for the side first putting them into use. Such thoughts were very much in the minds of those working in this field, but the attainment of a slow-neutron chain reaction seemed a necessary preliminary step in the development of our knowledge and became the first objective of the group interested in the problem. This also seemed an important step in convincing military authorities and the more skeptical scientists that the whole notion was not a pipe dream. Partly for these reasons and partly because of the extreme secrecy imposed about this time, the idea of an atomic bomb does not appear much in the records between the summer of 1940 and the fall of 1941.

铀炸弹的预期军事优势远比铀动力装置更为惊人。可以设想，几枚铀炸弹就可能决定性地使首先使用它们的一方赢得战争。这类想法在这一领域工作的人们心中占有很大分量，但实现慢中子链式反应似乎是发展知识所必需的预备步骤，并成为关注该问题的小组的首要目标。这似乎也是使军事当局和较为怀疑的科学家相信整个设想并非空想的重要一步。部分由于这些原因，部分由于这一时期施加的极端保密措施，在1940年夏至1941年秋之间的记录中，原子弹的设想出现不多。

### MILITARY USEFULNESS 军事效用

2.35. If all the atoms in a kilogram of U-235 undergo fission, the energy released is equivalent to the energy released in the explosion of about 20,000 short tons of TNT. If the critical size of a bomb turns out to be practically, in the range of one to one hundred kilograms, and all the other problems can be solved there remain two questions. First, how large a percentage of the fissionable nuclei can be made to undergo fission before the reaction stops; i.e., what is the efficiency of the explosion? Second, what is the effect of so concentrated a release of energy? Even if only 1 percent of the theoretically available energy is released, the explosion will still be of a totally different order of magnitude from that produced by any previously known type of bomb. The value of such a bomb was thus a question for military experts to consider very carefully.

如果一公斤铀-235中的所有原子都发生裂变，释放的能量相当于约20,000短吨TNT爆炸所释放的能量。如果炸弹的临界尺寸实际上在一至一百公斤范围内，并且所有其他问题都能解决，那么仍有两个问题。第一，在反应停止之前，能使多大比例的易裂变核发生裂变，即爆炸效率如何？第二，如此集中的能量释放会产生什么效应？即使只释放理论上可得能量的1%，爆炸的规模仍将完全不同于以往任何已知类型炸弹所产生的爆炸。因此，这种炸弹的价值是一个需要军事专家十分仔细考虑的问题。

### SUMMARY 摘要

2.36. It had been established (1) that uranium fission did occur with release of great amounts of energy; and (2) that in the process extra neutrons were set free which might start a chain reaction. It was not contrary to any known principle that such a reaction should take place and that it should have very important military application as a bomb. However, the idea was revolutionary and therefore suspect; it was certain that many technical operations of great difficulty would have to be worked out before such a bomb could be produced. Probably the only materials satisfactory for a bomb were either U-235, which would have to be separated from the 140-times more abundant isotope U-238, or Pu-239, an isotope of the hitherto unknown element plutonium, which would have to be generated by a controlled chain-reacting process itself hitherto unknown. To achieve such a controlled chain reaction it was clear that uranium metal and heavy water or beryllium or carbon might have to be produced in great quantity with high purity. Once bomb material was produced a process would have to be developed for using it safely and effectively. In some of the processes, health hazards of a new kind would be encountered.

已经确定：(1) 铀裂变确实发生，并释放大量能量；(2) 在此过程中释放出额外中子，可能引发链式反应。这种反应的发生以及它作为炸弹具有非常重要的军事应用，并不违背任何已知原理。然而，这一设想是革命性的，因而受到怀疑；可以肯定，在能够生产这种炸弹之前，必须研究出许多难度极大的技术操作。大概唯有铀-235或钚-239可用作满意的炸弹材料：前者必须从比它丰富140倍的同位素铀-238中分离出来；后者是此前未知的元素钚的一种同位素，必须通过本身亦属未知的受控链式反应过程来生成。要实现这样的受控链式反应，显然必须大量生产高纯度的铀金属以及重水、铍或碳。一旦生产出炸弹材料，还必须研究出一种安全而有效地使用它的方法。在某些过程中，还会遇到新型的健康危害。

### POLICY PROBLEM 政策问题

2.37. By the summer of 1940 the National Defense Research Committee had been formed and was asking many of the scientists in the country to work on various urgent military problems. Scientific personnel was limited although this was not fully realized at the time. It was, therefore, really difficult to decide at what rate work should be carried forward on an atomic bomb. The decision had to be reviewed at frequent intervals during the subsequent four years. An account of how these policy decisions were made is given in [Chapters III](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_iii.html) and [V](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_v.html).

到1940年夏，国防研究委员会已经成立，并邀请国内许多科学家从事各项紧迫的军事问题研究。科学人员是有限的，尽管当时并未充分认识到这一点。因此，究竟应以何种速度推进原子弹工作，确实难以决定。在随后的四年中，这一决定不得不频繁地重新审议。关于这些政策决定是如何作出的，见[第三章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_iii.html)和[第五章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_v.html)。
