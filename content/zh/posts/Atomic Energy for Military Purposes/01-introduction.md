1.1. The purpose of this report is to describe the scientific and technical developments in this country since 1940 directed toward the military use of energy from atomic nuclei. Although not written as a ”popular” account of the subject, this report is intended to be intelligible to scientists and engineers generally and to other college graduates with a good grounding in physics and chemistry. The equivalence of mass and energy is chosen as the guiding principle in the presentation of the background material of the ”Introduction.”
本报告旨在描述自1940年以来，我国(美国)在原子核能军事应用方面取得的科学技术进展。虽然本报告并非针对该主题撰写的“通俗读物”，但旨在使广大科学家和工程师，以及具备扎实物理和化学基础的其他大学毕业生能够理解其内容。在阐述“引言”部分的背景资料时，我们选择将质量与能量的等价性作为指导原则。

### THE CONSERVATION OF MASS AND OF ENERGY 质量与能量的守恒

1.2. There are two principles that have been cornerstones of the structure of modern science. The first - that matter can be neither created nor destroyed but only altered in form - was enunciated in the eighteenth century and is familiar to every student of chemistry; it has led to the principle known as the law of conservation of mass. The second - that energy can be neither created nor destroyed but only altered in form emerged in the nineteenth century and has ever since been the plague of inventors of perpetual-motion machines; it is known as the law of conservation of energy.
有两条原理一直是现代科学体系的基石。第一条——物质既不能被创造，也不能被毁灭，只能改变其形态——于十八世纪被提出，每位化学学生都耳熟能详；它衍生出了被称为“质量守恒定律”的原理。第二条原则——能量既不能被创造，也不能被毁灭，只能改变其形式——出现在十九世纪，此后一直困扰着永动机发明者；它被称为能量守恒定律。

1.3. These two principles have constantly guided and disciplined the development and application of science. For all practical purposes they were unaltered and separate until some five years ago. For most practical purposes they still are so, but it is now known that they are, in fact, two phases of a single principle for we have discovered that energy may sometimes be converted into matter and matter into energy. Specifically, such a conversion is observed in the phenomenon of nuclear fission of uranium, a process in which atomic nuclei split into fragments with the release of an enormous amount of energy. The military use of this energy has been the object of the research and production projects described in this report.
这两项原理一直指导并规范着科学的发展与应用。在实际应用中，直到大约五年前，它们一直保持不变且相互独立。在大多数实际应用中，情况依然如此，但现在人们已经知道，它们实际上是同一项原理的两个方面，因为我们发现，能量有时可以转化为物质，物质也可以转化为能量。具体而言，这种转化现象在铀的核裂变中得以观察到——在此过程中，原子核分裂成碎片，并释放出巨大的能量。本报告所述的研究和生产项目，其目的正是为了将这种能量用于军事用途。

### THE EQUIVALENCE OF MASS AND ENERGY 质量与能量的等价性

1.4. One conclusion that appeared rather early in the development of the theory of relativity was that the inertial mass of a moving body increased as its speed increased. This implied an equivalence between an increase in energy of motion of a body, that is, its kinetic energy, and an increase in its mass. To most practical physicists and engineers this appeared a mathematical fiction of no practical importance. Even Einstein could hardly have foreseen the present applications, but as early as 1905 he did clearly state that mass and energy were equivalent and suggested that proof of this equivalence might be found by the study of radioactive substances. He concluded that the amount of energy, E, equivalent to a mass, m, was given by the equation

E = mc<sup>2</sup>

where c is the velocity of light. If this is stated in actual numbers, its startling character is apparent. It shows that one kilogram (2.2 pounds) of matter, if converted entirely into energy, would give 25 billion kilowatt hours of energy. This is equal to the energy that would be generated by the total electric power industry in the United States (as of 1939) running for approximately two months. Compare this fantastic figure with the 8.5 kilowatt hours of heat energy which may be produced by burning an equal amount of coal.
在相对论的发展初期，就得出了一项结论：运动物体的惯性质量会随着其速度的增加而增大。这意味着物体运动能（即其动能）的增加与其质量的增加之间存在等效关系。对大多数从事实际工作的物理学家和工程师而言，这似乎只是一个毫无实际意义的数学虚构。就连爱因斯坦恐怕也难以预见到如今的各种应用，但他早在1905年就明确指出质量与能量是等价的，并建议可以通过研究放射性物质来证明这种等价性。他得出结论：与质量m等价的能量E由下列方程给出：

E = mc<sup>2</sup>

其中 c 代表光速。若用具体数字来表示，其惊人的程度便显而易见。这表明，一公斤（2.2磅）物质若完全转化为能量，将产生250亿千瓦时的能量。这相当于美国整个电力行业（截至1939年）连续运行约两个月所产生的总能量。将这一惊人的数字与燃烧同等重量煤炭所能产生的8.5千瓦时的热能相比，便不难看出其中的巨大差异。

1.5. The extreme size of this conversion figure was interesting in several respects. In the first place, it explained why the equivalence of mass and energy was never observed in ordinary chemical combustion. We now believe that the heat given off in such a combustion has mass associated with it, but this mass is so small that it cannot he detected by the most sensitive balances available. (It is of the order of a few billionths of a gram per mole.) In the second place, it was made clear that no appreciable quantities of matter were being converted into energy in any familiar terrestrial processes, since no such large sources of energy were known. Further, the possibility of initiating or controlling such a conversion in any practical way seemed very remote. Finally, the very size of the conversion factor opened a magnificent field of speculation to philosophers, physicists, engineers, and comic-strip artists. For twenty-five years such speculation was unsupported by direct experimental evidence, but beginning about 1930 such evidence began to appear in rapidly increasing quantity. Before discussing such evidence and the practical partial conversion of matter into energy that is our main theme, we shall review the foundations of atomic and nuclear physics. General familiarity with the atomic nature of matter and with the existence of electrons is assumed. Our treatment will be little more than an outline which may be elaborated by reference to books such as Pollard and Davidson’s *Applied Nuclear Physics* and Stranathan’s *The ”Particles” of Modern Physics*.
这一换算数字之巨大，在若干方面都引人注目。首先，它解释了为什么在普通化学燃烧中从未观察到质量与能量的等价性。我们现在认为，这种燃烧所释放的热量伴有相应的质量，但该质量极小，用现有最灵敏的天平也无法探测到。（其数量级约为每摩尔十亿分之几克。）其次，这一点表明，在任何熟悉的地球过程中，都没有可察觉数量的物质转化为能量，因为人们并不知道存在如此巨大的能量来源。此外，以任何切实可行的方式引发或控制这种转化，其可能性似乎极为渺茫。最后，正是这一换算系数的巨大数值，为哲学家、物理学家、工程师和连环画画家开辟了一片广阔的遐想天地。二十五年来，这类遐想一直没有直接实验证据的支持，但大约从1930年起，此类证据开始迅速大量出现。在讨论这些证据以及作为本报告主题的物质向能量的实际部分转化之前，我们将回顾原子物理学和核物理学的基础。这里假定读者一般熟悉物质的原子本性以及电子的存在。我们的论述仅相当于一个纲要，读者可参阅波拉德与戴维森（Pollard and Davidson）的《应用核物理学》（*Applied Nuclear Physics*）以及斯特拉纳森（Stranathan）的《现代物理学的“粒子”》（*The ”Particles” of Modern Physics*）等著作加以扩充。

### RADIOACTIVITY AND ATOMIC STRUCTURE 放射性与原子结构

1.6. First discovered by H. Becquerel in 1896 and subsequently studied by Pierre and Marie Curie, E. Rutherford, and many others, the phenomena of radioactivity have played leading roles in the discovery of the general laws of atomic structure and in the verification of the equivalence of mass and energy.
放射性现象由H.贝克勒尔（H. Becquerel）于1896年首次发现，此后又由皮埃尔·居里与玛丽·居里（Pierre and Marie Curie）、E.卢瑟福（E. Rutherford）以及许多其他人加以研究，在发现原子结构的一般规律以及验证质量与能量的等价性方面发挥了主导作用。

#### IONIZATION BY RADIOACTIVE SUBSTANCES 放射性物质引起的电离

1.7. The first phenomenon of radioactivity observed was the blackening of photographic plates by uranium minerals. Although this effect is still used to some extent in research on radioactivity, the property of radioactive substances that is of greatest scientific value is their ability to ionise gases. Under normal conditions air and other gases do not conduct electricity - otherwise power lines and electrical machines would not operate in the open as they do. But under some circumstances the molecules of air are broken apart into positively and negatively charged fragments, called ions. Air thus ionized does conduct electricity. Within a few months after the first discovery of radioactivity Becquerel found that uranium had the power to ionize air. Specifically he found that the charge on an electroscope would leak away rapidly through the air if some uranium salts were placed near it. (The same thing would happen to a storage battery if sufficient radioactive material were placed near by.) Ever since that time the rate of discharge of an electroscope has served as a measure of intensity of radioactivity. Furthermore, nearly all present-day instruments for studying radioactive phenomena depend on this ionization effect directly or indirectly. An elementary account of such instruments, notably electroscopes, Geiger-Müller counters, ionization chambers, and Wilson cloud chambers is given in Appendix 1.
最早观察到的放射性现象，是铀矿物使照相底片变黑。尽管这一效应至今仍在放射性研究中得到一定程度的应用，但放射性物质最具科学价值的性质，是它们能够使气体电离。在正常情况下，空气及其他气体不导电——否则输电线与电机就无法像现在这样在露天运行。但在某些情况下，空气分子会分裂为带正电和带负电的碎片，称为离子。如此电离的空气确实能够导电。在首次发现放射性之后的几个月内，贝克勒尔就发现铀具有使空气电离的能力。具体而言，他发现，若将一些铀盐放在验电器附近，验电器上的电荷便会迅速通过空气漏失。（若将足量的放射性物质放在蓄电池附近，蓄电池也会发生同样的情况。）自那时起，验电器的放电速率一直被用作衡量放射性强度的尺度。此外，当今几乎所有用于研究放射性现象的仪器，都直接或间接地依靠这种电离效应。附录1对这些仪器作了基础性说明，特别是验电器、盖革-米勒计数器、电离室和威尔逊云室。

#### THE DIFFERENT RADIATIONS OR PARTICLES 不同的辐射或粒子

1.8. Evidence that different radioactive substances differ in their ionizing power both in kind and in intensity indicates that there are differences in the ”radiations” emitted. Some of the radiations are much more penetrating than others; consequently, two radioactive samples having the same effect on an ”unshielded” electroscope may have very different effects if the electroscope is ”shielded,” i.e., if screens are interposed between the sample and the electroscope. These screens are said to absorb the radiation.
不同放射性物质在电离能力的种类和强度上存在差异，这一证据表明它们所发射的“辐射”也有所不同。有些辐射的穿透能力远强于其他辐射；因此，两个对“无屏蔽”验电器产生相同效应的放射性样品，在验电器“有屏蔽”时，即当样品与验电器之间插入屏片时，可能产生很不相同的效应。这些屏片据说是吸收了辐射。

1.9. Studies of absorption and other phenomena have shown that in fact there are three types of ”radiation” given off by radioactive substances. There are alpha particles, which are high-speed ionized helium atoms (actually the nuclei of helium atoms), beta particles, which are high-speed electrons, and gamma rays, which are electromagnetic radiations similar to X-rays. Of these only the gamma rays are properly called radiations, and even these act very much like particles because of their short wavelength. Such a ”particle” or quantum of gamma radiation is called a photon. In general, the gamma rays are very penetrating, the alpha and beta rays less so. Even though the alpha and beta rays are not very penetrating, they have enormous kinetic energies for particles of atomic size, energies thousands of times greater than the kinetic energies which the molecules of a gas have by reason of their thermal motion, and thousands of times greater than the energy changes per atom in chemical reactions. It was for this reason that Einstein suggested that studies of radioactivity might show the equivalence of mass and energy.
对吸收现象及其他现象的研究表明，放射性物质实际上发出三种“辐射”。一种是α粒子，即高速电离的氦原子（实际上是氦原子核）；一种是β粒子，即高速电子；还有一种是伽马射线，即类似于X射线的电磁辐射。其中只有伽马射线才可恰当地称为辐射，而且由于波长很短，就连伽马射线的行为也非常像粒子。这样的伽马辐射“粒子”或量子称为光子。一般来说，伽马射线穿透力很强，α射线和β射线则较弱。尽管α射线和β射线的穿透力不强，但就原子尺度的粒子而言，它们具有巨大的动能，比气体分子因热运动而具有的动能大数千倍，也比化学反应中每个原子的能量变化大数千倍。正因如此，爱因斯坦才提出，对放射性的研究或可证明质量与能量的等价性。

#### THE ATOM 原子

1.10. Before considering what types of atoms emit alpha, beta and gamma rays, and before discussing the laws that govern such emission, we shall describe the current ideas on how atoms are constructed, ideas based partly on the study of radioactivity.
在考察哪些类型的原子发射α射线、β射线和伽马射线，以及讨论支配这类发射的规律之前，我们先描述当前关于原子如何构造的观念，这些观念部分建立在放射性研究的基础之上。

1.11. According to our present view every atom consists of a small heavy nucleus approximately 10<sup>-12</sup> centimeters in diameter surrounded by a largely empty region 10<sup>-8</sup> centimeters in diameter in which electrons move somewhat like planets about the sun. The nucleus carries an integral number of positive charges, each 1.6 × 10<sup>-19</sup> coulombs in size. (See [Appendix 2](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_appendix_2.html) for a discussion of units.) Each electron carries one negative charge of this same size, and the number of electrons circulating around the nucleus is equal to the number of positive charges on the nucleus so that the atom as a whole has a net charge of zero.
按照我们目前的观点，每个原子都由一个直径约为10<sup>-12</sup>厘米的微小而沉重的原子核组成，核外是一个直径约为10<sup>-8</sup>厘米、大部分为空的空间，电子在其中运动，有些像行星绕太阳运行。原子核带有整数个正电荷，每个正电荷的大小为1.6 × 10<sup>-19</sup>库仑。（关于单位的讨论见[附录2](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_appendix_2.html)。）每个电子带有一个同等大小的负电荷，绕核运行的电子数等于核上的正电荷数，因此整个原子的净电荷为零。

1.12. Atomic number and Electronic Structure. The number of positive charges in the nucleus is called the atomic number, Z. It determines the number of electrons in the extranuclear structure, and this in turn determines the chemical properties of the atom. Thus all the atoms of a given chemical element have the same atomic number, and conversely all atoms having the same atomic number are atoms of the same element regardless of possible differences in their nuclear structure. The extranuclear electrons in an atom arrange themselves in successive shells according to well-established laws. Optical spectra arise from disturbances in the outer parts of this electron structure; X-rays arise from disturbances of the electrons close to the nucleus. The chemical properties of an atom depend on the outermost electrons, and the formation of chemical compounds is accompanied by minor rearrangements of these electronic structures. Consequently, when energy is obtained by oxidation, combustion, explosion, or other chemical processes, it is obtained at the expense of these structures so that the arrangement of the electrons in the products of the process must be one of lowered energy content. (Presumably the total mass of these products is correspondingly lower but not detectably so.) The atomic nuclei are not affected by any chemical process.
原子序数与电子结构。原子核中的正电荷数称为原子序数Z。它决定核外结构中的电子数，而后者又决定原子的化学性质。因此，某一化学元素的全部原子都具有相同的原子序数；反过来，所有具有相同原子序数的原子都属于同一元素，无论其核结构可能存在何种差异。原子中的核外电子按照公认的规律排列成一系列壳层。光谱源于这一电子结构外部的扰动；X射线源于靠近原子核的电子的扰动。原子的化学性质取决于最外层电子，化合物的形成伴随着这些电子结构的微小重排。因此，当通过氧化、燃烧、爆炸或其他化学过程获得能量时，所获得的能量是以这些结构为代价的，所以反应产物中电子的排列必定处于能量含量较低的状态。（据推测，这些产物的总质量也相应较低，但低得无法探测。）任何化学过程都不影响原子核。

1.13. Mass Number. Not only is the positive charge on a nucleus always an integral number of electronic charges, but the mass of the nucleus is always approximately a whole number times a fundamental unit of mass which is almost the mass of a proton, the nucleus of a hydrogen atom. (See Appendix 2.) This whole number is called the mass number, A, and is always at least twice as great as the atomic number except in the cases of hydrogen and a rare isotope of helium. Since the mass of a proton is about 1,800 times that of an electron, the mass of the nucleus is very nearly the whole mass of the atom.
质量数。原子核上的正电荷总是电子电荷的整数倍，不仅如此，原子核的质量也总是约等于某一基本质量单位的整数倍，而该基本质量单位几乎等于质子（氢原子核）的质量。（见附录2。）这一整数称为质量数A，除氢和一种稀有氦同位素的情况外，它总是至少为原子序数的两倍。由于质子的质量约为电子质量的1,800倍，原子核的质量几乎就是整个原子的质量。

1.14. Isotopes and Isobars. Two species of atoms having the same atomic number but different mass numbers are called isotopes. They are chemically identical, being merely two species of the same chemical element. If two species of atoms have the same mass number but different atomic numbers, they are called isobars and represent two different chemical elements.
同位素与同量异位素。原子序数相同而质量数不同的两种原子称为同位素。它们在化学上完全相同，只是同一化学元素的两种原子。若两种原子的质量数相同而原子序数不同，则称为同量异位素，它们代表两种不同的化学元素。

#### RADIOACTIVITY AND NUCLEAR CHANGE 放射性与核变化

1.15. If an atom emits an alpha particle (which has an atomic number of two and a mass of four), it becomes an atom of a different element with an atomic number lower by two and a mass number lower by four. The emission by a nucleus of a beta particle increases the atomic number by one and leaves the mass number unaltered. In some cases. these changes are accompanied by the emission of gamma rays. Elements which spontaneously change or ”disintegrate” in these ways are unstable and are described as being ”radioactive.” The only natural elements which exhibit this property of emitting alpha or beta particles are (with a few minor exceptions) those of very high atomic numbers and mass numbers, such as uranium, thorium, radium, and actinium, i.e., those known to have the most complicated nuclear structures.
若一个原子发射一个α粒子（其原子序数为2，质量为4），它便成为另一种元素的原子，其原子序数减少2，质量数减少4。原子核发射一个β粒子则使原子序数增加1，质量数保持不变。在某些情况下，这些变化伴随着伽马射线的发射。以这种方式自发变化或“衰变”的元素是不稳定的，被称为“放射性”元素。具有发射α粒子或β粒子这一性质的天然元素（仅有少数例外）都是原子序数和质量数很高的元素，例如铀、钍、镭和锕，也就是已知核结构最为复杂的那些元素。

#### HALF-LIVES; THE RADIOACTIVE SERIES 半衰期；放射系

1.16. All the atoms of a particular radioactive species have the same probability of disintegrating in a given time, so that an appreciable sample of radioactive material, containing many millions of atoms, always changes or ”disintegrates” at the same rate. This rate at which the material changes is expressed in terms of the ”half-life”, the time required for one half the atoms initially present to disintegrate, which evidently is constant for any particular atomic species. Half-lives of radioactive materials range from fractions of a second for the most unstable to billions of years for those which are only slightly unstable. Often, the ”daughter” nucleus like its radioactive ”parent” is itself radioactive and so on down the line for several successive generations of nuclei until a stable one is finally reached. There are three such families or series comprising all together about forty different radioactive species. The radium series starts from one isotope of uranium, the actinium series from another isotope of uranium, and the thorium series from thorium. The final product of each series, after ten or twelve successive emissions of alpha and beta particles, is a stable isotope of lead.
某一放射性物种的所有原子在给定时间内衰变的概率相同，因此，含有数百万个原子的可观放射性样品总是以相同的速率变化或“衰变”。物质变化的这一速率用“半衰期”表示，即最初存在的原子有一半发生衰变所需的时间；显然，对任何特定原子物种而言，半衰期是恒定的。放射性物质的半衰期从最不稳定者的几分之一秒，到仅轻微不稳定者的数十亿年不等。“子”核往往与其放射性“母”核一样本身也具有放射性，如此逐代延续若干代原子核，直至最终达到稳定核。这样的家族或系列共有三个，合计约包括四十种不同的放射性物种。镭系始于铀的一种同位素，锕系始于铀的另一种同位素，钍系则始于钍。每个系列经过十次或十二次连续的α粒子和β粒子发射后，最终产物都是铅的一种稳定同位素。

#### FIRST DEMONSTRATION OF ARTIFICIAL NUCLEAR DISINTEGRATION 人工核衰变的首次演示

He<sup>4</sup> + N<sup>14</sup> → 0<sup>17</sup> + H<sup>1</sup>

meaning that a helium nucleus of mass number 4 (an alpha particle) striking a nitrogen nucleus of mass number 14 produces an oxygen nucleus of mass number 17 and a hydrogen nucleus of mass number 1. The hydrogen nucleus, known as the ”proton,” is of special importance since it has the smallest mass of any nucleus. Although protons do not appear in natural radioactive processes, there is much direct evidence that they can be knocked out of nuclei.
这表示，一个质量数为4的氦核（α粒子）撞击一个质量数为14的氮核，产生一个质量数为17的氧核和一个质量数为1的氢核。氢核称为“质子”，具有特殊重要性，因为它的质量是所有原子核中最小的。尽管质子不出现在天然放射性过程中，但有许多直接证据表明，质子可以从原子核中被击出。

#### THE NEUTRON 中子

1.18. In the decade following Rutherford’s work many similar experiments were performed with similar results. One series of experiments of this type led to the discovery of the neutron, which will be discussed in some detail since the neutron is practically the theme song of this whole project.
在卢瑟福的工作之后的十年里，人们进行了许多类似的实验，并得到类似的结果。其中一系列此类实验导致了中子的发现；由于中子几乎可以说是整个项目的主题曲，我们将对它作较为详细的讨论。

1.19. In 1930 W. Bothe and H. Becker in Germany found that if the very energetic natural alpha particles from polonium fell on certain of the light elements, specifically beryllium, boron or lithium, an unusually penetrating radiation was produced. At first this radiation was thought to be gamma radiation although it was more penetrating than any gamma rays known, and the details of experimental results were very difficult to interpret on this basis. The next important contribution was reported in 1932 by Irene Curie and F. Joliot in Paris. They showed that if this unknown radiation fell on paraffin or any other hydrogen containing compound it ejected protons of very high energy. This was not in itself inconsistent with the assumed gamma-ray nature of the new radiation, but detailed quantitative analysis of the data became increasingly difficult to reconcile with such an hypothesis. Finally (later in 1932) J. Chadwick in England performed a series of experiments showing that the gamma-ray hypothesis was untenable. He suggested that in fact the new radiation consisted of uncharged particles of approximately the mass of the proton, and he performed a series of experiments verifying his suggestion. Such uncharged particles are now called neutrons.
1930年，德国的W.博特（W. Bothe）和H.贝克尔（H. Becker）发现，若来自钋的高能天然α粒子落在某些轻元素上，特别是铍、硼或锂上，便会产生一种穿透性异常强的辐射。起初，人们认为这种辐射是伽马辐射，尽管它比已知的任何伽马射线都更具穿透性，而且实验结果的细节很难在这一基础上加以解释。下一个重要贡献由巴黎的伊雷娜·居里（Irene Curie）和F.约里奥（F. Joliot）于1932年报告。他们表明，若这种未知辐射落在石蜡或任何其他含氢化合物上，便会击出能量极高的质子。这本身与新辐射具有伽马射线性质的假设并不矛盾，但对数据的详细定量分析却越来越难以与这一假说相协调。最后（1932年晚些时候），英国的J.查德威克（J. Chadwick）进行了一系列实验，证明伽马射线假说站不住脚。他提出，这种新辐射实际上由质量约等于质子的不带电粒子组成，并进行了一系列实验来验证自己的提法。这种不带电粒子现在称为中子。

1.20. The one characteristic of neutrons which differentiates them from other subatomic particles is the fact that they are uncharged. This property of neutrons delayed their discovery, makes them very penetrating, makes it impossible to observe them directly, and makes them very important as agents in nuclear change. To be sure, an atom in its normal state is also uncharged, but it is ten thousand times larger than a neutron and consists of a complex system of negatively charged electrons widely spaced around a positively charged nucleus. Charged particles (such as protons, electrons, or alpha particles) and electromagnetic radiations (such as gamma rays) lose energy in passing through matter. They exert electric forces which ionize atoms of the material through which they pass. (It is such ionization processes that make the air electrically conducting in the path of electric sparks and lightning flashes.) The energy taken up in ionization equals the energy lost by the charged particle, which slows down, or by the gamma ray, which is absorbed. The neutron, however, is unaffected by such forces; it is affected only by a very short-range force, i.e., a force that comes into play when the neutron comes very close indeed to an atomic nucleus. This is the kind of force that holds a nucleus together in spite of the mutual repulsion of the positive charges in it. Consequently a free neutron goes on its way unchecked until it makes a ”head-on” collision with an atomic nucleus. Since nuclei are very small, such collisions occur but rarely and the neutron travels a long way before colliding. In the case of a collision of the ”elastic” type, the ordinary laws of momentum apply as they do in the elastic collision of billiard balls. If the nucleus that is struck is heavy, it acquires relatively little speed, but if it is a proton, which is approximately equal in mass to the neutron, it is projected forward with a large fraction of the original speed of the neutron, which is itself correspondingly slowed. Secondary projectiles resulting from these collisions may be detected, for they are charged and produce ionization. The uncharged nature of the neutron makes it not only difficult to detect but difficult to control. Charged particles can be accelerated, decelerated, or deflected by electric or magnetic fields which have no effect on neutrons. Furthermore, free neutrons can be obtained only from nuclear disintegrations; there is no natural supply. The only means we have of controlling free neutrons is to put nuclei in their way so that they will be slowedand deflected or absorbed by collisions. As we shall see, these effects are of the greatest practical importance.
中子区别于其他亚原子粒子的一个特征，是它不带电。中子的这一性质延迟了它的发现，使其具有很强的穿透性，使其无法被直接观察，并使其成为核变化中非常重要的作用者。诚然，处于正常状态的原子也不带电，但原子比中子大一万倍，并且由一个复杂的系统组成，其中带负电的电子稀疏地分布在一个带正电的原子核周围。带电粒子（如质子、电子或α粒子）和电磁辐射（如伽马射线）在通过物质时会损失能量。它们施加电力，使所穿过的物质中的原子电离。（正是这种电离过程使电火花和闪电路径上的空气具有导电性。）电离所吸收的能量等于带电粒子（其速度因而减慢）或伽马射线（其被吸收）所损失的能量。然而，中子不受这类力的影响；它只受一种非常短程力的影响，也就是说，这种力只在中子确实非常接近原子核时才起作用。正是这种力使原子核尽管内部正电荷相互排斥仍能保持在一起。因此，一个自由中子会不受阻碍地前进，直到与一个原子核发生“迎头”碰撞。由于原子核非常小，这类碰撞很少发生，中子在碰撞前会行进很长距离。在“弹性”碰撞的情况下，普通的动量定律仍然适用，就像台球的弹性碰撞一样。若被撞击的核较重，它获得的速率相对较小；但若被撞击的是质子，由于其质量与中子大致相等，质子会以中子原有速率的很大一部分被向前击出，而中子本身则相应减慢。这些碰撞产生的次级抛射物可以被探测到，因为它们带电并产生电离。中子不带电的性质不仅使其难以探测，也使其难以控制。带电粒子可以通过电场或磁场加速、减速或偏转，而这些场对中子没有影响。此外，自由中子只能从核衰变中获得；自然界没有现成的供应。我们控制自由中子的唯一手段，是把原子核挡在它们的路径上，使它们因碰撞而减慢、偏转或被吸收。我们将会看到，这些效应具有极大的实际重要性。

#### THE POSITRON AND THE DEUTERON 正电子与氘核

1.21. The year 1932 brought the discovery not only of the neutron but also of the positron. The positron was first observed by C. D. Anderson at the California Institute of Technology. It has the same mass and the same magnitude of charge as the electron, but the charge is positive instead of negative. Except as a particle emitted by artificially radioactive nuclei it is of little interest to us.
1932年不仅发现了中子，还发现了正电子。正电子最初由加利福尼亚理工学院的C. D.安德森（C. D. Anderson）观察到。它的质量和电荷大小与电子相同，但电荷为正而非负。除了作为人工放射性原子核发射的粒子之外，它对我们的意义不大。

1.22. One other major discovery marked the year 1932. H. C. Urey, F. G. Brickwedde, and G. M. Murphy found that hydrogen had an isotope of mass number 2, present in natural hydrogen to one part in 5,000. Because of its special importance this heavy species of hydrogen is given a name of its own, deuterium, and the corresponding nucleus is called the deuteron. Like the alpha particle the deuteron is not one of the fundamental particles but does play an important role in certain processes for producing nuclear disintegration.
1932年还标志着另一项重大发现。H. C.尤里（H. C. Urey）、F. G.布里克韦德（F. G. Brickwedde）和G. M.墨菲（G. M. Murphy）发现，氢有一种质量数为2的同位素，在天然氢中的丰度为五千分之一。由于其特殊重要性，这种重氢被赋予专门的名称——氘，相应的原子核称为氘核。与α粒子一样，氘核并非基本粒子之一，但在某些产生核衰变的过程中确实起重要作用。

#### NUCLEAR STRUCTURE 核结构

1.23. The idea that all elements are made out of a few fundamental particles is an old one. It is now firmly established. We believe that there are three fundamental particles - the neutron, the proton, and the electron. A complete treatise would also discuss the positron, which we have mentioned, the neutrino and the mesotron. The deuteron and alpha particle, which have already been mentioned, are important complex particles.
所有元素都由少数几种基本粒子构成，这一观念由来已久。如今它已牢固确立。我们认为存在三种基本粒子——中子、质子和电子。一部完整的论著还会讨论我们已经提到的正电子、中微子和介子。已经提到的氘核和α粒子则是重要的复合粒子。

1.24. According to our present views the nuclei of all atomic species are made up of neutrons and protons. The number of protons is equal to the atomic number, Z. The number of neutrons, N, is equal to the difference between the mass number and the atomic number, or A-Z. There are two sets of forces acting on these particles, ordinary electric coulomb forces of repulsion between the positive charges and very short/range forces between all the particles. These last forces are only partly understood, and we shall not attempt to discuss them. Suffice it to say that combined effects of these attractive and repulsive forces are such that only certain combinations of neutrons and protons are stable. If the neutrons and protons are few in number, stability occurs when their numbers are about equal. For larger nuclei, the proportion of neutrons required for stability is greater. Finally, at the end of the periodic table, where the number of protons is over 90 and the number of neutrons nearly 150, there are no completely stable nuclei. (Some of the heavy nuclei are almost stable as evidenced by very long half-lives.) If an unstable nucleus is formed artificially by adding an extra neutron or proton, eventually a change to a stable form occurs. Strangely enough, this is not accomplished by ejecting a proton or a neutron but by ejecting a positron or an electron; apparently within the nucleus a proton converts itself into a neutron and positron (or a neutron converts itself into a proton and electron), and the light charged particle is ejected. In other words, the mass number remains the same but the atomic number changes. The stability conditions are not very critical so that for a given mass number, i.e., given total number of protons and neutrons, there may be several stable arrangements of protons and neutrons (at most three or five) giving several isobars. For a given atomic number, i.e., given number of protons, conditions can vary still more widely so that some of the heavy elements have as many as ten or twelve stable isotopes. Some two hundred and fifty different stable nuclei have been identified, ranging in mass number from one to two hundred and thirty-eight and in atomic number from one to ninety-two.
按照我们目前的观点，所有原子物种的原子核都由中子和质子组成。质子数等于原子序数Z。中子数N等于质量数与原子序数之差，即A-Z。作用于这些粒子的力有两组：正电荷之间普通的电库仑斥力，以及所有粒子之间的非常短程的力。后一种力只被部分理解，我们不打算讨论。只需说明，这些吸引力和排斥力的综合效应使得只有某些中子与质子的组合才是稳定的。若中子数和质子数都较少，则当二者数目大致相等时便稳定。对于较大的原子核，达到稳定所需的中子比例更大。最后，在周期表的末端，质子数超过90、中子数接近150，那里没有完全稳定的原子核。（某些重核几乎稳定，其极长的半衰期即为明证。）若通过添加一个额外中子或质子人工形成不稳定核，最终会发生向稳定形态的转变。奇怪的是，这一转变不是通过发射质子或中子，而是通过发射正电子或电子来完成的；显然，在原子核内部，一个质子转变为中子与正电子（或一个中子转变为质子与电子），而那个轻的带电粒子被发射出去。换言之，质量数保持不变，但原子序数发生变化。稳定条件并不十分苛刻，因此对于给定的质量数，即给定的质子与中子总数，可能存在若干种稳定的质子与中子排列（至多三种或五种），从而形成若干种同量异位素。对于给定的原子序数，即给定的质子数，情况可以变化得更为宽泛，因此某些重元素可有多达十种或十二种稳定同位素。目前已鉴定出约二百五十种不同的稳定原子核，其质量数从1到238，原子序数从1到92。

1.25. All the statements we have been making are based on experimental evidence. The theory of nuclear forces is still incomplete but it has been developed on quantum-mechanical principles sufficiently to explain not only the above observations but more detailed empirical data on artificial radioactivity and on differences between nuclei with odd and even mass numbers.
我们所作的陈述都以实验证据为基础。核力理论仍不完整，但它已在量子力学原理的基础上发展到足以解释上述观察结果，并且足以解释关于人工放射性以及奇数与偶数质量数原子核之间差异的更详细经验数据。

#### ARTIFICIAL RADIOACTIVITY 人工放射性

1.26. We mentioned the emission of positrons or electrons by nuclei seeking stability. Electron emission (beta rays) was already familiar in the study of naturally radioactive substances, but positron emission was not found in the case of such substances. In fact, the general discussion presented above obviously was based in part on information that cannot be presented in this report. We shall, however, give a brief account of the discovery of ”artificial” radioactivity and what is now known about it.
我们提到过原子核为寻求稳定而发射正电子或电子。电子发射（β射线）在天然放射性物质的研究中已为人所熟知，但在这种物质的情况下并未发现正电子发射。事实上，上文所给出的一般性讨论显然部分依赖于无法在本报告中呈现的信息。不过，我们将简要叙述“人工”放射性的发现以及目前对它的了解。

1.27. In 1934, Curie and Joliot reported that certain light elements (boron, magnesium, aluminum) which had been bombarded with alpha particles continued to emit positrons for some time after the bombardment was stopped. In other words, alpha-particle bombardment produced radioactive forms of boron, magnesium, and aluminum. Curie and Joliot actually measured half-lives of 14 minutes, 2.5 minutes, and 3.25 minutes, respectively for the radioactive substances formed by the alpha particle bombardment.
1934年，居里和约里奥报告说，某些经α粒子轰击的轻元素（硼、镁、铝）在轰击停止后仍继续发射正电子一段时间。换言之，α粒子轰击产生了硼、镁和铝的放射性形态。居里和约里奥实际测得，α粒子轰击所形成的放射性物质的半衰期分别为14分钟、2.5分钟和3.25分钟。

1.28. This result stimulated similar experiments all over the world. In particular, E. Fermi reasoned that neutrons, because of their lack of charge, should be effective in penetrating nuclei, especially those of high atomic number which repel protons and alpha particles strongly. He was able to verify his prediction almost immediately, finding that the nucleus of the bombarded atom captured the neutron and that there was thus produced an unstable nucleus which then achieved stability by emitting an electron. Thus, the final, stable nucleus was one unit higher in mass number and one unit higher in atomic number than the initial target nucleus.
这一结果推动了世界各地的类似实验。特别是，E.费米（E. Fermi）推断，中子由于不带电，应当能够有效地穿透原子核，尤其是那些强烈排斥质子和α粒子的高原子序数核。他几乎立即验证了自己的预言，发现被轰击原子的原子核俘获了中子，从而产生了一个不稳定核，后者随后通过发射一个电子达到稳定。因此，最终的稳定核在质量数上比初始靶核高一个单位，在原子序数上也高一个单位。

1.29. As a result of innumerable experiments carried out since 1934, radioactive isotopes of nearly every element in the periodic table can now be produced. Some of them revert to stability by the emission of positrons, some by the emission of electrons, some by a process known as K-electron capture which we shall not discuss, and a small number (probably three) by alpha particle emission. Altogether some five hundred unstable nuclear species have been observed, and in most cases their atomic numbers and mass numbers have been identified.
由于自1934年以来进行了无数实验，如今周期表中几乎每种元素的放射性同位素都可以制备。其中一些通过发射正电子恢复稳定，一些通过发射电子，一些通过一种称为K电子俘获的过程（我们不拟讨论），还有少数（可能为三种）通过发射α粒子。迄今已观察到约五百种不稳定核物种，其中大多数情况下其原子序数和质量数都已确定。

1.30. Not only do these artificially radioactive elements play an important role throughout the project with which we are concerned, but their future value in medicine, in ”tracer” chemistry, and in many other fields of research can hardly be overestimated.
这些人工放射性元素不仅在我们所关注的整个项目中发挥重要作用，而且它们在医学、“示踪”化学以及许多其他研究领域中的未来价值无论怎样估计都不为过。

### ENERGY CONSIDERATIONS 能量方面的考虑

#### NUCLEAR BINDING ENERGIES 核结合能

1.31. In describing radioactivity and atomic structure we have deliberately avoided quantitative data and have not mentioned any applications of the equivalence of mass and energy which we announced as the guiding principle of this report. Now we must speak of quantitative details, not merely of general principles.
在描述放射性和原子结构时，我们有意避开了定量数据，也没有提到我们宣布为本报告指导原则的质量与能量等价性的任何应用。现在我们必须讨论定量细节，而不仅仅是一般原理。

1.32. We have spoken of stable and unstable nuclei made up of assemblages of protons and neutrons held together by nuclear forces. It is a general principle of physics that work must be done on a stable system to break it up. Thus, if an assemblage of neutrons and protons is stable, energy must be supplied to separate its constituent particles. If energy and mass are really equivalent. then the total mass of a stable nucleus should be less than the total mass of the separate protons and neutrons that go to make it up. This mass difference, then, should be equivalent to the energy required to disrupt the nucleus completely, which is called the binding energy. Remember that the masses of all nuclei were ”approximately” whole numbers. It is the small differences from whole numbers that are significant.
我们谈到过由质子和中子聚集而成、靠核力维系在一起的稳定核与不稳定核。物理学的一条一般原理是，要使一个稳定系统分裂，必须对它做功。因此，若由中子和质子组成的聚集体是稳定的，就必须供给能量才能将其组成粒子分开。如果能量与质量确实等价，那么一个稳定原子核的总质量就应当小于构成它的各个独立质子与中子的质量总和。于是，这一质量差应当等于完全瓦解该原子核所需的能量，这种能量称为结合能。请记住，所有原子核的质量都“近似”为整数。真正有意义的是它们与整数之间的微小差异。

1.33. Consider the alpha particle as an example. It is stable; since its mass number is four and its atomic number two it consists of two protons and two neutrons. The mass of a proton is 1.00758 and that of a neutron is 1.00893 (see Appendix 2), so that the total mass of the separate components of the helium nucleus is
以α粒子为例。它是稳定的；由于其质量数为4、原子序数为2，它由两个质子和两个中子组成。质子的质量为1.00758，中子的质量为1.00893（见附录2），因此氦核各独立组分的质量总和为

2 x 1.00758 + 2 x 1.00893 = 4.03302

whereas the mass of the helium nucleus itself is 4.00280. Neglecting the last two decimal places we have 4.033 and 4.003, a difference of 0.030 mass units. This, then, represents the ”binding energy” of the protons and neutrons in the helium nucleus. It looks small, but recalling Einstein’s equation, E = mc<sup>2</sup>, we remember that a small amount of mass is equivalent to a large amount of energy. Actually 0.030 mass units is equal to 4.5 × 10<sup>-6</sup> ergs per nucleus or 2.7 × 10<sup>19</sup> ergs per gram molecule of helium. In units more familiar to the engineer or chemist, this means that to break up the nuclei of all the helium atoms in a gram of helium would require 1.62 × 10<sup>11</sup> gram calories or 190,000 kilowatt hours of energy. Conversely, if free protons and neutrons could be assembled into helium nuclei, this energy would be released.
而氦核本身的质量为4.00280。略去最后两位小数，我们得到4.033和4.003，二者相差0.030个质量单位。这便代表氦核中质子与中子的“结合能”。它看起来很小，但回想爱因斯坦方程E = mc<sup>2</sup>，我们记得少量质量相当于大量能量。实际上，0.030个质量单位等于每个原子核4.5 × 10<sup>-6</sup>尔格，或每克分子氦2.7 × 10<sup>19</sup>尔格。用工程师或化学家更为熟悉的单位来说，这意味着要瓦解一克氦中所有氦原子的原子核，需要1.62 × 10<sup>11</sup>克卡或190,000千瓦时的能量。反过来，若能把自由的质子和中子组装成氦核，这一能量就会被释放出来。

1.34. Evidently it is worth exploring the possibility of getting energy by combining protons and neutrons or by transmuting one kind of nucleus into another. Let us begin by reviewing present-day knowledge of the binding energies of various nuclei.
显然，值得探索通过结合质子和中子、或通过将一种原子核嬗变为另一种原子核来获取能量的可能性。让我们先回顾一下当今关于各种原子核结合能的知识。

#### MASS SPECTRA AND BINDING ENERGIES 质谱与结合能

1.35. Chemical atomic-weight determinations give the average weight of a large number of atoms of a given element. Unless the element has only one isotope, the chemical atomic weight is not proportional to the mass of individual atoms. The mass spectrograph developed by F. W. Aston and others from the earlier apparatus of J. J. Thomson measures the masses of individual isotopes. Indeed, it was just such measurements that proved the existence of isotopes and showed that on the atomic-weight scale the masses of all atomic species were very nearly whole numbers. These whole numbers, discovered experimentally, are the mass numbers which we have already defined and which represent the sums of the numbers of the protons and neutrons; their discovery contributed largely to our present views that all nuclei are combinations of neutrons and protons.
化学原子量测定给出的是某一元素大量原子的平均重量。除非该元素只有一种同位素，否则化学原子量并不与单个原子的质量成正比。F. W.阿斯顿（F. W. Aston）等人在J. J.汤姆孙（J. J. Thomson）早期装置的基础上研制出的质谱仪，能够测量单个同位素的质量。事实上，正是这类测量证明了同位素的存在，并表明在原子量标度上所有原子物种的质量都非常接近整数。这些由实验发现的整数，就是我们已定义的质量数，它们代表质子数和中子数之和；它们的发现极大地促成了我们目前的观点，即所有原子核都是中子与质子的组合。

1.36. Improved mass spectrograph data supplemented in a few cases by nuclear reaction data have given accurate figures for binding energies for many atomic species over the whole range of atomic masses. This binding energy, B, is the difference between the true nuclear mass, M, and the sum of the masses of all the protons and neutrons in the nucleus. That is,
改进后的质谱仪数据，在少数情况下辅以核反应数据，已为整个原子质量范围内的许多原子物种提供了准确的结合能数值。结合能B等于真实核质量M与核中所有质子和中子质量总和之差。即，

B = (ZM<sub>p</sub> + NM<sub>n</sub>) - M

where M<sub>p</sub> and M<sub>n</sub> are the masses of the proton and neutron respectively, Z is the number of protons, N = A-Z is the number of neutrons and M is the true mass of the nucleus. It is more interesting to study the binding energy per particle, B/A, than B itself. Such a study shows that, apart from fluctuations in the light nuclei, the general trend of the binding energy per particle is to increase rapidly to a flat maximum around A = 60 (nickel) and then decrease again gradually. Evidently the nuclei in the middle of the periodic table - nuclei of mass numbers 40 to 100 - are the most strongly bound. Any nuclear reaction where the particles in the resultant nuclei are more strongly bound than the particles in the initial nuclei will release energy. Speaking in thermochemical terms, such reactions are exothermic. Thus, in general, energy may be gained by combining light nuclei to form heavier ones or by breaking very heavy ones into two or three smaller fragments. Also, there are a number of special cases of exothermic nuclear disintegrations among the first ten or twelve elements of the periodic table, where the binding energy per particle varies irregularly from one element to another.
其中M<sub>p</sub>和M<sub>n</sub>分别为质子和中子的质量，Z为质子数，N = A-Z为中子数，M为原子核的真实质量。研究每个粒子的结合能B/A比研究B本身更有意义。这样的研究表明，除轻核中的起伏外，每个粒子的结合能的总趋势是迅速增大，在A = 60（镍）附近达到一个平坦的最大值，然后再次逐渐减小。显然，周期表中部的原子核——质量数为40至100的原子核——结合得最牢固。任何核反应，只要生成核中的粒子比初始核中的粒子结合得更牢固，就会释放能量。用热化学术语来说，这类反应是放热的。因此，一般来说，可以通过将轻核结合成较重的核，或将极重的核分裂成两三个较小的碎片来获得能量。此外，在周期表前十种或十二种元素中，还存在若干放热核衰变的特例，其中每个粒子的结合能因元素不同而无规律地变化。

1.37. So far we seem to be piling one supposition on another. First we assumed that mass and energy were equivalent; now we are assuming that atomic nuclei can be rearranged with a consequent reduction in their total mass, thereby releasing energy which can then be put to use. It is time to talk about some experiments that convinced physicists of the truth of these statements.
到目前为止，我们似乎是在把一个假设叠加在另一个假设之上。首先我们假定质量与能量等价；现在我们又假定原子核可以重新排列，从而使其总质量减少，进而释放出可供利用的能量。现在该谈谈一些使物理学家相信这些说法为真的实验了。

#### EXPERIMENTAL PROOF OF THE EQUIVALENCE OF MASS AND ENERGY 质量与能量等价性的实验证明

1.38. As we have already said, Rutherford’s work in 1919 on artificial nuclear disintegration was followed by many similar experiments. Gradual improvement in high-voltage technique made it possible to substitute artificially produced high-speed ions of hydrogen or helium for natural alpha particles. J. D. Cockcroft and E. T. S. Walton in Rutherford’s laboratory were the first to succeed in producing nuclear changes by such methods. In 1932 they bombarded a target of lithium with protons of 700 kilovolts energy and found that alpha particles were ejected from the target as a result of the bombardment. The nuclear reaction which occurred can be written symbolically as
如前所述，卢瑟福1919年关于人工核衰变的工作之后，出现了许多类似的实验。高压技术的逐步改进，使得用人工产生的高速氢离子或氦离子替代天然α粒子成为可能。卢瑟福实验室的J. D.考克饶夫（J. D. Cockcroft）和E. T. S.沃尔顿（E. T. S. Walton）最先用这种方法成功地产生了核变化。1932年，他们用能量为700千伏的质子轰击锂靶，发现轰击使α粒子从靶中射出。所发生的核反应可用符号写为

<sub>3</sub>Li<sup>7</sup> + <sub>1</sub>H<sup>1</sup> → <sub>2</sub>He<sup>4</sup> + <sub>2</sub>He<sup>4</sup>

where the subscript represents the positive charge on the nucleus (atomic number) and the superscript is the number of massive particles in the nucleus (mass number). As in a chemical equation, quantities on the left must add up to those on the right; thus the subscripts total four and the superscripts eight on each side.
其中下标表示原子核上的正电荷（原子序数），上标表示核中有质量的粒子数（质量数）。如同化学方程式一样，左侧的各量之和必须等于右侧；因此两侧的下标合计均为4，上标合计均为8。

1.39. Neither mass nor energy has been included in this equation. In general, the incident proton and the resultant alpha particles will each have kinetic energy. Also, the mass of two alpha particles will not be precisely the same as the sum of the masses of a proton and a lithium atom. According to our theory, the totals of mass and energy taken together should be the same before and after the reaction. The masses were known from mass spectra. On the left (Li<sup>7</sup> + H<sup>1</sup>) they totaled 8.0241, on the right (2 He<sup>4</sup>) 8.0056, so that 0.0185 units of mass had disappeared in the reaction. The experimentally determined energies of the alpha particles were approximately 8. 5 million electron volts each, a figure compared to which the kinetic energy of the incident proton could be neglected. Thus 0.0185 units of mass had disappeared and 17 Mev of kinetic energy had appeared. Now 0.0185 units of mass is 3.07 × 10<sup>-26</sup> grams, 17 Mev is 27.2 × 10<sup>-6</sup> ergs and c is 3 × 10<sup>10</sup> centimeters/sec. (See Appendix 2.) If we substitute these figures into Einstein’s equation, E = mc<sup>2</sup>, on the left side we have 27.2 × 10<sup>-6</sup> ergs and on the right side we have 27.6 × 10<sup>-6</sup> ergs, so that the equation is found to be satisfied to a good approximation. In other words, these experimental results prove that the equivalence of mass and energy was correctly stated by Einstein.
这一方程既未包含质量，也未包含能量。一般来说，入射质子和生成的α粒子各自都具有动能。此外，两个α粒子的质量不会与一个质子和一个锂原子的质量之和完全相同。按照我们的理论，反应前后质量与能量的总和应当相同。质量可由质谱得知。左侧（Li<sup>7</sup> + H<sup>1</sup>）合计为8.0241，右侧（2 He<sup>4</sup>）为8.0056，因此反应中有0.0185个质量单位消失。实验测得的α粒子能量各约为8.5百万电子伏特，与这一数值相比，入射质子的动能可以忽略不计。于是，0.0185个质量单位消失，同时出现了17兆电子伏特的动能。现在，0.0185个质量单位等于3.07 × 10<sup>-26</sup>克，17兆电子伏特等于27.2 × 10<sup>-6</sup>尔格，c为3 × 10<sup>10</sup>厘米/秒。（见附录2。）若将这些数字代入爱因斯坦方程E = mc<sup>2</sup>，左侧为27.2 × 10<sup>-6</sup>尔格，右侧为27.6 × 10<sup>-6</sup>尔格，可见该方程在很好的近似程度上得到满足。换言之，这些实验结果证明，爱因斯坦对质量与能量等价性的表述是正确的。

### NUCLEAR REACTIONS 核反应

#### METHODS OF NUCLEAR BOMBARDMENT 核轰击方法

1.40. Cockcroft and Walton produced protons of fairly high energy by ionizing gaseous hydrogen and then accelerating the ions in a transformer-rectifier high-voltage apparatus. A similar procedure can be used to produce high-energy deuterons from deuterium or high-energy alpha particles from helium. Higher energies can be attained by accelerating the ions in cyclotrons or Van de Graaff machines. However, to obtain high-energy gamma radiation or - most important of all - to obtain neutrons, nuclear reactions themselves must be used as sources. Radiations of sufficiently high energy come from certain naturally radioactive materials or from certain bombardments. Neutrons are commonly produced by the bombardment of certain elements, notably beryllium or boron, by natural alpha particles, or by bombarding suitable targets with protons or deuterons. The most common source of neutrons is a mixture of radium and beryllium where the alpha particles from radium and its decay products penetrate the Be<sup>9</sup> nuclei, which then give off neutrons and become stable C<sup>12</sup> nuclei (ordinary carbon). A frequently used ”beam” source of neutrons results from accelerated deuterons impinging on ”heavy water” ice. Here the high-speed deuterons strike the target deuterons to produce neutrons and He<sup>3</sup> nuclei. Half a dozen other reactions are also used involving deuterium, lithium, beryllium, or boron as targets. Note that in all these reactions the total mass number and total charge number are unchanged.
考克饶夫和沃尔顿先使气态氢电离，然后在变压器-整流器高压装置中加速这些离子，从而产生能量相当高的质子。类似的方法可用于从氘产生高能氘核，或从氦产生高能α粒子。通过在回旋加速器或范德格拉夫起电机中加速离子，可以获得更高的能量。然而，要获得高能伽马辐射，或者——最重要的是——要获得中子，就必须以核反应本身作为来源。能量足够高的辐射来自某些天然放射性物质或某些轰击过程。中子通常通过用天然α粒子轰击某些元素（特别是铍或硼），或用质子或氘核轰击合适的靶来产生。最常见的中子源是镭与铍的混合物，其中来自镭及其衰变产物的α粒子穿透Be<sup>9</sup>核，后者随即放出中子并变成稳定的C<sup>12</sup>核（普通碳）。一种常用的中子“束”源，是由加速后的氘核撞击“重水”冰而产生的。在这里，高速氘核撞击靶中的氘核，产生中子和He<sup>3</sup>核。此外还使用五六种其他反应，以氘、锂、铍或硼作为靶。请注意，在所有这些反应中，总质量数和总电荷数都不变。

1.41. To summarize, the agents that are found to initiate nuclear reactions are - in approximate order of importance - neutrons, deuterons, protons, alpha particles, gamma rays and, rarely, heavier particles.
概括而言，已发现能够引发核反应的作用物，按重要性大致排序为：中子、氘核、质子、α粒子、伽马射线，以及很少使用的较重粒子。

#### RESULTS OF NUCLEAR BOMBARDMENT 核轰击的结果

1.42. Most atomic nuclei can be penetrated by at least one type of atomic projectile (or by gamma radiation). Any such penetration may result in a nuclear rearrangement in the course of which a fundamental particle is ejected or radiation is emitted or both. The resulting nucleus may be one of the naturally available stable species, or - more likely - it may be an atom of a different type which is radioactive, eventually changing to still a different nucleus. This may in turn be radioactive and, if so, will again decay. The process continues until all nuclei have changed to a stable type. There are two respects in which these artificially radioactive substances differ from the natural ones: many of them change by emitting positrons (unknown in natural radioactivity) and very few of them emit alpha particles. In every one of the cases where accurate measurements have been made, the equivalence of mass and energy has been demonstrated and the mass-energy total has remained constant. (Sometimes it is necessary to invoke neutrinos to preserve mass-energy conservation.)
大多数原子核至少能被一种原子抛射物（或伽马辐射）穿透。任何这类穿透都可能引起核重排，在此过程中会发射一个基本粒子或发出辐射，或两者兼有。生成的原子核可能是自然界存在的稳定物种之一，或者——更可能的是——它可能是另一种类型的原子，具有放射性，最终变为另一种原子核。后者又可能具有放射性，若是如此，便会再次衰变。这一过程持续进行，直到所有原子核都转变为稳定类型。这些人工放射性物质与天然放射性物质有两点不同：其中许多通过发射正电子而变化（天然放射性中未曾发现正电子），而极少发射α粒子。在每一个已进行精确测量的情形中，质量与能量的等价性都得到了证明，而且质量-能量总和保持不变。（有时必须引入中微子才能保持质量-能量守恒。）

#### NOTATION 符号表示法

1.43. A complete description of a nuclear reaction should include the nature, mass and energy of the incident particle, also the nature (mass number and atomic number), mass and energy (usually zero) of the target particle, also the nature, mass and energy of the ejected particles (or radiation), and finally the nature, mass and energy of the remainder. But all of these are rarely known and for many purposes their complete specification is unnecessary. A nuclear reaction is frequently described by a notation that designates first the target by chemical symbol and mass number if known, then the projectile, then the emitted particle, and then the remainder. In this scheme the neutron is represented by the letter n, the proton by p, the deuteron by d. the alpha particle by α, and the gamma ray by γ. Thus the radium-beryllium neutron reaction can be written Be<sup>2</sup> (α, n)C<sup>12</sup> and the deuteron-deuteron reaction H<sup>2</sup>(d, n)He<sup>3</sup>.
对核反应的完整描述应包括入射粒子的性质、质量和能量，靶粒子的性质（质量数和原子序数）、质量和能量（通常为零），射出的粒子（或辐射）的性质、质量和能量，最后是剩余物的性质、质量和能量。但这些信息很少全部已知，而且在许多场合下无需完整说明。核反应常用一种符号表示法来描述：先以化学符号和已知时的质量数表示靶，然后是入射粒子，再是发射出的粒子，最后是剩余物。在这一表示法中，中子用字母n表示，质子用p表示，氘核用d表示，α粒子用α表示，伽马射线用γ表示。因此，镭-铍中子反应可写为Be<sup>2</sup> (α, n)C<sup>12</sup>，氘核-氘核反应可写为H<sup>2</sup>(d, n)He<sup>3</sup>。

#### TYPES OF REACTION 反应类型

1.44. Considering the five different particles (n, p, d,α, γ) both as projectiles and emitted products, we might expect to find twenty-five combinations possible. Actually the deuteron very rarely occurs as a product particle, and the photon (gamma rays) initiates only two types of reaction. There are, however, a few other types of reaction, such as (n, 2n), (d, H<sup>3</sup>), and fission, which bring the total known types to about twenty-five. Perhaps the (n, γ) reaction should be specifically mentioned as it is very important in one process which will concern us. It is often called ”radiative capture” since the neutron remains in the nucleus and only a gamma ray comes out.
若把五种不同的粒子（n、p、d、α、γ）同时视为入射粒子和发射产物，我们或许以为会有二十五种可能的组合。实际上，氘核极少作为产物粒子出现，而光子（伽马射线）只能引发两类反应。不过，还有少数其他类型的反应，如(n, 2n)、(d, H<sup>3</sup>)和裂变，使已知反应类型的总数达到约二十五种。或许应特别提到(n, γ)反应，因为它在我们将要关注的一个过程中非常重要。它常被称为“辐射俘获”，因为中子留在原子核内，只有一束伽马射线射出。

#### PROBABILITY AND CROSS SECTION 概率与截面

1.45. So far nothing has been said about the probability of nuclear reactions. Actually it varies widely. There is no guarantee that a neutron or proton headed straight for a nucleus will penetrate it at all. It depends on the nucleus and on the incident particle. In nuclear physics, it is found convenient to express probability of a particular event by a ”cross section.” Statistically, the centers of the atoms in a thin foil can be considered as points evenly distributed over a plane. The center of an atomic projectile striking this plane has geometrically a definite probability of passing within a certain distance (r) of one of these points. In fact, if there are n atomic centers in an area A of the plane, this probability is nπ r<sup>2</sup>/A, which is simply the ratio of the aggregate area of circles of radius r drawn around the points to the whole area. If we think of the atoms as impenetrable steel discs and the impinging particle as a bullet of negligible diameter, this ratio is the probability that the bullet will strike a steel disc, i.e., that the atomic projectile will be stopped by the foil. If it is the fraction of impinging atoms getting through the foil which is measured, the result can still be expressed in terms of the equivalent stopping cross section of the atoms. This notion can be extended to any interaction between the impinging particle and the atoms in the target. For example, the probability that an alpha particle striking a beryllium target will produce a neutron can be expressed as the equivalent cross section of beryllium for this type of reaction.
到目前为止，尚未谈到核反应的概率。实际上，这一概率变化很大。并不能保证一个径直射向原子核的中子或质子一定会穿透它。这取决于原子核和入射粒子。在核物理学中，人们发现用“截面”来表示某一特定事件的发生概率较为方便。从统计上看，薄箔中原子的中心可以视为均匀分布在一个平面上的点。一个原子抛射物撞击该平面时，其中心在几何上具有一定的概率从这些点中某一个的某一距离（r）范围内经过。事实上，若平面上一块面积A内有n个原子中心，则该概率为nπ r<sup>2</sup>/A，这不过是围绕这些点所画半径为r的圆的面积总和与整个面积之比。若把原子看作不可穿透的钢圆盘，把入射粒子看作直径可忽略的子弹，这个比值就是子弹击中钢圆盘的概率，亦即原子抛射物被薄箔阻止的概率。若测量的是穿透薄箔的入射原子所占的比例，其结果仍可用原子的等效阻止截面来表示。这一概念可以推广到入射粒子与靶中原子之间的任何相互作用。例如，α粒子撞击铍靶产生中子的概率，可以用铍对这种反应的等效截面来表示。

在核物理学中，习惯上认为入射粒子的直径可以忽略。因此，任何核过程截面的技术定义是：

表：核过程截面的技术定义。表中以“发生的过程数”除以“入射粒子数”，等于“每平方厘米的靶核数”乘以“以平方厘米计的核截面”。

It should be noted that this definition is for the cross section per nucleus. Cross sections can be computed for any sort of process, such as capture scattering, production of neutrons, etc. In many cases, the number of particles emitted or scattered in nuclear processes is not measured directly; one merely measures the attenuation produced in a parallel beam of incident particles by the interposition of a known thickness of a particular material. The cross section obtained in this way is called the total cross section and is usually denoted by σ.
应当注意，这一定义针对的是每个原子核的截面。截面可以针对任何类型的过程计算，例如俘获、散射、中子产生等。在许多情况下，核过程中发射或散射的粒子数并不直接测量；人们只测量在平行入射粒子束中插入已知厚度的某种材料所产生的衰减。这样得到的截面称为总截面，通常用σ表示。

1.47. As indicated in paragraph 1.11, the typical nuclear diameter is of the order of 10<sup>-12</sup>cm. We might therefore expect the cross sections for nuclear reactions to be of the order of π d<sup>2</sup>/4 or roughly 10<sup>-24</sup>cm<sup>2</sup> and this is the unit in which they are usually expressed. Actually the observed cross sections vary enormously. Thus for slow neutrons absorbed by the (n, γ) reaction the cross section in some cases is as much as 1,000 × 10<sup>-24</sup> centimeters<sup>2</sup>, while the cross sections for transmutations by gamma-ray absorption are in the neighborhood of (1/1,000) × 10<sup>-24</sup> centimeters<sup>2</sup>.

#### PRACTICABILITY OF ATOMIC POWER IN 1939 1939年原子能的可行性

#### SMALL SCALE OF EXPERIMENTS 实验规模很小

1.48. We have talked glibly about the equivalence of mass and energy and about nuclear reactions, such as that of protons on lithium, where energy was released in relatively large amounts. Now let us ask why atomic power plants did not spring up all over the world in the thirties. After all, if we can get 2.76 × 10<sup>-6</sup> ergs from an atom of lithium struck by a proton, we might expect to obtain approximately half a million kilowatt hours by combining a gram of hydrogen with seven grams of lithium. It looks better than burning coal. The difficulties are in producing the high-speed protons and in controlling the energy produced. All the experiments we have been talking about were done with very small quantities of material, large enough in numbers of atoms, to be sure, but in terms of ordinary masses infinitesimal - not tons or pounds or grams, but fractions of micrograms. The amount of energy used up in the experiment was always far greater than the amount generated by the nuclear reaction.
我们一直在轻描淡写地谈论质量与能量的等价性，以及诸如质子轰击锂这类释放出相对大量能量的核反应。现在让我们问一问：为什么二十世纪三十年代没有原子能发电厂在全世界遍地开花？毕竟，如果从一个被质子击中的锂原子中可获得2.76 × 10<sup>-6</sup>尔格的能量，那么我们或许期望将一克氢与七克锂结合，就能得到约五十万千瓦时的能量。这看起来比烧煤更好。困难在于产生高速质子以及控制所产生的能量。我们一直在谈论的所有实验，所用的物质数量都极少，就原子数目而言固然足够大，但就普通质量而言则微乎其微——不是吨、磅或克，而是微克的若干分之一。实验中消耗的能量总是远大于核反应所产生的能量。

1.49. Neutrons are particularly effective in producing nuclear disintegration. Why weren't they used? If their initial source was an ion beam striking a target, the limitations discussed in the last paragraph applied. If a radium and beryllium source was to be used, the scarcity of radium was a difficulty.
中子在产生核衰变方面特别有效。为什么没有使用中子呢？如果其中子初始来源是离子束轰击靶，那么上一段所讨论的局限同样存在。如果要使用镭与铍中子源，镭的稀缺便是一个困难。

#### THE NEED OF A CHAIN REACTION 对链式反应的需求

1.50. Our common sources of power, other than sunlight and water power, are chemical reactions - usually the combustion of coal or oil. They release energy as the result of rearrangements of the outer electronic structures of the atoms, the same kind of process that supplies energy to our bodies. Combustion is always self-propagating; thus lighting a fire with a match releases enough heat to ignite the neighboring fuel, which releases more heat which ignites more fuel and so on. In the nuclear reactions we have described this is not generally true; neither the energy released nor the new particles formed are sufficient to maintain the reaction. But we can imagine nuclear reactions emitting particles of the same sort that initiate them and in sufficient numbers to propagate the reaction in neighboring nuclei. Such a self- propagating reaction is called a ”chain reaction” and such conditions must be achieved if the energy of the nuclear reactions with which we are concerned is to be put to large-scale use.
除阳光和水力之外，我们常用的动力来源是化学反应——通常是煤或石油的燃烧。这些反应通过原子外层电子结构的重排释放能量，这与为我们的身体提供能量的过程属于同一类。燃烧总是自持传播的；因此，用火柴点火会释放足够的热量去点燃邻近的燃料，后者又释放更多热量，点燃更多燃料，如此继续。在我们所描述的核反应中，情况通常并非如此；无论是释放的能量还是形成的新粒子，都不足以维持反应。但我们可以设想，核反应发射出与引发它的粒子同类、且数量足以在邻近原子核中传播反应的粒子。这种自持传播的反应称为“链式反应”；若要把我们所关注的核反应能量用于大规模用途，就必须实现这样的条件。

There was much talk of atomic power and some talk of atomic bombs. But the last great step in this preliminary period came after four years of stumbling. The effects of neutron bombardment of uranium, the most complex element known, had been studied by some of the ablest physicists. The results were striking but confusing. The story of their gradual interpretation is intricate and highly technical, a fascinating tale of theory and experiment. Passing by the earlier inadequate explanations, we shall go directly to the final explanation, which, as so often happens, is relatively simple.

#### DISCOVERY OF URANIUM FISSION 铀裂变的发现

1.52. As has already been mentioned, the neutron proved to be the most effective particle for inducing nuclear changes. This was particularly true for the elements of highest atomic number and weight where the large nuclear charge exerts strong repulsive forces on deuteron or proton projectiles but not on uncharged neutrons. The results of the bombardment of uranium by neutrons had proved interesting and puzzling. First studied by Fermi and his colleagues in 1934, they were not properly interpreted until several years later.
如前所述，中子被证明是引发核变化最有效的粒子。对于原子序数和原子量最高的元素尤其如此，因为这些元素的大核电荷对氘核或质子入射粒子施加强烈的斥力，而对不带电的中子则不然。中子轰击铀的结果被证明既有趣又令人困惑。费米及其同事于1934年首次研究了这些结果，但直到几年之后它们才得到恰当的解释。

#### GENERAL DISCUSSION OP FISSION 裂变的一般讨论

1.54. Consider the suggestion of Frisch and Meitner in the light of the two general trends that had been discovered in nuclear structure: first, that the proportion of neutrons goes up with atomic number; second, that the binding energy per particle is a maximum for the nuclei of intermediate atomic number. Suppose the U-238 nucleus is broken exactly in half; then, neglecting the mass of the incident neutron, we have two nuclei of atomic number 46 and mass number 119. But the heaviest stable isotope of palladium (Z = 46) has a mass number of only 110. Therefore to reach stability each of these imaginary new nuclei must eject nine neutrons, becoming Pd-110 nuclei; or four neutrons in each nucleus must convert themselves to protons by emitting electrons, thereby forming stable tin nuclei of mass number 119 and atomic number 50; or a combination of such ejections and conversions must occur to give some other pair of stable nuclei. Actually, as was suggested by Hahn and Strassmann’s identification of barium (Z = 56, A = 135 to 140) as a product of fission, the split occurs in such a way as to produce two unequal parts of mass numbers about 140 and 90 with the emission of a few neutrons and subsequent radioactive decay by electron emission until stable nuclei are formed. Calculations from binding-energy data show that any such rearrangement gives an aggregate resulting mass considerably less than the initial mass of the uranium nucleus, and thus that a great deal of energy must be released.
让我们结合核结构中已发现的两大趋势来考察弗里施和迈特纳的提议：第一，中子的比例随原子序数增大而上升；第二，每个粒子的结合能在中等原子序数的原子核处达到最大值。假设U-238核恰好分成两半；那么，忽略入射中子的质量，我们得到两个原子序数为46、质量数为119的原子核。但钯（Z = 46）最重的稳定同位素质量数仅为110。因此，为达到稳定，这些假想的新核每个都必须发射九个中子，成为Pd-110核；或者每个核中有四个中子必须通过发射电子转变为质子，从而形成质量数为119、原子序数为50的稳定锡核；或者必须通过此类发射与转变的组合，得到另一对稳定核。实际上，正如哈恩和斯特拉斯曼把钡（Z = 56，A = 135至140）鉴定为裂变产物所提示的那样，分裂的方式是产生质量数约为140和90的两个不相等部分，同时发射少数中子，随后通过电子发射进行放射性衰变，直至形成稳定核。根据结合能数据所作的计算表明，任何这类重排所产生的总质量都远小于铀核的初始质量，因此必然释放大量能量。

1.55. Evidently, there were three major implications of the phenomenon of fission: the release of energy, the production of radioactive atomic species and the possibility of a neutron chain reaction. The energy release might reveal itself in kinetic energy of the fission fragments and in the subsequent radioactive disintegration of the products. The possibility of a neutron chain reaction depended on whether neutrons were in fact emitted - a possibility which required investigation.
显然，裂变现象有三项重大含义：能量的释放、放射性原子物种的产生，以及中子链式反应的可能性。能量释放可能表现为裂变碎片的动能以及产物随后的放射性衰变。中子链式反应的可能性取决于是否确实发射中子——这一可能性有待研究。

1 56. These were the problems suggested by the discovery of fission, the kind of problem reported in the journals in 1939 and 1940 and since then investigated largely in secret. The study of the fission process itself, including production of neutrons and fast fragments has been largely carried out by physicists using counters, cloud chambers, etc. The study and identification of the fission products has been carried out largely by chemists, who have had to perform chemical separations rapidly even with submicroscopic quantities of material and to make repeated determinations of the half-lives of unstable isotopes. We shall summarize the state of knowledge as of June 1940. By that time the principal facts about fission had been discovered and revealed to the scientific world. A chain reaction had not been obtained, but its possibility - at least in principle - was clear and several paths that might lead to it had been suggested.

#### GENERAL STATE OF NUCLEAR PHYSICS 核物理学的总体状况

1.59. By 1940 nuclear reactions had been intensively studied for over ten years. Several books and review articles on nuclear physics had been published. New techniques had been developed for producing and controlling nuclear projectiles, for studying artificial radioactivity, and for separating submicroscopic quantities of chemical elements produced by nuclear reactions. Isotope masses had been measured accurately. Neutron-capture cross sections had been measured. Methods of slowing down neutrons had been developed. Physiological effects of neutrons had been observed; they had even been tried in the treatment of cancer. All such information was generally available; but it was very incomplete. There were many gaps and many inaccuracies. The techniques were difficult and the quantities of materials available were often submicroscopic. Although the fundamental principles were clear, the theory was full of unverified assumptions and calculations were hard to make. Predictions made in 1940 by different physicists of equally high ability were often at variance. \[It is a well known fact in the physics community that repeated calculations tend to converge to a desired result for all involved. At this point, everybody stops calculating.\] The subject was in all too many respects an art, rather than a science.
到1940年，核反应已被深入研究十多年。已出版若干关于核物理学的书籍和综述文章。已发展出产生和控制核抛射物、研究人工放射性，以及分离核反应所产生的亚微观数量化学元素的新技术。同位素质量已被精确测量。中子俘获截面已被测量。减慢中子的方法已得到发展。中子的生理效应已被观察到；人们甚至尝试将其用于癌症治疗。所有这些信息都可普遍获得，但很不完整，存在许多空白和许多不准确之处。技术难度很大，可用的材料数量往往为亚微观级。尽管基本原理是清楚的，但理论中充满未经证实的假设，计算也难以进行。1940年，能力同样杰出的不同物理学家所作的预测常常彼此不一致。\[物理学界一个众所周知的事实是，反复计算往往会使所有相关者收敛到所期望的结果。到了这一步，所有人便停止计算。\]这门学科在许多方面实在更像一门技艺，而非一门科学。

#### SUMMARY 小结

1.60. Looking back on the year 1940, we see that all the prerequisites to a serious attack on the problem of producing atomic bombs and controlling atomic power were at hand. It had been proved that mass and energy were equivalent. It had been proved that the neutrons initiating fission of uranium reproduced themselves in the process and that therefore a multiplying chain reaction might occur with explosive force. To be sure, no one knew whether the required conditions could be achieved, but many scientists had clear ideas as to the problems involved and the directions in which solutions might be sought. The next chapter of this report gives a statement of the problems and serves as a guide to the developments of the past five years.
回顾1940年，我们看到，认真攻克制造原子弹和控制原子能这一问题的所有前提条件都已具备。质量与能量等价已得到证明。已证明引发铀裂变的中子在该过程中会自我复制，因此可能以爆炸性的力量发生增殖链式反应。诚然，没有人知道所需条件能否实现，但许多科学家对所涉及的问题以及可能寻求解决方案的方向都有清晰的想法。本报告的下一章将陈述这些问题，并作为过去五年发展的指南。
