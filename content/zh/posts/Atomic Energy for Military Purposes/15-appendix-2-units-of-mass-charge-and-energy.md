MASS<br /> Since the proton and the neutron are the fundamental particles out of which all nuclei are built, it would seem natural to use the mass of one or the other of them as a unit of mass. The choice would probably be the proton, which is the nucleus of a hydrogen atom. There arc good reasons, historical and otherwise, why neither the proton nor the neutron was chosen. Instead, the mass unit used in atomic and nuclear physics is one sixteenth of the mass of the predominant oxygen isotope, 0<sup>16</sup>, and is equal to 1.6603 × 10<sup>-24</sup> gram. Expressed in terms of this unit, the mass of the proton is 1.00758 and the mass of the neutron is 1.00893. (Chemists usually use a very slightly different unit of mass.)
质量<br /> 由于质子和中子是构成所有原子核的基本粒子，用其中任何一个的质量作为质量单位似乎是自然的。选择很可能是质子，因为它是氢原子的原子核。无论从历史还是其他方面来看，都有充分理由说明为什么既未选择质子，也未选择中子。相反，原子物理学和核物理学中使用的质量单位是主要氧同位素氧-16（O<sup>16</sup>）质量的十六分之一，等于1.6603 × 10<sup>-24</sup> 克。用这一单位表示，质子的质量为1.00758，中子的质量为1.00893。（化学家通常使用一种质量略有不同的单位。）

CHARGE<br /> The unit of electric charge used in nuclear science is the positive charge of the proton. It is equal in magnitude but opposite in sign to the charge on the electron and is therefore often called the electronic charge. One electronic charge is 1.60 × 10<sup>-19</sup> coulomb. It may be recalled that a current of one ampere flowing for one second conveys a charge of one coulomb; i.e., one electronic charge equals 1.60 x10<sup>-19</sup> ampere second.
电荷<br /> 核科学中使用的电荷单位是质子的正电荷。它在数值上与电子电荷相等而符号相反，因此常被称为电子电荷。一个电子电荷为1.60 × 10<sup>-19</sup> 库仑。可以回顾一下，一安培的电流流动一秒钟输送一库仑的电荷；也就是说，一个电子电荷等于1.60 × 10<sup>-19</sup> 安培秒。

ENERGY<br /> The energy unit used in nuclear physics is the electron volt, which is defined as equal to the kinetic energy which a particle carrying one electronic charge acquires in falling freely through a potential drop of one volt. It is often convenient to use the million-times greater unit: million electron volt (Mev).
能量<br /> 核物理学中使用的能量单位是电子伏，其定义是：一个携带单位电子电荷的粒子在通过一伏特的电势降时自由降落所获得的动能。通常方便的是使用其百万倍大的单位：兆电子伏（Mev）。
