As was pointed out in Chapter VI, the control of a chain reacting pile is greatly facilitated by the fact that some of the neutrons resulting from uranium fission are not emitted until more than a second after fission occurs. It was therefore important to study this effect experimentally. Such experiments were described by Snell, Nedzel and Ibser in a report dated May 15, 1942 from which we quote as follows:
正如第六章所指出的，链式反应堆的控制之所以大大便利，是因为铀裂变产生的某些中子要到裂变发生后一秒以上才发射出来。因此，对这一效应进行实验研究很重要。斯内尔（Snell）、内泽尔（Nedzel）和伊布泽（Ibser）在1942年5月15日的一份报告中描述了这类实验，我们引述如下：

“The present experiment consists of two interrelated parts - one concerned with the decay curve, and one concerned with the intensity of the delayed neutrons measured in terms of that of the 'instantaneous' fission neutrons.
“本实验由两个相互关联的部分组成——一部分涉及衰变曲线，另一部分涉及缓发中子的强度，后者以“瞬发”裂变中子的强度为基准来测量。

### THE DECAY CURVE OF THE DELAYED NEUTRONS 缓发中子的衰变曲线

“The neutron source was the beryllium target of the University of Chicago cyclotron struck by a beam of up to 20 microA of 8 MeV deuterons. Near the target was placed a hollow shell made of tinned iron and containing 106 pounds. of U3O8. This was surrounded by about 2” of paraffin. The interior of the shell was filled with paraffin, except for an axial hole which accommodated a BF3- filled proportional counter. The counter was connected through an amplifier to a scaling circuit (’scale of 64') equipped with interpolating lights and a Cenco impulse counter. A tenth-second timer, driven by a synchronous motor, and hundredth-second stop watch were mounted on the panel of the scaler, close to the interpolating lights and impulse counter. This group of dials and lights was photographed at an appropriately varying rate by a Sept camera which was actuated by hand. The result was a record on movie film of times and counts, from which the decay curves were plotted.
“中子源是芝加哥大学回旋加速器的铍靶，用最高达20微安的8兆电子伏氘束轰击。靶旁放置了一个由镀锡铁制成的空心壳，内装106磅U<sub>3</sub>O<sub>8</sub>。其周围包有约2英寸厚的石蜡。壳内部除一个容纳充BF<sub>3</sub>正比计数管的轴向孔外，均填充石蜡。计数管通过放大器连接到带有内插灯和Cenco脉冲计数器的定标电路（“64进制”）。一个由同步电机驱动的十分之一秒计时器和一个百分之一秒秒表安装在定标器的面板上，靠近内插灯和脉冲计数器。这一组表盘和灯由一台手动操作的Sept相机以适当变化的速率拍摄。结果是在电影胶片上记录了时间和计数，据此绘制了衰变曲线。

“The actual procedure was as follows: During bombardment the stop watch was started and the timer was running continuously; the counter and amplifier were on, but the pulses leaving the amplifier were grounded. The scaler was set at zero. After a warning signal the cyclotron was shut off by one operator, while another operator switched the output of the amplifier from ground into the scaler, and started taking photographs. It was easy to take the first photograph within half a second of turning off the cyclotron. Sixty to a hundred photographs were taken during a typical run. The necessity of using both a stop watch and a timer arose from the fact that the hundredth-second precision of the stop watch was needed for the small time intervals between photographs during the initial part of the run, but the watch ran down and stopped before the counting was complete. The timer then gave sufficient precision for the later time intervals.
“实际程序如下：在轰击期间，启动秒表，计时器持续运行；计数管和放大器开启，但放大器输出的脉冲被接地。定标器置零。在发出警告信号后，一名操作员关闭回旋加速器，另一名操作员将放大器的输出从接地切换到定标器，并开始拍照。在关闭回旋加速器后的半秒内拍摄第一张照片是容易做到的。在一次典型运行中拍摄了六十到一百张照片。之所以同时使用秒表和计时器，是因为在运行初期照片之间的短时间间隔需要秒表的百分之一秒精度，但秒表在计数完成之前就会走完并停止。此后，计时器为较晚的时间间隔提供了足够的精度。

“Some forty runs were taken under varying experimental conditions. Short activations of one or two seconds were given for best resolution of the short periods. Long, intense bombardments lasting 15-20 minutes, as close as possible to the target, were made to make the long period activities show up with a maximum intensity. Some 5-minute bombardments were made, keeping the cyclotron beam as steady as possible, to study the relative saturation intensities of the various activities; in these activations the cyclotron beam was reduced to 1 or 2 &amp;mul; A to prevent the initial counting rate from becoming too high for a counter (300 per sec. was taken as a reasonable upper limit for reliable counting). Two BF<sub>3</sub> counters were available, one having a thermal neutron cross section of 2.66 sq. centimeters., and the other 0.43 sq. centimeters. After a strong activation, we could follow the decay of the delayed neutrons for some 13 minutes. Background counts (presumably chiefly due to spontaneous fission neutrons) were taken and were subtracted from the readings. They amounted to about 0.4 counts per sec. for the large counter.
“在不同实验条件下共进行了约四十次运行。为了获得短周期的最佳分辨率，给予了一两秒的短时间活化。为了以最大强度显示长周期活动，在尽可能靠近靶的地方进行了持续15至20分钟的长而强烈的轰击。还进行了一些5分钟的轰击，尽量保持回旋加速器束流稳定，以研究各种活动的相对饱和强度；在这些活化中，回旋加速器束流被降低到1至2微安，以防止初始计数率对计数管来说过高（每秒300次被视为可靠计数的合理上限）。有两个BF<sub>3</sub>计数管可用，一个的热中子截面为2.66平方厘米，另一个为0.43平方厘米。在一次强活化之后，我们可以追踪缓发中子的衰变约13分钟。还测量了本底计数（推测主要来自自发裂变中子），并从读数中减去。对于大计数管，本底约为每秒0.4次计数。

“A study of all the decay curves gives the following as a general picture of the neutron-emitting activities present:
“对所有衰变曲线的研究给出了关于所存在的中子发射活动的如下总体图景：

“Any activity of period longer than 57 sec. failed to appear even after the most intense bombardment we could give, lasting 20 minutes. The relative initial intensities given are the average values obtained from three curves.
“任何周期长于57秒的活动，即使在我们所能给予的最强烈、持续20分钟的轰击之后，也未能出现。所给出的相对初始强度是从三条曲线得到的平均值。

“These results give the following equation for the decay curve, of the delayed neutrons after activation to saturation:
“这些结果给出了活化至饱和后缓发中子衰变曲线的如下方程：

Activity = constant (1.2e<sup>-0.28t</sup> + 1.2e<sup>-0.099t</sup> + 1.0e<sup>-0.029t</sup> + 0.135e<sup>-0.012t</sup>)

where t is in seconds.”
其中t以秒为单位。”

The second part of the experiment measured the total number of neutrons emitted in the time interval 0.01 sec. to 2.0 min. after the cyclotron was turned off. Assuming that all the delayed neutrons observed were in the four groups measured in the first part of the experiment, this second result indicated that 1.0 ± 0.2 per cent of the neutrons emitted in uranium fission are delayed by at least 0.01 sec. and that about 0.07 per cent are delayed by as much as a minute. By designing the effective value of k, the multiplication factor, for a typical operating pile to be only 1.01 with all the controls removed and the total variation in k from one control rod to be 0.002, the number of delayed neutrons is sufficient to allow easy control.
实验的第二部分测量了回旋加速器关闭后0.01秒至2.0分钟的时间间隔内发射的中子总数。假定所观察到的全部缓发中子都属于实验第一部分所测得的四个组，这第二个结果表明，铀裂变中发射的中子有1.0 ± 0.2%至少延迟0.01秒，约有0.07%延迟长达一分钟。通过把典型运行堆的有效倍增系数k设计为在取下全部控制装置时仅为1.01，且一根控制棒引起的k总变化为0.002，缓发中子的数量就足以实现容易的控制。
