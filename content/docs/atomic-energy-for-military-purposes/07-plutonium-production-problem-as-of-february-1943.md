### INTRODUCTION 引言

#### NEED OF DECISIONS 需要作出的决策

7.1. By the first of 1 January 1943, the Metallurgical Laboratory had achieved its first objective, a chain-reacting pile, and was well on the way to the second, a process for extracting the plutonium produced in such a pile. It was clearly time to formulate more definite plans for a production plant. The policy decisions were made by the Policy Committee (see [Chapter V](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_v.html)) on the recommendations from the Laboratory Director (A. H. Compton), from the S-1 Executive Committee, and from the Reviewing Committee that had visited Chicago in December 1942. The only decisions that had already been made were that the first chain-reacting pile should be dismantled and then reconstructed on a site a short distance from Chicago and that a 1,000-kilowatt plutonium plant should be built at Clinton, Tennessee.

到1943年1月1日，冶金实验室已经实现了它的第一个目标——一座链式反应堆，并且在第二个目标——从这种反应堆所生产的钚中提取钚的流程——方面也已取得很大进展。显然，此时应当为生产工厂制定更为明确的计划。各项政策决定由政策委员会（见[第五章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_v.html)）根据实验室主任（A. H.康普顿）、S-1执行委员会以及1942年12月访问芝加哥的审查委员会的建议作出。当时已经作出的唯一决定是：第一座链式反应堆应当拆除，然后在距芝加哥不远的一处场址上重建；以及在田纳西州克林顿建造一座1,000千瓦的钚生产工厂。

### THE SCALE OF PRODUCTION 生产规模

7.2. The first decision to be made was on the scale of production that should be attempted. For reasons of security the figure decided upon may not be disclosed here. It was very large.

需要作出的第一项决定是应当尝试多大的生产规模。出于安全原因，所确定的数字不能在此披露。这一数字非常庞大。

### THE MAGNITUDE OF THE PROBLEM 问题的规模

7.3. As we have seen, the production of one gram of plutonium per day corresponds to a generation of energy at the rate of 500 to 1,500 kilowatts. Therefore a plant for large-scale production of plutonium will release a very large amount of energy. The problem therefore was to design a plant of this capacity on the basis of experience with a pile that could operate at a power level of only 0.2 kilowatt. As regards the plutonium separation work, which was equally important, it was necessary to draw plans for an extraction and purification plant which would separate some grams a day of plutonium from some tons of uranium, and such planning had to be based on information obtained by microchemical studies involving only half a milligram of plutonium. To be sure, there was information available for the design of the large-scale pile and separation plant from auxiliary experiments and from large-scale studies of separation processes using uranium as a stand-in for plutonium, but even so the proposed extrapolations both as to chain-reacting piles and as to separation processes were staggering. In peacetime no engineer or scientist in his right mind would consider making such a magnification in a single stage, and even in wartime only the possibility of achieving. tremendously important results, could justify it.

正如我们已经看到的，每天生产一克钚相当于以500至1,500千瓦的速率产生能量。因此，一座大规模生产钚的工厂将释放非常巨大的能量。于是，问题在于要根据一座只能以0.2千瓦功率水平运行的反应堆的经验，来设计具有这种生产能力的工厂。就同样重要的钚分离工作而言，必须为一座提取和纯化工厂制订计划，该厂每天要从数吨铀中分离出数克钚，而这样的规划必须以仅涉及半毫克钚的微量化学研究所得的信息为基础。诚然，从辅助实验以及以铀代替钚进行分离流程的大规模研究中，可以获得用于设计大型反应堆和分离工厂的信息，但即便如此，无论就链式反应堆还是就分离流程而言，拟议中的外推都是惊人的。在和平时期，任何头脑正常的工程师或科学家都不会考虑在单个阶段作如此巨大的放大；即便在战时，也只有实现极其重要成果的可能性才能证明这种做法是合理的。

### ASSIGNMENT OF RESPONSIBILITY 责任的分配

7.4. As soon as it had been decided to go ahead with large-scale production of plutonium, it was evident that a great expansion in organization was necessary. The Stone and Webster Engineering Corporation had been selected as the overall engineering and construction firm for the DSM Project soon after the Manhattan District was placed in charge of construction work in June 1942. By October 1942, it became evident that various component parts of the work were too far separated physically and were too complicated technically to be handled by a single company- especially in view of the rapid pace required. Therefore it was decided that it would be advantageous if Stone and Webster were relieved of that portion of the work pertaining to the construction of plutonium production facilities. This was done, and General Groves selected the E. I. du Pont de Nemours and Company as the firm best able to carry on this phase of the work. The arrangements made with various industrial companies by the Manhattan District took various forms. The arrangements with du Pont are discussed in detail as an example.

一旦决定着手大规模生产钚，显然就必须大幅扩展组织机构。1942年6月曼哈顿工程区接管建筑工作后不久，斯通-韦伯斯特工程公司（Stone and Webster Engineering Corporation）即被选定为DSM项目的总工程与建筑公司。到1942年10月，情况已经表明，工程的各个组成部分在地理上相距过远，在技术上又过于复杂，难以由一家公司承担——尤其是考虑到所需的速度。因此决定，若能让斯通-韦伯斯特公司免去与钚生产设施建设有关的那部分工作，将是有利的。这一调整付诸实施，格罗夫斯将军选定杜邦公司（E. I. du Pont de Nemours and Company）为最有能力承担这一阶段工作的公司。曼哈顿工程区与各工业公司所作的各种安排形式不同。与杜邦公司的安排将作为一个例子详细讨论。

7.5. General Groves broached the question to W. S. Carpenter, Jr., president of du Pont, and after considerable discussion with him and other officials of the firm, du Pont agreed to undertake the work. In their acceptance, they made it plain and it was understood by all concerned that du Pont was undertaking the, work only because the War Department considered the work to be of the utmost importance, and because General Groves stated that this view as to importance was one held personally by the President of the United States, the Secretary of War, the Chief of Staff, and General Groves, and because of General Groves' assertion that du Pont was by far the organization best qualified for the job. At the same time, it was recognized that the du Pont Company already had assumed all the war-connected activities which their existing organization could be expected to handle without undue difficulty.

格罗夫斯将军向杜邦公司总裁小W. S.卡彭特（W. S. Carpenter, Jr.）提出了这一问题，经过与他和公司其他高级管理人员的多次讨论，杜邦公司同意承担这项工作。在接受时，杜邦公司明确表示——所有有关方面也都理解——杜邦公司承担这项工作，仅仅是因为陆军部认为这项工作极为重要，是因为格罗夫斯将军指出，美国总统、陆军部长、参谋长和格罗夫斯将军本人都持这一关于重要性的看法，并且是因为格罗夫斯将军断言杜邦公司是迄今最适合承担这项工作的组织。同时，各方也认识到，杜邦公司在其现有组织能够预期在不过度困难的情况下处理的范围内，已经承担了所有与战争有关的活动。

7.6. The du Pont Company, in accepting the undertaking, insisted that the work be conducted without profit and without patent rights of any kind accruing to them. The du Pont Company did request, however, that in view of the unknown character of the field into which they were being asked to embark, and in view of the unpredictable hazards involved, the Government provide maximum protection against losses sustained by du Pont.

杜邦公司在接受这一任务时坚持，这项工作应在不谋取利润、也不使公司获得任何形式专利权的条件下进行。然而，杜邦公司确实请求，鉴于公司被要求进入的领域具有未知性质，并且鉴于所涉风险无法预测，政府应提供最大限度的保护，以免杜邦公司遭受损失。

7.7. The cost-plus-a-fixed-fee contract between the Government and du Pont established a fixed fee of \$1.00. The Government agreed to pay all costs of the work by direct reimbursement or through allowances provided by the contract to cover administrative and general expenses allocated to the work in accordance with normal du Pont accounting practices as determined by audit by certified public accountants. Under the terms of the contract, any portion of these allowances not actually expended by du Pont will, at the conclusion of the work, be returned to the United States. The contract also provided that no patent rights would accrue to the company.

政府与杜邦公司之间的成本加固定费用合同确定固定费用为1.00美元。政府同意支付这项工作的全部费用，方式可以是直接报销，也可以通过合同规定的津贴，用于支付按杜邦公司正常会计惯例分摊到这项工作上的管理和一般费用，这些惯例须经注册会计师审计确定。根据合同条款，杜邦公司实际未支用的任何部分津贴，将在工作结束时归还美国政府。合同还规定，公司不获得任何专利权。

7.8. The specific responsibilities assumed by du Pont were to engineer, design, and construct a small-scale semi-works at the Clinton Engineer Works in Tennessee and to engineer, design, construct, and operate a large-scale plutonium production plant of large capacity at the Hanford Engineer Works in the State of Washington. Because of its close connection with fundamental research, the Clinton semi-works was to be operated under the direction of the University of Chicago. A large number of key technical people from du Pont were to be used on a loan basis at Chicago and at Clinton, to provide the University with much needed personnel, particularly men with industrial experience, and to train certain of such personnel for future service at Hanford.

杜邦公司承担的具体责任是：在田纳西州克林顿工程厂承担一家小规模半工厂的工程、设计和建造；在华盛顿州汉福德工程厂承担一座生产能力很大的大型钚生产工厂的工程、设计、建造和运营。由于其与基础研究的密切联系，克林顿半工厂将在芝加哥大学的指导下运营。杜邦公司的大批关键技术人员将以借用方式在芝加哥和克林顿工作，为大学提供急需的人员，特别是具有工业经验的人员，并培训其中某些人员以备将来在汉福德工作。

7.9. Inasmuch as du Pont was being asked to step out of its normal role in chemistry into a new field involving nuclear physics, it was agreed that it would be necessary for them to depend most heavily upon the Metallurgical Laboratory of the University of Chicago for fundamental research and development data and for advice. The du Pont Company had engineering and industrial experience, but it needed the Metallurgical Laboratory for nuclear-physics and radiochemistry experience. The Metallurgical Laboratory conducted the fundamental research on problems bearing on the design and operation of the semi-works and large-scale production plants. It proposed the essential parts of the plutonium production and recovery processes and equipment, answered the many specific questions raised by du Pont, and studied and concurred in the final du Pont decisions and designs.

由于杜邦公司被要求从其化学领域的正常角色跨入一个涉及核物理学的新领域，各方一致认为，公司必须最倚重芝加哥大学冶金实验室提供基础研究与开发数据以及咨询意见。杜邦公司具有工程和工业经验，但它需要冶金实验室的核物理学和放射化学经验。冶金实验室就与半工厂和大型生产工厂的设计及运行有关的问题开展基础研究。它提出了钚生产和回收流程及设备的关键部分，回答了杜邦公司提出的许多具体问题，并对杜邦公司的最终决定和设计进行了研究并表示赞同。

7.10. The principal purpose of the Clinton semi-works was development of methods of operation for plutonium recovery. The semi-works had to include of course, a unit for plutonium production, in order to provide plutonium to be recovered experimentally. In the time and with the information available, the Clinton production unit could not be designed to be an early edition of the Hanford production units which, therefore, had to be designed, constructed and operated without major guidance from Clinton experience. In fact, even the Hanford recovery units had to be far along in design and procurement of equipment before Clinton results became available. However, the Clinton semi-works proved to be an extremely important tool in the solution of the many completely new problems encountered at Hanford. It also produced small quantities of plutonium which, along with Metallurgical Laboratory data on the properties of plutonium, enabled research in the use of this material to be advanced many months.

克林顿半工厂的主要目的是开发钚回收的操作方法。半工厂当然必须包括一个钚生产单元，以便提供钚供实验回收之用。在当时的时间和可获得的信息条件下，克林顿生产单元无法设计成汉福德生产单元的早期版本，因此汉福德生产单元不得不在没有克林顿经验作为主要指导的情况下进行设计、建造和运行。事实上，甚至汉福德的回收单元也必须在克林顿的结果可供使用之前就完成大部分设计和设备采购。然而，克林顿半工厂证明是解决汉福德所遇到的许多全新问题的一个极为重要的工具。它还生产了少量钚，这些钚连同冶金实验室关于钚性质的数据，使有关这种材料用途的研究提前了许多个月。

### CHOICE OF PLANT SITE 厂址的选择

7.11. Once the scale of production had been agreed upon and the responsibilities assigned, the nature of the plant and its whereabouts had to be decided. The site in the Tennessee Valley, known officially as the Clinton Engineer Works, had been acquired by the Army for the whole program as recommended in the report to the President (see [Chapter V](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_v.html)).

一旦生产规模商定、责任分配完毕，就必须决定工厂的性质及其位置。位于田纳西河谷的场址，正式名称为克林顿工程厂，已由陆军为整个计划征用，这是给总统的报告中所建议的（见[第五章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_v.html)）。

7.12. Reconsideration at the end of 1942 led General Groves to the conclusion that this site was not sufficiently isolated for a large-scale plutonium production plant. At that time, it was conceivable that conditions might arise under which a large pile might spread radioactive material over a large enough area to endanger neighboring centers of population. In addition to the requirement of isolation, there remained the requirement of a large power supply which had originally determined the choice of the Tennessee site. To meet these two requirements a new site was chosen and acquired on the Columbia River in the central part of the State of Washington near the Grand Coulee power line. This site was known as the Hanford Engineer Works.

1942年底的重新考虑使格罗夫斯将军得出结论：该场址对于一座大型钚生产工厂而言隔离程度不够。当时可以设想，可能会出现这样的情况：一座大型反应堆可能将放射性物质散布到足够大的区域，从而危及邻近的人口中心。除隔离要求之外，还有一项大电力供应的要求，而正是这项要求当初决定了田纳西场址的选择。为满足这两项要求，在华盛顿州中部哥伦比亚河畔、靠近大古力输电线路处选定并征用了一处新场址。该场址称为汉福德工程厂。

7.13. Since the Columbia River is the finest supply of pure cold river water in this country, the Hanford site was well suited to either the helium-cooled plant originally planned or to the water-cooled plant actually erected. The great distances separating the home office of du Pont in Wilmington, Delaware, the pilot plant at Clinton, Tennessee, the Metallurgical Laboratory at Chicago, and the Hanford site were extremely inconvenient, but this separation could not be avoided. Difficulties also were inherent in bringing workmen to the site and in providing living accommodations for them.

由于哥伦比亚河是美国最优质的纯净冷河水供应源，汉福德场址既适合最初计划的氦冷却工厂，也适合实际建造的水冷却工厂。杜邦公司总部所在地特拉华州威尔明顿、田纳西州克林顿的中试厂、芝加哥的冶金实验室以及汉福德场址之间相距遥远，极为不便，但这种分离无法避免。把工人运送到场址以及为他们提供住宿，也存在固有的困难。

### CHOICE OF TYPE OF PLANT 工厂类型的选择

7.14. It was really too early in the development to make a carefully weighed decision as to the best type of plutonium production plant. Yet a choice had to be made so that design could be started and construction begun as soon as possible. Actually a tentative choice was made and then changed.

在开发工作的这一阶段，要对最佳类型的钚生产工厂作出经过仔细权衡的决定，确实为时过早。然而必须作出选择，以便尽快开始设计和施工。实际上，先作出了一个暂定选择，随后又加以改变。

7.15. In November 1942, the helium-cooled plant was the first choice of the Metallurgical Laboratory. Under the direction of T. Moore and M. C. Leverett, preliminary plans for such a plant had been worked out. The associated design studies were used as bases for choice of site, choice of accessory equipment, etc. Although these studies had been undertaken partly because it had been felt that they could be carried through more quickly for a helium-cooled plant than for a water-cooled plant, many difficulties were recognized. Meanwhi1e the theoretical group under Wigner, with the cooperation of the engineering personnel, had been asked to prepare a report on a water-cooled plant of high power output. This group had been interested in water-cooling almost from the beginning of the project and was able to incorporate the results of its studies in a report issued on January 9, 1943. This report contained many important ideas that were incorporated in the design of the production plant erected at Hanford.

1942年11月，氦冷却工厂是冶金实验室的首选。在T.摩尔（T. Moore）和M. C.莱弗里特（M. C. Leverett）的指导下，已经制订出这种工厂的初步计划。相关的设计研究被用作选择场址、选择附属设备等的依据。尽管开展这些研究的部分原因是人们觉得氦冷却工厂的研究比水冷却工厂的研究可以更快完成，但仍认识到许多困难。与此同时，维格纳（Wigner）领导的理论组在工程人员的合作下，被要求编写一份关于高功率输出水冷却工厂的报告。该组几乎从项目开始时就对水冷却感兴趣，并得以将其研究结果纳入1943年1月9日发表的一份报告。这份报告包含许多重要构想，这些构想后来被纳入汉福德所建生产工厂的设计。

7.16. When du Pont came into the picture, it at first accepted the proposal of a helium-cooled plant but after further study decided in favor of water cooling. The reasons for the change were numerous. Those most often mentioned were the hazard from leakage of a high-pressure gas coolant carrying radioactive impurities, the difficulty of getting large blowers quickly, the large amount of helium required, the difficulty of loading and unloading uranium from the pile, and the relatively low power output per kilogram of uranium metal. These considerations had to be balanced against the peculiar disadvantages of a water-cooled plant, principally the greater complexity of the pile itself and the dangers of corrosion.

杜邦公司介入后，起初接受了氦冷却工厂的方案，但经过进一步研究后决定支持水冷却。改变的理由很多。最常提到的理由是：携带放射性杂质的高压气体冷却剂泄漏的危险；迅速获得大型鼓风机的困难；所需氦气数量巨大；从反应堆装卸铀的困难；以及每公斤铀金属的功率输出相对较低。这些考虑必须与水冷却工厂特有的缺点相权衡，主要是反应堆本身更为复杂以及腐蚀的危险。

7.17. Like so many decisions in this project, the choice between various types of plant had to be based on incomplete scientific information. The information is still incomplete, but there is general agreement that water cooling was the wise choice.

与本项目中的许多决定一样，各种类型工厂之间的选择不得不建立在并不完整的科学信息之上。这些信息至今仍不完整，但人们普遍认为水冷却是明智的选择。

### THE PROBLEMS OF PLANT DESIGN 工厂设计中的问题

#### SPECIFICATION OF THE OVERALL PROBLEM 总体问题的界定

7.18. In [Chapter II](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_ii.html) of this report we attempted to define the general problem of the uranium project as it appeared in the summer of 1940. We now wish to give precise definition to the problem of the design of a large-scale plant for the production of plutonium. The objective had already been delimited by decisions as to scale of production, type of plant, and site. As it then stood, the specific problem was to design a water-cooled graphite-moderated pile (or several such piles) with associated chemical separation plant to produce a specified, relatively large amount of plutonium each day, the plant to be built at the Hanford site beside the Columbia River. Needless to say, speed of construction and efficiency of operation were prime considerations.

在本报告[第二章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_ii.html)中，我们试图界定1940年夏铀项目所呈现的一般问题。现在我们要精确界定设计一座大规模钚生产工厂的问题。目标已经通过关于生产规模、工厂类型和场址的各项决定而确定。就当时的情况而言，具体问题是设计一座水冷却石墨慢化反应堆（或若干座此类反应堆）以及配套的化学分离工厂，以每天生产规定数量的、相对大量的钚，工厂建在哥伦比亚河畔的汉福德场址。不用说，建设速度和运行效率是首要考虑。

### NATURE OF THE LATTICE 栅格的性质

7.19. The lattices we have been describing heretofore consisted of lumps of uranium imbedded in the graphite moderator. There are two objections to such a type of lattice for production purposes: first, it is difficult to remove the uranium without disassembling the pile; second, it is difficult to concentrate the coolant at the uranium lumps, which are the points of maximum production of heat. It was fairly obvious that both these difficulties could be avoided if a rod lattice rather than a point lattice could be used, that is, if the uranium could be concentrated along lines passing through the moderator instead of being situated merely at points. There was little doubt that the rod arrangement would be excellent structurally and mechanically, but there was real doubt as to whether it was possible to build such a lattice which would still have a multiplication factor k greater than unity. This became a problem for both the theoretical and experimental physicists. The theoretical physicists had to compute what was the optimum spacing and diameter of uranium rods; the experimental physicists had to perform exponential experiments on lattices of this type in order to check the findings of the theoretical group.

我们迄今所描述的栅格由嵌入石墨慢化剂中的铀块组成。用于生产目的的这种栅格类型有两点不足：第一，不拆卸反应堆就难以取出铀；第二，难以将冷却剂集中于铀块处，而那里是产热最多的部位。相当明显，如果能够采用棒状栅格而非点状栅格，也就是说，如果能够将铀集中在穿过慢化剂的直线上，而不是仅仅分布于若干点，那么这两个困难都可以避免。毫无疑问，棒状布置在结构和机械上都将十分优越，但它是否可能建成这样一种栅格而仍具有大于1的增殖系数k，确实存在疑问。这成为理论物理学家和实验物理学家共同的问题。理论物理学家必须计算铀棒的最佳间距和直径；实验物理学家必须对这种类型的栅格进行指数实验，以检验理论组的结论。

### LOADING AND UNLOADING 装料与卸料

7.20. Once the idea of a lattice with cylindrical symmetry was accepted, it became evident that the pile could be unloaded and reloaded without disassembly since the uranium could be pushed out of the cylindrical channels in the graphite moderator and new uranium inserted. The decision had to be made as to whether the uranium should be in the form of long rod;, which had advantages from the nuclear-physics point of view, or of relatively short cylindrical pieces, which had advantages from the point of view of handling. In either case, the materials would be so very highly radioactive that unloading would have to be carried out by remote control, and the unloaded uranium would have to be handled by remote control from behind shielding.

一旦圆柱对称栅格的构想被接受，显然就可以在不拆卸的情况下装卸反应堆，因为可以把铀从石墨慢化剂中的圆柱形通道推出，并装入新的铀。必须决定铀应当采用长棒形式——从核物理学的角度看具有优点——还是采用相对较短的圆柱块形式——从操作的角度看具有优点。无论采用哪种形式，材料都将具有极强的放射性，因此卸料必须通过远距离控制进行，卸出的铀必须在屏蔽后方通过远距离控制来处理。

### POSSIBLE MATERIALS; CORROSION 可能的材料；腐蚀

7.21. If water was to be used as coolant, it would have to be conveyed to the regions where heat was generated through channels of some sort. Since graphite pipes were not practical, some other kind of pipe would have to be used. But the choice of the material for the pipe, like the choice of all the materials to be used in the pile, was limited by nuclear-physics considerations. The pipes must be made of some material whose absorption cross section for neutrons was not large enough to bring the value of k below unity. Furthermore, the pipes must be made of material which would not disintegrate under the heavy density of neutron and gamma radiation present in the pile. Finally, the pipes must meet all ordinary requirements of cooling-system pipes: they must not leak; they must not corrode; they must not warp.

如果用水作冷却剂，就必须通过某种通道把水输送到产生热量的区域。由于石墨管道不实用，必须使用某种其他管道。但是，管道材料的选择，如同反应堆中所用一切材料的选择一样，受到核物理学考虑的限制。管道必须由这样一种材料制成：其中子吸收截面不至于大到使k值降到1以下。此外，管道材料在反应堆内强烈的中子辐射和伽马辐射密度下不得崩解。最后，管道还必须满足冷却系统管道的一切普通要求：不得泄漏；不得腐蚀；不得变形。

7.22. From the nuclear-physics point of view there were seven possible materials (Pb, Bi, Be, AI, Mg, Zn, Sn), none of which had high neutron-absorption cross sections. No beryllium tubing was available, and of all the other metals only aluminum was thought to be possible from a corrosion point of view. But it was by no means certain that aluminum would be satisfactory, and doubts about the corrosion of the aluminum pipe were not settled until the plant had actually operated for some time.

从核物理学的角度看，有七种可能的材料（Pb、Bi、Be、Al、Mg、Zn、Sn），其中没有一种具有高中子吸收截面。当时没有铍管可供使用，而在所有其他金属中，从腐蚀的角度看，只有铝被认为可行。但铝是否令人满意远未确定，对铝管腐蚀的疑虑直到工厂实际运行一段时间后才消除。

7.23. While the choice of material for the piping was very difficult, similar choices-involving both nuclear-physics criteria and radiation-resistance criteria-had to be made for all other materials that were to be used in the pile. For example, the electric insulating materials to be used in any instruments buried in the pile must not disintegrate under the radiation. In certain instances where control or experimental probes had to be inserted and removed from the pile, the likelihood had to be borne in mind that the probes would become intensely radioactive as a result of their exposure in the pile and that the degree to which this would occur would depend on the material used.

虽然管道材料的选择非常困难，但反应堆中要使用的所有其他材料也必须作出类似的选择——既涉及核物理学标准，也涉及耐辐射标准。例如，埋在反应堆内的任何仪器所使用的电绝缘材料，不得在辐射下崩解。在某些情况下，必须把控制探针或实验探针插入反应堆并从中取出，此时必须牢记：这些探针由于在反应堆中受到照射而将变成强放射性，其程度取决于所使用的材料。

7.24. Finally, it was not known what effect the radiation fields in the pile would have on the graphite and the uranium. It was later found that the electric resistance, the elasticity, and the heat conductivity of the graphite all change with exposure to intense neutron radiation.

最后，当时并不知道反应堆中的辐射场会对石墨和铀产生什么影响。后来发现，石墨的电阻、弹性和导热性都会随强中子辐射的照射而改变。

### PROTECTION OF THE URANIUM FROM CORROSION 铀的防腐蚀保护

7.25. The most efficient cooling procedure would have been to have the water flowing in direct contact with the uranium in which the heat was being produced. Indications were that this was probably out of the question because the uranium would react chemically with the water, at least to a sufficient extent to put a dangerous amount of radioactive material into solution and probably to the point of disintegrating the uranium slugs. Therefore it was necessary to find some method of protecting the uranium from direct contact with the water. Two possibilities were considered: one was some sort of coating, either by electro-plating or dipping; the other was sealing the uranium slug in a protective jacket or ”can.” Strangely enough, this ”canning problem” turned out to be one of the most difficult problems encountered in such piles.

最有效的冷却方式本应是让水直接与产生热量的铀接触流动。有迹象表明，这很可能不可行，因为铀会与水发生化学反应，至少会达到使危险数量的放射性物质进入溶液的程度，而且很可能达到使铀块崩解的程度。因此，必须找到某种方法保护铀，使其不与水直接接触。考虑过两种可能性：一种是某种涂层，可以通过电镀或浸渍实现；另一种是把铀块密封在保护套或“罐”中。奇怪的是，这个“制罐问题”竟成为此类反应堆中所遇到的最困难的问题之一。

### WATER SUPPLY 供水

7.26. The problem of dissipating thousands of kilowatts of energy is by no means a small one. How much water was needed depended, of course, on the maximum temperature to which the water could safely be heated and the maximum temperature to be expected in the intake from the Columbia River; certainly the water supply requirement was comparable to that of a fair-sized city. Pumping stations, filtration and treatment plants all had to be provided. Furthermore, the system had to be a very reliable one; it was necessary to provide fast-operating controls to shut down the chain-reacting unit in a hurry in case of failure of the water supply. If it was decided to use ”once-through” cooling instead of recirculation, a retention basin would be required so that the radioactivity induced in the water might die down before the water was returned to the river. The volume of water discharged was going to be so great that such problems of radioactivity were important, and therefore the minimum time that the water must be held for absolute safety had to be determined.

消散数千千瓦能量的问题绝非小事。需要多少水，当然取决于水能够安全加热到的最高温度以及哥伦比亚河取水口可能出现的最高温度；可以肯定，供水需求相当于一座中等城市的用水量。必须设置泵站、过滤厂和处理厂。此外，系统必须非常可靠；必须设置快速动作的控制装置，以便在供水中断时迅速停闭链式反应单元。如果决定采用“一次通过式”冷却而非循环冷却，就需要一个滞留池，使水中诱发的放射性在河水回归河流之前衰减。排放的水量将非常大，因此这类放射性问题很重要，所以必须确定为保证绝对安全而必须滞留水的最短时间。

### CONTROLS AND INSTRUMENTATION 控制与仪器

7.27. The control problem was very similar to that discussed in connection with the first chain-reacting pile except that everything was on a larger scale and was, therefore, potentially more dangerous. It was necessary to provide operating controls which would automatically keep the pile operating at a determined power level. Such controls had to be connected with instruments in the pile which would measure neutron density or some other property which indicated the power level. There would also have to be emergency controls which would operate almost instantaneously if the power level showed signs of rapid increase or if there was any interruption of the water supply. It was highly desirable that there be some means of detecting incipient difficulties such as the plugging of a single water tube or a break in the coating of one of the uranium slugs. All these controls and instruments had to be operated from behind the thick shielding walls described below.

控制问题与第一座链式反应堆所讨论的控制问题非常相似，只是规模更大，因此潜在危险性也更大。必须设置运行控制装置，自动使反应堆在既定功率水平下运行。这类控制装置必须与反应堆内测量中子密度或其他指示功率水平的特性的仪器相连。还必须设置应急控制装置，在功率水平出现迅速上升迹象或供水中断时几乎瞬时动作。非常理想的是，应有某种手段探测初期的困难，例如单根水管堵塞或某个铀块包壳破裂。所有这些控制装置和仪器都必须在下文所述的厚屏蔽墙后方操作。

### SHEILDING 屏蔽

7.28. As we have mentioned a number of times, the radiation given off from a pile operating at a high power level is so strong as to make it quite impossible for any of the operating personnel to go near the pile. Furthermore, this radiation, particularly the neutrons, has a pronounced capacity for leaking out through holes or cracks in barriers. The whole of a power pile therefore has to be enclosed in very thick walls of concrete, steel, or other absorbing material. But at the same time it has to be possible to load and unload the pile through these shields and to carry the water supply in and out through the shields. The shields should not only be radiation-tight but air-tight since air exposed to the radiation in the pile would become radioactive.

正如我们多次提到的，高功率运行的反应堆所发出的辐射极强，使任何运行人员都完全不可能靠近反应堆。此外，这种辐射，特别是中子，具有明显的穿透屏障上孔洞或裂缝而泄漏的能力。因此，动力反应堆整体必须封闭在混凝土、钢或其他吸收材料的厚墙内。但同时必须能够通过屏蔽装卸反应堆，并使供水进出屏蔽。屏蔽不仅应不透辐射，还应不透气，因为暴露于反应堆辐射中的空气会变成放射性。

7.29. The radiation dangers that require shielding in the pile continue through a large part of the separation plant. Since the fission products associated with the production of the plutonium are highly radioactive, the uranium after ejection from the pile must be handled by remote control from behind shielding and must be shielded during transportation to the separation plant. All the stages of the separation plant, including analyses, must be handled by remote control from behind shields up to the point where the plutonium is relatively free of radioactive fission products.

反应堆中需要屏蔽的辐射危险在分离工厂的大部分区域依然存在。由于与钚生产相关的裂变产物具有强放射性，铀从反应堆卸出后必须在屏蔽后方通过远距离控制处理，并且在运往分离工厂的过程中必须加以屏蔽。分离工厂的所有工序，包括分析，都必须在屏蔽后方通过远距离控制进行，直至钚相对不含放射性裂变产物为止。

### MAINTENANCE 维护

7.30. The problem of maintenance is very simply stated. There could not be any maintenance inside the shield or pile once the pile had operated. The same remark applies to a somewhat lesser extent to the separation unit, where it was probable that a shut-down for servicing could be effected, provided, of course, that adequate remotely-controlled decontamination processes were carried out in order to reduce the radiation intensity below the level dangerous to personnel. The maintenance problem for the auxiliary parts of the plant was normal except for the extreme importance of having stand-by pumping and power equipment to prevent a sudden accidental breakdown of the cooling system.

维护问题可以很简单地说明。反应堆一旦运行，屏蔽内或反应堆内就不能进行任何维护。同样的话在较小程度上也适用于分离单元：那里很可能可以停机维修，前提当然是进行了充分的远距离控制去污过程，使辐射强度降到对人员有危险的水平以下。工厂辅助部分的维护问题是常规的，只是备用泵送和动力设备极为重要，以防止冷却系统突然意外故障。

### SCHEDULE OF LOADING AND UNLOADING 装料与卸料的时间安排

7.31. Evidently the amount of plutonium in an undisturbed operating pile increases with time of operation. Since Pu-239 itself undergoes fission its formation tends to maintain the chain reaction, while the gradual disappearance of the U-235 and the appearance of fission products with large neutron absorption cross sections tend to stop the reaction. The determination of when a producing pile should be shut down and the plutonium extracted involves a nice balancing of these factors against time schedules, material costs, separation-process efficiency, etc. Strictly speaking, this problem is one of operation rather than of design of the plant, but some thought had to be given to it in order to plan the flow of uranium slugs to the pile and from the pile to the separation plant.

显然，在未受扰动的运行反应堆中，钚的数量随运行时间而增加。由于Pu-239本身会发生裂变，它的形成倾向于维持链式反应；而U-235的逐渐消失以及具有大中子吸收截面的裂变产物的出现，则倾向于停止反应。确定生产反应堆何时应当停堆并提取钚，需要在这些因素与时间安排、材料成本、分离流程效率等之间作精细的权衡。严格说来，这个问题属于运行问题而非工厂设计问题，但为了规划铀块流入反应堆以及从反应堆流向分离工厂，必须对此有所考虑。

### SIZE OF UNITS 单元的规模

7.32. We have been speaking of the production capacity of the plant only in terms of overall production rate. Naturally, a given rate of production might be achieved in a single large pile or in a number of smaller ones. The principal advantage of the smaller piles would be the reduction in construction time for the first pile, the possibility of making alterations in later piles, and perhaps most important-the improbability of simultaneous breakdown of all piles. The disadvantage of small piles is that they require disproportionately large amounts of uranium, moderator, etc. There is, in fact, a preferred ”natural size” of pile which can be roughly determined on theoretical grounds.

我们一直只按总生产率来谈论工厂的生产能力。自然，给定的生产率既可以通过一座大型反应堆实现，也可以通过若干座较小的反应堆实现。较小反应堆的主要优点是：缩短第一座反应堆的建设时间；有可能在后续反应堆中作出改动；或许最重要的是——所有反应堆同时发生故障的可能性较小。小反应堆的缺点是所需铀、慢化剂等的数量不成比例地大。事实上，存在一种优先的“天然尺寸”反应堆，可以根据理论大致确定。

### GENERAL NATURE OF THE SEPARATION PLANT 分离工厂的一般性质

7.33. As we have already pointed out, the slugs coming from the pile are highly radioactive and therefore must be processed by remote control in shielded compartments. The general scheme to be followed was suggested in the latter part of 1942, particularly in connection with plans for the Clinton separation plant. This scheme was to build a ”canyon” which would consist of a series of compartments with heavy concrete walls arranged in a line and almost completely buried in the ground. Each compartment would contain the necessary dissolving or precipitating tanks or centrifuges. The slugs would come into the compartment at one end of the canyon; they would then be dissolved and go through the various stages of solution, precipitation, oxidation, or reduction, being pumped from one compartment to the next until a solution of plutonium free from uranium and fission products came out in the last compartment. As in the case of the pile, everything would be operated by remote control from above ground, but the operations would be far more complicated than in the case of the pile. However, as far as the chemical operations themselves were concerned, their general nature was not so far removed from the normal fields of activity of the chemists involved.

正如我们已经指出的，从反应堆送出的铀块具有强放射性，因此必须在屏蔽隔间内通过远距离控制处理。所遵循的总体方案是在1942年下半年提出的，特别是结合克林顿分离工厂的计划。该方案是建造一条“峡谷”，由一系列隔间组成，隔间之间以厚混凝土墙相隔，排成一线，几乎完全埋在地下。每个隔间内设有必要的溶解槽、沉淀槽或离心机。铀块从峡谷一端的隔间进入；随后被溶解，经过溶解、沉淀、氧化或还原等各个阶段，从一个隔间泵送到下一个隔间，直到最后一个隔间输出不含铀和裂变产物的钚溶液。与反应堆的情况一样，一切都在地面以上通过远距离控制操作，但操作要比反应堆复杂得多。然而，就化学操作本身而言，其一般性质与有关化学家的正常活动领域相去不远。

### ANALYTICAL CONTROL 分析控制

7.34. In the first stages of the separation process even the routine analysis of samples which was necessary in checking the operation of the various chemical processes had to be done by remote control. Such testing was facilitated, however, by use of radioactive methods of analysis as well as conventional chemical analyses.

在分离过程的最初阶段，连检查各化学过程运行情况所必需的样品常规分析，都必须在远距离控制下进行。不过，利用放射性分析方法以及常规化学分析，使这种检验变得较为方便。

### WASTE DISPOSAL 废物处置

7.35. The raw material (uranium) is not dangerously radioactive. The desired product (plutonium) does not give off penetrating radiation, but the combination of its alpha-ray activity and chemical properties makes it one of the most dangerous substances known if it once gets into the body. However, the really troublesome materials are the fission products, i.e., the major fragments into which uranium is split by fission. The fission products are very radioactive and include some thirty elements. Among them are radioactive xenon and radioactive iodine. These are released in considerable quantity when the slugs are dissolved and must be disposed of with special care. High stacks must be built which will carry off these gases along with the acid fumes from the first dissolving unit, and it must be established that the mixing of the radioactive gases with the atmosphere will not endanger the surrounding territory. (As in all other matters of health, the tolerance standards that were set and met were so rigid as to leave not the slightest probability of danger to the health of the community or operating personnel.)

原料（铀）不具有危险放射性。所需产品（钚）不发出穿透性辐射，但其α射线活性和化学性质的结合，使它一旦进入体内便成为已知最危险的物质之一。然而，真正麻烦的材料是裂变产物，即铀经裂变分裂成的主要碎片。裂变产物具有强放射性，包含约三十种元素。其中有放射性氙和放射性碘。这些物质在铀块溶解时大量释放，必须特别小心地处置。必须建造高烟囱，将这类气体连同第一溶解单元的酸性烟雾一起排走，并且必须确认放射性气体与大气的混合不会危及周围地区。（与所有其他健康事项一样，所制定并达到的容许标准极为严格，以至对社区或运行人员健康造成危险的可能性微乎其微。）

7.36. Most of the other fission products can be retained in solution but must eventually be disposed of. Of course, possible pollution of the adjacent river must be considered. (In fact, the standards of safety set and met with regard to river pollution were so strict that neither people nor fish down the river can possibly be affected.)

大多数其他裂变产物可以保留在溶液中，但最终必须加以处置。当然，必须考虑对邻近河流可能造成的污染。（事实上，就河流污染所制定并达到的安全标准极为严格，河流下游的人和鱼都不可能受到影响。）

### RECOVERY OF URANIUM 铀的回收

7.37. Evidently, even if the uranium were left in the pile until all the U-235 had undergone fission, there would still be a large amount of U-238 which had not been converted to plutonium. Actually the process is stopped long before this stage is reached. Uranium is an expensive material and the total available supply is seriously limited. Therefore the possibility of recovering it after the plutonium is separated must be considered. Originally there was no plan for recovery, but merely the intention of storing the uranium solution. Later, methods of large-scale recovery were developed.

显然，即使把铀留在反应堆中直到所有U-235都发生裂变，仍会有大量未转化为钚的U-238。实际上，过程远未达到这一阶段就已停止。铀是昂贵的材料，可获得的供应总量严重有限。因此，必须考虑在分离出钚之后回收铀的可能性。最初没有回收计划，只是打算把铀溶液贮存起来。后来，大规模回收的方法得到了发展。

### CORROSION IN THE SEPARATION PLANT 分离工厂中的腐蚀

7.38. An unusual feature of the chemical processes involved was that these processes occur in the presence of a high density of radiation. Therefore the containers used may corrode more rapidly than they would under normal circumstances. Further more, any such corrosion will be serious because of the difficulty of access. For a long time, information was sadly lacking on these, dangers.

所涉化学过程的一个不寻常特点是，这些过程在高密度辐射存在下进行。因此，所使用的容器可能比在正常情况下腐蚀得更快。此外，任何这类腐蚀都将因难以接近而变得严重。很长一段时间内，关于这些危险的信息严重缺乏。

### EFFECT OF RADIATION ON CHEMICAL REACTIONS 辐射对化学反应的影响

7.39. The chemical reactions proposed for an extraction process were, of course, tested in the laboratory. However, they could not be tested with appreciable amounts of plutonium nor could they be tested in the presence of radiation of anything like the expected intensity. Therefore it was realized that a process found to be successful in the laboratory might not work in the plant.

为提取流程提出的化学反应当然在实验室中经过了试验。然而，既不能用可观数量的钚进行试验，也不能在接近预期强度的辐射存在下进行试验。因此人们认识到，在实验室中证明成功的流程，在工厂中可能行不通。

### CHOICE OF PROCESS 流程的选择

7.40. The description given above as to what was to happen in the successive chambers in the canyon was very vague. This was necessarily so, since even by January 1943 no decision had been made as to what process would be used for the extraction and purification of plutonium. The major problem before the Chemistry Division of the Metallurgical Laboratory was the selection of the best process for the plant.

上文关于峡谷中各相继隔间将发生什么的描述非常笼统。这必然如此，因为甚至到1943年1月，仍未决定将采用何种流程来提取和纯化钚。冶金实验室化学部面临的主要问题是为工厂选择最佳流程。

### THE HEALTH PROBLEM 健康问题

7.41. Besides the hazards normally present during construction and operation of a large chemical plant, dangers of a new kind were expected here. Two types of radiation hazard were anticipated-neutrons generated in the pile, and alpha particles, beta particles, and gamma rays emitted by products of the pile. Although the general effects of these radiations had been proved to be similar to those of X-rays, very little detailed knowledge was available. Obviously the amounts of radioactive material to be handled were many times greater than had ever been encountered before.

除大型化工厂建造和运行期间通常存在的危害外，这里还预期会有新类型的危险。预见到两类辐射危害——反应堆中产生的中子，以及反应堆产物发射的α粒子、β粒子和伽马射线。尽管这些辐射的一般效应已被证明与X射线相似，但可获得的详细知识很少。显然，需要处理的放射性物质数量比以往任何时候所遇到的都要大许多倍。

7.42. The health group had to plan three programs: (1) provision of instruments and clinical tests to detect any evidence of dangerous exposure of the personnel; (2) research on the effects of radiation on persons, instruments, etc.; and (3) estimates of what shielding and safety measures must be incorporated in the design and plan of operation of the plant.

健康组必须规划三项工作：（1）提供仪器和临床检验，以发现人员受到危险照射的任何迹象；（2）研究辐射对人、仪器等的影响；（3）估计在工厂设计和运行计划中必须纳入哪些屏蔽和安全措施。

### THE PROPERTIES OF PLUTONIUM 钚的性质

7.43. Although we were embarking on a major enterprise to produce plutonium, we still had less than a milligram to study and still had only limited familiarity with its properties. The study of plutonium, therefore, remained a major problem of the Metallurgical Laboratory.

尽管我们正在着手一项生产钚的大型事业，但我们可供研究的钚仍不足一毫克，对其性质的了解仍然有限。因此，钚的研究仍是冶金实验室的一项主要课题。

### THE TRAINING OF OPERATORS 操作人员的培训

7.44. Evidently the operation of a full-scale plant of the type planned would require a large and highly skilled group of operators. Although du Pont had a tremendous background of experience in the operation of various kinds of chemical plant, this was something new and it was evident that operating personnel would need special training. Such training was carried out partly in Chicago and its environs, but principally at the Clinton Laboratories.

显然，运行计划中那种类型的全规模工厂，需要一支庞大且技能高超的操作人员队伍。尽管杜邦公司在各类化工厂的运行方面拥有丰富的经验背景，但这是一种新事物，显然操作人员需要专门培训。这种培训部分在芝加哥及其附近进行，但主要在克林顿实验室进行。

### THE NEED FOR FURTHER INFORMATION 对进一步信息的需求

7.45. In the preceding paragraphs of this chapter we have outlined the problems confronting the group charged with. designing and building a plutonium production plant. In [Chapter VI](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_vi.html) the progress in this field up to the end of 1942 was reviewed. Throughout these chapters it is made clear that a great deal more information was required to assure the success of the plant. Such answers as had been obtained to most of the questions were only tentative. Consequently research had to be pushed simultaneously with planning and construction.

在本章前面的段落中，我们概述了负责设计和建造钚生产工厂的团队所面临的问题。[第六章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_vi.html)回顾了到1942年底这一领域的进展。贯穿这些章节可以清楚地看到，为确保工厂的成功还需要多得多的信息。对大多数问题所得到的答案都只是暂定的。因此，研究必须与规划和建设同时推进。

### THE RESEARCH PROGRAM 研究计划

7.46. To meet the need for further information, research programs were laid out for the Metallurgical Laboratory and the Clinton Laboratory. The following passage is an excerpt from the 1943 program of the Metallurgical Project:

为满足对进一步信息的需求，为冶金实验室和克林顿实验室制定了研究计划。以下段落摘自冶金项目1943年计划：

“*Product Production Studies*. These include all aspects of the research, development and semi-works studies necessary for the design, construction, and operation of chain-reacting piles to produce plutonium or other materials.

“*产品生产研究*。包括为设计、建造和运行用于生产钚或其他材料的链式反应堆所必需的研究、开发和半工厂研究的各个方面。

> *Pile Characteristics*. Theoretical studies and experiments on lattice structures to predict behavior in high-level piles, such as temperature and barometric effects, neutron characteristics, pile poisoning, etc.
> *Control of Reacting Units*. Design and experimental tests of devices for controlling rate of reaction in piles.
> *Cooling of Reacting Units*. Physical studies of coolant material, engineering problems of circulation, corrosion, erosion, etc.
> *Instrumentation*. Development of instruments and technique for monitoring pile and surveying radiation throughout plant area.
> *Protection*. Shielding, biological effects of radiation at pile and clinical effects of operations associated with pile.
> *Materials*. Study of physical (mechanical and nuclear) properties of construction and process materials used in pile construction and operation.
> *Activation Investigations*. Production of experimental amounts of radioactive materials in cyclotron and in piles and study of activation of materials by neutrons, protons, electrons, gamma rays, etc.
> *Pile Operation*. Study of pile operation procedures such as materials handling, instrument operation, etc.
> *Process Design*. Study of possible production processes as a whole leading to detailed work in other categories.

> *反应堆特性*。关于栅格结构的理论研究和实验，以预测高功率反应堆中的行为，如温度和气压效应、中子特性、反应堆中毒等。
> *反应单元的控制*。控制反应堆中反应速率的装置的设计与实验检验。
> *反应单元的冷却*。冷却剂材料的物理研究，循环、腐蚀、侵蚀等工程问题。
> *仪器*。开发用于监测反应堆和勘查全厂区域辐射的仪器与技术。
> *防护*。屏蔽、反应堆处辐射的生物学效应以及反应堆相关操作的临床效应。
> *材料*。研究用于反应堆建造和运行的结构材料与工艺材料的物理（力学和核）性质。
> *活化研究*。在回旋加速器和反应堆中生产实验用数量的放射性材料，并研究中子、质子、电子、伽马射线等对材料的活化。
> *反应堆运行*。研究反应堆运行程序，如材料搬运、仪器操作等。
> *流程设计*。把可能的生产流程作为一个整体加以研究，从而引出其他类别中的详细工作。

“Product Recovery Studies. These include all aspects of the work necessary for the development of processes for the extraction of plutonium and possible by-products from the pile material and their preparation in purified form. Major effort at the Metallurgical Laboratory will be on a single process to be selected by June 1, 1943 for the production of plutonium, but alternatives will continue to be studied both at the Metallurgical Laboratory and Clinton with whatever manpower is available.

“产品回收研究。包括为开发从反应堆材料中提取钚及可能副产物并将其制备成纯化形态的流程所必需的一切方面的工作。冶金实验室的主要力量将集中于1943年6月1日前选定的单一钚生产流程，但冶金实验室和克林顿将继续以可用的人力研究各种替代方案。

> *Separation*. Processes for solution of uranium, extraction of plutonium and decontamination by removal of fission products.
> *Concentration, Purification and Product Reduction*. Processes leading to production of plutonium as pure metal, and study of properties of plutonium necessary to its production.
> *Wastes*. Disposal and possible methods of recovery of fission products and metal from wastes.
> *Instrumentation*. Development and testing of instruments for monitoring chemical processes and surveying radiation throughout the area.
> *Protection*. Shielding studies, determination of biological effects of radioactive dusts, liquids, solids, and other process materials, and protective measures.
> *Materials*. Corrosion of equipment 'materials, and radiation stability. Necessary purity and purity analysis of process materials, etc.
> *Recovery of Activated Materials*. Development of methods and actual recovery of activated material (tracers, etc.) from cyclotron and pile-activated materials.
> *Operations Studies*. Equipment performance, process control, material handling operations, etc.
> *Process Design*. Study of product recovery processes as a whole (wet processes, physical methods) leading to detailed work in other categories.

> *分离*。铀的溶解、钚的提取以及通过去除裂变产物实现去污的流程。
> *浓缩、纯化与产品还原*。制取纯金属钚的流程，以及为生产钚所必需的钚性质研究。
> *废物*。处置以及从废物中回收裂变产物和金属的可能方法。
> *仪器*。开发和检验用于监测化学过程和勘查全区域辐射的仪器。
> *防护*。屏蔽研究，确定放射性粉尘、液体、固体及其他工艺材料的生物学效应，以及防护措施。
> *材料*。设备的腐蚀与材料，以及辐射稳定性。工艺材料所需的纯度及纯度分析等。
> *活化材料的回收*。开发从回旋加速器和反应堆活化材料中实际回收活化材料（示踪剂等）的方法。
> *运行研究*。设备性能、过程控制、材料搬运操作等。
> *流程设计*。把产品回收流程作为一个整体加以研究（湿法流程、物理方法），从而引出其他类别中的详细工作。

“Fundamental Research. Studies of the fundamental physical, chemical and biological phenomena occurring in chain-reacting piles, and basic properties of all materials involved. Although the primary emphasis at Clinton is on the semi-works level, much fundamental research will require Clinton conditions (high radiation intensity, large scale processes).

“基础研究。研究链式反应堆中发生的基本物理、化学和生物现象，以及所涉一切材料的基本性质。虽然克林顿的主要重点在半工厂层面，但许多基础研究将需要克林顿的条件（高辐射强度、大规模流程）。

> *Nuclear Physics*. Fundamental properties of nuclear fission such as cross section, neutron yield, fission species, etc. Other nuclear properties important to processes, such as cross sections, properties of moderators, neutron effect on materials, etc.
> *General Physics*. Basic instrument (electronic, ionization, optical, etc.) research, atomic mass determinations, neutron, alpha, beta, gamma radiation studies, X-ray investigations, etc.
> *Radiation Chemistry*. Effects of radiation on chemical processes and chemical reactions produced by radiation.
> *Nuclear Chemistry*. Tracing of fission products, disintegration constants, chains, investigation of nuclei of possible use to project.
> *Product Chemistry*. Chemical properties of various products and basic studies in separation and purification of products.
> *General Chemistry*. Chemistry of primary materials and materials associated with process, including by-products.
> *General Biology*. Fundamental studies of effects of radiation on living matter, metabolism of important materials, etc.
> *Clinical Investigations*. Basic investigations, such as hematology, pathology, etc.
> *Metallurgical Studies*. Properties of U, Pu, Be, etc.
> *Engineering Studies*. Phenomena basic to corrosion and similar studies essential to continued engineering development of processes.”

> *核物理学*。核裂变的基本性质，如截面、中子产额、裂变物种等。对流程重要的其他核性质，如截面、慢化剂的性质、中子对材料的影响等。
> *普通物理学*。基础仪器（电子、电离、光学等）研究，原子质量测定，中子、α、β、伽马辐射研究，X射线研究等。
> *辐射化学*。辐射对化学过程的影响以及由辐射产生的化学反应。
> *核化学*。裂变产物的追踪、衰变常数、链，以及对项目可能有用的原子核的研究。
> *产品化学*。各种产品的化学性质，以及产品分离和纯化的基础研究。
> *普通化学*。主要材料及与流程相关材料（包括副产物）的化学。
> *普通生物学*。辐射对生命物质影响的基础研究、重要物质的代谢等。
> *临床研究*。基础研究，如血液学、病理学等。
> *冶金研究*。U、Pu、Be等的性质。
> *工程研究*。腐蚀的基础现象以及为流程的持续工程开发所必需的类似研究。

7.47. An examination of this program gives an idea of the great range of investigations which were considered likely to give relevant information. Many of the topics listed are not specific research problems such as might be solved by a small team of scientists working for a few months but are whole fields of investigation that might be studied with profit for years. It was necessary to pick the specific problems that were likely to give the most immediately useful results but at the same time it was desirable to try to uncover general principles. For example, the effect of radiation on the properties of materials (“radiation stability”) was almost entirely unknown. It was necessary both to make empirical tests on particular materials that might be used in a pile and to devise general theories of the observed effects. Every effort was made to relate all work to the general objective: a successful production plant.

对这一计划加以审视，可以看出被认为可能提供有关信息的调查范围之广。所列许多课题并非具体的研究问题（那种可以由一小队科学家在几个月内解决的问题），而是可以有益地研究多年的整个研究领域。必须挑选那些可能产生最直接有用成果的具体问题，但同时也有必要努力揭示一般原理。例如，辐射对材料性质的影响（“辐射稳定性”）几乎完全未知。既必须对可能用于反应堆的特定材料进行经验性试验，也必须建立关于所观察效应的普遍理论。人们尽一切努力使所有工作都与总目标——一座成功的生产工厂——相联系。

这些人的职责是确保上述研究计划得到执行，并把重要结果报告给杜邦公司。他们还有责任回答杜邦公司提出的问题，并对杜邦公司提交的计划予以批准或批评。

### COOPERATION BETWEEN THE METALLURGICAL LABORATORY AND DU PONT 冶金实验室与杜邦公司之间的合作

7.50. Since du Pont was the design and construction organization and the Metallurgical Laboratory was the research organization, it was obvious that close cooperation was essential. Not only did du Pont need answers to specific questions, but they could benefit by criticism and suggestions on the many points where the Metallurgical group was especially well-informed. Similarly, the Metallurgical group could profit by the knowledge of du Pont on many technical questions of design, construction, and operation. To promote this kind of cooperation du Pont stationed one of their physicists, J. B. Miles, at Chicago, and had many other du Pont men, particularly C. H. Greenewalt, spend much of their time at Chicago. Miles and Greenewalt regularly attended meetings of the Laboratory Council. There was no similar reciprocal arrangement although many members of the laboratory visited Wilmington informally. In addition, J. A. Wheeler was transferred from Chicago to Wilmington and became a member of the du Pont staff. There was, of course, constant exchange of reports and letters, and conferences were held frequently between Compton and R. Williams of du Pont. Whitaker spent much of his time at Wilmington during the period when the Clinton plant was being designed and constructed.

由于杜邦公司是设计和建设单位，而冶金实验室是研究单位，显然密切合作必不可少。杜邦公司不仅需要具体问题的答案，而且可以在冶金组特别熟悉的许多问题上从批评和建议中受益。同样，冶金组也可以从杜邦公司在设计、建造和运行的许多技术问题上的知识中获益。为促进这种合作，杜邦公司把他们的一位物理学家J. B.迈尔斯（J. B. Miles）派驻芝加哥，并让许多其他杜邦人员，特别是C. H.格林沃尔特（C. H. Greenewalt），把大量时间花在芝加哥。迈尔斯和格林沃尔特定期参加实验室委员会的会议。没有类似的互惠安排，尽管实验室的许多成员非正式地访问了威尔明顿。此外，J. A.惠勒（J. A. Wheeler）从芝加哥转到威尔明顿，成为杜邦公司的工作人员。当然，报告和信件不断交流，康普顿与杜邦公司的R.威廉斯（R. Williams）经常举行会议。在克林顿工厂设计和建造期间，惠特克把大量时间花在威尔明顿。

### SUMMARY 小结

7.51. By January 1943, the decision had been made to build a plutonium production plant with a large capacity. This meant a pile developing thousands of kilowatts and a chemical separation plant to extract the product. The du Pont Company was to design, construct, and operate the plant; the Metallurgical Laboratory was to do the necessary research. A site was chosen on the Columbia River at Hanford, Washington. A tentative decision to build a helium-cooled plant was reversed in favor of water-cooling. The principal problems were those involving lattice design, loading and unloading, choice of materials particularly with reference to corrosion and radiation, water supply, controls and instrumentation, health hazards, chemical separation process, and design of the separation plant. Plans were made for the necessary fundamental and technical research and for the training of operators. Arrangements were made for liaison between du Pont and the Metallurgical Laboratory.

到1943年1月，已经作出建造一座生产能力很大的钚生产工厂的决定。这意味着要有一座产生数千千瓦的反应堆和一座提取产品的化学分离工厂。杜邦公司将设计、建造和运行该工厂；冶金实验室将进行必要的研究。在华盛顿州汉福德的哥伦比亚河畔选定了一处场址。建造氦冷却工厂的暂定决定被推翻，转而支持水冷却。主要问题涉及栅格设计、装料与卸料、材料选择（特别是关于腐蚀和辐射）、供水、控制与仪器、健康危害、化学分离流程以及分离工厂的设计。为必要的基础研究和技术研究以及操作人员的培训制定了计划。为杜邦公司与冶金实验室之间的联络作了安排。
