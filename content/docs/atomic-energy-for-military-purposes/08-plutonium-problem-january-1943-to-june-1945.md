### INTRODUCTION 引言

8.1. The necessity for pushing the design and construction of the full-scale plutonium plant simultaneously with research and development inevitably led to a certain amount of confusion and inefficiency. It became essential to investigate many alternative processes. It became necessary to investigate all possible causes of failure even when the probability of their becoming serious was very small. Now that the Hanford plant is producing plutonium successfully, we believe it is fair to say that a large percentage of the results of investigation made between the end of 1942 and the end of 1944 will never be used-at least not for the originally intended purposes. Nevertheless had the Hanford plant run into difficulties, anyone of the now superfluous investigations might have furnished just the information required to convert failure into success. Even now it is impossible to say that future improvements may not depend on the results of researches that seem unimportant today.

推动全尺寸钚工厂的设计与建造同研究与开发同步进行的必要性，不可避免地造成了一定程度的混乱与低效。必须研究许多备选工艺。必须研究一切可能的失败原因，即使这些原因变得严重的概率很小。如今汉福德工厂已成功生产钚，我们认为可以公正地说，1942年底至1944年底之间所进行的研究成果，很大一部分将永远不会被使用——至少不会用于最初设想的目的。然而，倘若汉福德工厂遇到困难，任何一项如今看来多余的调查都可能恰好提供所需的信息，使失败转为成功。即使在现在，也无法断言未来的改进不会依赖于今天看来并不重要的研究成果。

8.2. It is estimated that thirty volumes will be required for a complete report of the significant scientific results of researches conducted under the auspices of the Metallurgical Project. Work was done on every item mentioned on the research program presented in the last chapter. In the present account it would be obviously impossible to give more than a brief abstract of all these researches. We believe this would be unsatisfactory and that it is preferable to give a general discussion of the chain-reacting units and separation plants as they now operate, with some discussion of the earlier developments.

据估计，要完整报告在冶金项目主持下所进行的各项研究的重要科学成果，将需要三十卷篇幅。上一章提出的研究计划中所列出的每一个项目都开展了工作。在本报告中，显然不可能对这些研究作超出简要摘要的叙述。我们认为那样做不能令人满意，因此更可取的做法是，对目前运行中的链式反应装置和分离工厂作一般性讨论，并对早期的发展作一些说明。

### THE CHAIN REACTION IN A PILE 反应堆中的链式反应

8.3. In [Chapter I](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_i-a.html) and other early chapters we have given brief accounts of the fission process, pile operation, and chemical separation. We shall now review these topics from a somewhat different point of view before describing the plutonium production plants themselves.

在[第一章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_i-a.html)及其他早期章节中，我们简要叙述了裂变过程、反应堆运行和化学分离。现在，在描述钚生产工厂本身之前，我们将从略有不同的角度回顾这些主题。

8.4. The operation of a pile depends on the passage of neutrons through matter and on the nature of the collisions of neutrons with the nuclei encountered. The collisions of principal importance are the following:

反应堆的运行取决于中子穿过物质的过程，以及中子与所遇原子核碰撞的性质。最重要的碰撞如下：

I. Collisions in which neutrons are scattered and lose appreciable amounts of energy. (a) Inelastic collisions of fast neutrons with uranium nuclei. (b) Elastic collisions of fast or moderately fast neutrons with the light nuclei of the moderator material; these collisions serve to reduce the neutron energy to very low (so-called thermal) energies.

I. 中子被散射并损失可观能量的碰撞。(a) 快中子与铀核的非弹性碰撞。(b) 快中子或中等速度中子与慢化剂材料轻核的弹性碰撞；这些碰撞的作用是把中子能量降低到很低的（所谓热）能量。

II. Collisions in which the neutrons are absorbed. (a) Collisions which result in fission of nuclei and give fission products and additional neutrons. (b) Collisions which result in the formation of new nuclei which subsequently disintegrate radioactively (e.g., <sub>92</sub>U<sup>239</sup> which produces <sub>94</sub>Pu<sup>239</sup>).

II. 中子被吸收的碰撞。(a) 导致核裂变并产生裂变产物和额外中子的碰撞。(b) 导致形成新核、随后发生放射性衰变的碰撞（例如 <sub>92</sub>U<sup>239</sup> 生成 <sub>94</sub>Pu<sup>239</sup>）。

8.5. Only the second class of collision requires further discussion. As regards collisions of Type II (a), the most important in a pile are the collisions between neutrons and U-235, but the high-energy fission of U -238 and the thermal fission of Pu-239 also take place. Collisions of Type II (b) are chiefly those between neutrons and U-238. Such collisions occur for neutrons of all energies, but they are most likely to occur for neutrons whose energies lie in the ”resonance” region located somewhat above thermal energies. The sequence of results of the Type II (b) collision is represented as follows:

只有第二类碰撞需要进一步讨论。就 II(a) 型碰撞而言，反应堆中最重要的是中子与 U-235 的碰撞，但 U-238 的高能裂变和 Pu-239 的热裂变也会发生。II(b) 型碰撞主要是中子与 U-238 之间的碰撞。这类碰撞对一切能量的中子都会发生，但对能量位于热能之上不远的“共振”区内的中子最为可能发生。II(b) 型碰撞的一系列结果表示如下：

8.6. Any other non-fission absorption processes are important chiefly because they waste neutrons; they occur in the moderator, in U-235, in the coolant, in the impurities originally present, in the fission products, and even in plutonium itself.

任何其他非裂变吸收过程之所以重要，主要在于它们浪费中子；这类过程发生在慢化剂、U-235、冷却剂、最初存在的杂质、裂变产物乃至钚本身之中。

8.7. Since the object of the chain reaction is to generate plutonium, we would like to absorb all excess neutrons in U-238, leaving just enough neutrons to produce fission and thus to maintain the chain reaction. Actually the tendency of the neutrons to be absorbed by the dominant isotope U-238 is so great compared to their tendency to produce fission in the 140-times-rarer U-235 that the principal design effort had to be directed toward favoring the fission (as by using a moderator, a suitable lattice, materials of high purity, etc.,) in order to maintain the chain reaction.

由于链式反应的目的在于生成钚，我们希望把全部过剩中子都在 U-238 中吸收，只留下恰好足够的中子来产生裂变，从而维持链式反应。实际上，与在稀少140倍的 U-235 中产生裂变的倾向相比，中子被占主导地位的同位素 U-238 吸收的倾向如此之大，以致主要的设计工作不得不致力于有利于裂变（例如采用慢化剂、合适的栅格、高纯度材料等），以维持链式反应。

### LIFE HISTORY OF ONE GENERATION OF NEUTRONS 一代中子的生命史

8.8. All the chain-reacting piles designed by the Metallurgical Laboratory or with its cooperation consist of four categories of material-the uranium metal, the moderator, the coolant, and the auxiliary materials such as water tubes, casings of uranium, control strips or rods, impurities, etc. All the piles depend on stray neutrons from spontaneous fission or cosmic rays to initiate the reaction.

冶金实验室自行设计或合作设计的所有链式反应堆，都由四类材料构成——铀金属、慢化剂、冷却剂，以及诸如水管、铀套管、控制条或控制棒、杂质等辅助材料。所有反应堆都依靠自发裂变或宇宙射线产生的杂散中子来引发反应。

8.9. Suppose that the pile were to be started by simultaneous release (in the uranium metal) of N high-energy neutrons. Most of these neutrons originally have energies above the threshold energy of fission of U -238. However, as the neutrons pass back and forth in the metal and moderator, they suffer numerous inelastic collisions with the uranium and numerous elastic collisions with the moderator, and all these collisions serve to reduce the energies below that threshold. Specifically, in a typical graphite-moderated pile a neutron that has escaped from the uranium into the graphite travels on the average about 2.5 centimeters between collisions and makes on the *average* about 200 elastic collisions before passing from the graphite back into the uranium. Since at each such collision a neutron loses on the average about one sixth of its energy, a one-Mev neutron is reduced to thermal energy (usually taken to be 0.025 electron volt) considerably before completing a single transit through the graphite. There are, of course, many neutrons that depart from this average behavior, and there will be enough fissions produced by fast neutrons to enhance slightly the number of neutrons present. The enhancement may be taken into account by multiplying the original number of neutrons N by a factor ε which is called the fast-fission effect or the fast-multiplication factor.

假设反应堆通过（在铀金属中）同时释放 N 个高能中子而启动。这些中子大多最初具有高于 U-238 裂变阈能的能量。然而，当中子在金属和慢化剂中来回穿行时，会与铀发生大量非弹性碰撞，与慢化剂发生大量弹性碰撞，所有这些碰撞都使能量降到该阈能以下。具体而言，在典型的石墨慢化反应堆中，一个从铀中逃入石墨的中子，在两次碰撞之间平均行进约2.5厘米，并在从石墨返回铀之前平均发生约200次弹性碰撞。由于在每次这样的碰撞中中子平均损失约六分之一的能量，一个1兆电子伏特的中子在完成一次穿过石墨的行程之前很久就已降到热能（通常取为0.025电子伏特）。当然，有许多中子偏离这种平均行为，快中子产生的裂变将足以略微增加所存在的中子数。这一增加可以通过将原始中子数 N 乘以一个因子 ε 来计入，ε 称为快裂变效应或快倍增因子。

8.10. As the average energy of the Nε neutrons present continues to fall, inelastic collision in the uranium becomes unimportant, the energy being reduced essentially only in the moderator. However, the chance of non-fission absorption (resonance capture) in U-238 becomes significant as the intermediate or resonance energy region is reached. Actually quite a number of neutrons in this energy region will be absorbed regardless of choice of lattice design. The effect of such capture may be expressed by multiplying Nε by a factor p, (which is always less than one) called the ”resonance escape probability” which is the probability that a given neutron starting with energy above the resonance region will reach thermal energies without absorption in U-238. Thus from the original N high-energy neutrons we obtain Nεp neutrons of thermal energy.

随着所存在的 Nε 个中子的平均能量继续下降，铀中的非弹性碰撞变得不重要，能量基本上只在慢化剂中降低。然而，当达到中能或共振能区时，U-238 中非裂变吸收（共振俘获）的机会变得显著。实际上，无论选择何种栅格设计，这一能区都有相当多的中子会被吸收。这种俘获的效应可以用将 Nε 乘以一个因子 p 来表示，p（总是小于1）称为“共振逃逸概率”，即一个给定中子从共振区以上的能量出发、在未被 U-238 吸收的情况下到达热能的概率。于是，从最初的 N 个高能中子，我们得到 Nεp 个热能中子。

8.11. Once a neutron has reached thermal energy the chance that it will lose more energy by collision is no greater than the chance that it will gain energy. Consequently the neutrons will remain at this average energy until they are absorbed. In the thermal-energy region the chance for absorption of the neutron by the moderator, the coolant and the auxiliary materials is greater than at higher energies. At any rate it is found that we introduce little error into our calculations by assuming all such unwanted absorption takes place in this energy region. We now introduce a factor f, called the thermal utilization factor, which is defined as the probability that a given thermal neutron will be absorbed in the uranium. Thus from the original N fast neutrons we have obtained Nεpf thermal neutrons which are absorbed by uranium.

一旦中子达到热能，它因碰撞而进一步损失能量的机会并不大于它获得能量的机会。因此，中子在未被吸收之前将保持在这一平均能量。在热能区，中子被慢化剂、冷却剂和辅助材料吸收的机会大于在更高能量时。无论如何，可以发现在假定所有这类不需要的吸收都发生于该能区时，引入计算的误差很小。现在我们引入一个因子 f，称为热利用因子，其定义为给定的热中子将在铀中被吸收的概率。于是，从最初的 N 个快中子，我们得到 Nεpf 个被铀吸收的热中子。

8.12. Although there are several ways in which the normal mixture of uranium isotopes can absorb neutrons, the reader may recall that we defined in a previous chapter a quantity η, which is the number of fission neutrons produced for each thermal neutron absorbed in uranium regardless of the details of the process. If, therefore, we multiply the number of thermal neutrons absorbed in uranium, Nεpf, by η, we have the number of new high speed neutrons generated by the original N high speed neutrons in the course of their lives. If Nεpfη is greater than N, we have a chain reaction and the number of neutrons is continually increasing. Evidently the product εpfη = k<sub>∞</sub>, the multiplication factor already defined in [Chapter IV](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_iv.html).

尽管铀同位素的正常混合物可以通过若干方式吸收中子，读者可能还记得我们在前一章定义了量 η，即铀中每吸收一个热中子所产生的裂变中子数，而不论过程的具体细节。因此，若将铀中吸收的热中子数 Nεpf 乘以 η，就得到最初 N 个快中子在生命过程中所产生的新快中子数。若 Nεpfη 大于 N，便发生链式反应，中子数不断增加。显然，乘积 εpfη = k<sub>∞</sub>，即[第四章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_iv.html)中已定义的倍增因子。

8.13. Note that no mention has been made of neutrons escaping from the pile. Such mention has been deliberately avoided since the value of k<sub>∞</sub> as defined above applies to an infinite lattice. From the known values of k<sub>∞</sub> and the fact that these piles do operate, one finds that the percentage of neutrons escaping cannot be very great. As we saw in [Chapter II](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_ii.html), the escape of neutrons becomes relatively less important as the size of the pile increases. If it is necessary to introduce in the pile a large amount of auxiliary material such as cooling-system pipes, it is necessary to build a somewhat larger pile to counteract the increase in absorption.

请注意，尚未提到从反应堆中逃逸的中子。这种提及是有意避免的，因为上文定义的 k<sub>∞</sub> 值适用于无限栅格。由已知的 k<sub>∞</sub> 值以及这些反应堆确实运行这一事实可以推知，逃逸中子的百分比不可能很大。正如我们在[第二章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_ii.html)中所见，随着反应堆尺寸增大，中子逃逸相对而言变得不那么重要。如果必须在反应堆中引入大量辅助材料，例如冷却系统管道，就必须建造稍大一些的反应堆，以抵消吸收的增加。

8.14. To sum up, a pile operates by reducing high-energy neutrons to thermal energies by the use of a moderator-lattice arrangement, then allowing the thermal-energy neutrons to be absorbed by uranium, causing fission which regenerates further high-energy neutrons. The regeneration of neutrons is aided slightly by the fast neutron effect; it is impeded by resonance absorption during the process of energy reduction, by absorption in graphite and other materials, and by neutron escape.

总之，反应堆的运行方式是：利用慢化剂栅格布置把高能中子降为热能，然后让热能中子被铀吸收，引起裂变并再生出更多高能中子。中子的再生略受快中子效应之助；它受到能量降低过程中的共振吸收、石墨及其他材料中的吸收以及中子逃逸的阻碍。

### THE EFFECTS OF REACTION PRODUCTS ON THE MULTIPLICATION FACTOR 反应产物对倍增因子的影响

8.15. Even at the high power level used in the Hanford piles, only a few grams of U-238 and of U-235 are used up per day per million grams of uranium present. Nevertheless the effects of these changes are very important. As the U-235 is becoming depleted, the concentration of plutonium is increasing. Fortunately, plutonium itself is fissionable by thermal neutrons and so tends to counterbalance the decrease of U-235 as far as maintaining the chain reaction is concerned. However, other fission products are being produced also. These consist typically of unstable and relatively unfamiliar nuclei so that it was originally impossible to predict how great an undesirable effect they would have on the multiplication constant. Such deleterious effects are called poisoning.

即使在汉福德反应堆所使用的高功率水平下，每百万克所存在的铀每天也只消耗几克 U-238 和 U-235。然而，这些变化的影响非常重要。随着 U-235 逐渐贫化，钚的浓度不断增加。幸运的是，钚本身可被热中子裂变，所以就维持链式反应而言，它倾向于抵消 U-235 的减少。然而，其他裂变产物也在不断产生。这些产物典型地由不稳定的、相对陌生的原子核组成，因此最初无法预言它们对倍增常数会产生多大的不良影响。这类有害影响称为中毒。

### THE REACTION PRODUCTS AND THE SEPARATION PROBLEM 反应产物与分离问题

8.16. There are two main parts of the plutonium production process at Hanford: actual production in the pile, and separation of the plutonium from the uranium slugs in which it is formed. We turn now to a discussion of the second part, the separation process.

汉福德钚生产过程有两个主要部分：在反应堆中的实际生产，以及从生成钚的铀棒中分离钚。现在我们转向第二部分，即分离过程的讨论。

8.17. The uranium slugs containing plutonium also contain other elements resulting from the fission of U-235. When a U-235 nucleus undergoes fission, it emits one or more neutrons and splits into two fragments of comparable size and of total mass 235 or less. Apparently fission into precisely equal masses rarely occurs, the most abundant fragments being a fragment of mass number between 134 and 144 and a fragment of mass number between 100 and 90. Thus there are two groups of fission products: a heavy group with mass numbers extending approximately from 127 to 154, and a light group from approximately 115 to 83: These fission products are in the main unstable isotopes of the thirty or so known elements in these general ranges of mass number. Typically they decay by successive beta emissions accompanied by gamma radiation finally to form known stable nuclei. The half-lives of the various intermediate nuclei range from fractions of a second to a year or more; several of the important species have half-lives of the order of a month or so. About twenty different elements are present in significant concentration. The most abundant of these comprises slightly less than 10 per cent of the aggregate.

含钚的铀棒还含有 U-235 裂变产生的其他元素。当一个 U-235 核发生裂变时，它发射一个或多个中子，并分裂为两个大小相近、总质量为235或更小的碎片。显然，分裂为完全相等的质量很少发生，最丰富的碎片是质量数在134至144之间的碎片和质量数在100至90之间的碎片。因此有两组裂变产物：重组，质量数大致从127延伸到154；轻组，大致从115到83。这些裂变产物主要是这些质量数范围内约三十种已知元素的不稳定同位素。它们典型地通过连续的β发射并伴随伽马辐射而衰变，最终形成已知的稳定核。各种中间核的半衰期从几分之一秒到一年或更长；其中几种重要物种的半衰期约为一个月左右。约有二十种不同元素以显著浓度存在。其中最丰富者略低于总量的10%。

8.18. In addition to radioactive fission products, U-239 and Np-239 (intermediate products in the formation of plutonium) are present in the pile and are radioactive. The concentrations of all these products begin to build up at the moment the pile starts operating. Eventually the rate of radioactive decay equals the rate of formation so that the concentrations become constant. For example, the number of atoms of U-239 produced per second is constant for a pile operating at a fixed power level. According to the laws of radioactive disintegration, the number of U-239 atoms disappearing per second is proportional to the number of such atoms present and is thus increasing during the first few minutes or hours after the pile is put into operation. Consequently there soon will be practically as many nuclei disintegrating each second as are formed each second. Equilibrium concentrations for other nuclei will be approached in similar manner, the equilibrium concentration being proportional to the rate of formation of the nucleus and to its half-life. Products which are stable or of extremely long half-life (e.g., plutonium) will steadily increase in concentration for a considerable time. When the pile is stopped, the radioactivity of course continues, but at a continually diminishing absolute rate. Isotopes of very short half-life may ”drop out of sight” in a few minutes or hours; others of longer half-life keep appreciably active for days or months. Thus at any time the concentrations of the various products in a recently stopped pile depend on what the power level was, on how long the pile ran, and on how long it has been shut down. Of course, the longer the pile has run, the larger is the concentration of plutonium and (unfortunately) the larger is the concentration of long-lived fission products. The longer the ”cooling” period, i.e., the period between removal of material from the pile and chemical treatment, the lower is the radiation intensity from the fission products. A compromise must be made between such considerations as the desire for a long running and cooling time on the one hand and the desire for early extraction of the plutonium on the other hand.

除放射性裂变产物外，反应堆中还存在着 U-239 和 Np-239（钚形成过程中的中间产物），它们也具有放射性。所有这些产物的浓度从反应堆开始运行的时刻起便开始积累。最终，放射性衰变速率等于生成速率，于是浓度变为恒定。例如，对于在固定功率水平下运行的反应堆，每秒生成的 U-239 原子数是恒定的。按照放射性衰变定律，每秒消失的 U-239 原子数与所存在的此类原子数成正比，因此在反应堆投入运行后的最初几分钟或几小时内不断增加。于是，不久之后每秒衰变的核数实际上将与每秒生成的核数一样多。其他核的平衡浓度将以类似方式趋近，平衡浓度与该核的生成速率及其半衰期成正比。稳定或半衰期极长的产物（例如钚）将在相当长的时间内浓度稳步增加。反应堆停堆后，放射性当然继续存在，但绝对速率不断减小。半衰期很短的同位素可能在几分钟或几小时内“消失不见”；半衰期较长的其他同位素则在数天或数月内仍保持可观的放射性。因此，在刚停堆的反应堆中，各种产物的浓度在任何时刻都取决于功率水平是多少、反应堆运行了多久以及停堆了多久。当然，反应堆运行时间越长，钚的浓度越大，而且（不幸的是）长寿命裂变产物的浓度也越大。“冷却”期，即从反应堆取出材料到进行化学处理之间的时期，越长，裂变产物的辐射强度就越低。必须在诸如希望运行时间长和冷却时间长这一方面与希望尽早提取钚这一方面之间作出折中。

8.19. Tables can be prepared showing the chemical concentrations of plutonium and the various fission products as functions of power level, length of operation, and length of cooling period. The half-life of the U-239 is so short that its concentration becomes negligible soon after the pile shuts down. The neptunium becomes converted fairly rapidly to plutonium. Of course, the total weight of fission products, stable and unstable, remains practically constant after the pile is stopped. For the Clinton and Hanford operating conditions the maximum plutonium concentration attained is so small as to add materially to the difficulty of chemical separation.

可以编制表格，显示钚和各种裂变产物的化学浓度随功率水平、运行时长和冷却期长短变化的函数关系。U-239 的半衰期很短，因此反应堆停堆后不久其浓度便变得可以忽略。镎相当迅速地转变为钚。当然，裂变产物（稳定与不稳定）的总重量在反应堆停堆后实际上保持不变。就克林顿和汉福德的运行条件而言，所达到的最大钚浓度小到足以大大增加化学分离的困难。

### THE CHOICE OF A CHEMICAL SEPARATION PROCESS 化学分离工艺的选择

8.20. The problem then is to make a chemical separation at the daily rate of, say, several grams of plutonium from several thousand grams of uranium contaminated with large amounts of dangerously radioactive fission products comprising twenty different elements. The problem is especially difficult as the plutonium purity requirements are very high indeed.

于是，问题在于每天以例如几克钚的速率，从数千克被大量危险放射性裂变产物（包含二十种不同元素）污染的铀中进行化学分离。由于对钚纯度的要求确实非常高，这一问题尤其困难。

8.21. Four types of method for chemical separation were examined: volatility, absorption, solvent extraction, and precipitation. The work on absorption and solvent extraction methods has been extensive and such methods may be increasingly used in the main process or in waste recovery, but the Hanford Plant was designed for a precipitation process.

考察了四类化学分离方法：挥发性、吸附、溶剂萃取和沉淀。关于吸附和溶剂萃取方法的工作已很广泛，这类方法可能越来越多地用于主工艺或废料回收，但汉福德工厂是按沉淀工艺设计的。

Paragraphs 8.22-8.26 are quoted or paraphrased from a general report of the Metallurgical Laboratory prepared in the spring of 1945.

第8.22至8.26段引自或转述自冶金实验室1945年春编写的一份综合报告。

8.22. The phenomena of co-precipitation, i.e., the precipitation of small concentrations of one element along with a ”carrier” precipitate of some other element, had been commonly used in radioactive chemistry, and was adopted for plutonium separation. The early work on plutonium chemistry, confined as it was to minute amounts of the element, made great use of precipitation reactions from which solubility properties could be deduced. It was therefore natural that precipitation methods of separation were the most advanced at the time when the plant design was started. It was felt that, should the several steps in the separations process have to be developed partly by the empirical approach, there would be less risk in the scale-up of a precipitation process than, for example, of one involving solid-phase reactions. In addition, the precipitation processes then in mind could be broken into a sequence of repeated operations (called cycles), thereby limiting the number of different equipment pieces requiring design and allowing considerable process change without equipment change. Thus, while the basic plant design was made with one method in mind, the final choice of a different method led to no embarrassments.

共沉淀现象，即一种元素的少量浓度随另一种元素的“载体”沉淀一同沉淀的现象，在放射化学中早已普遍使用，并被采用于钚的分离。早期钚化学工作由于所处理的只是微量该元素，因而大量利用沉淀反应，并从中推断溶解度性质。因此，在工厂设计开始时，沉淀分离方法最为先进，这是很自然的。当时认为，如果分离过程中的若干步骤不得不部分地依靠经验方法加以开发，那么放大沉淀工艺的风险要比例如涉及固相反应的工艺更小。此外，当时设想的沉淀工艺可以分解为一系列重复操作（称为循环），从而限制了需要设计的不同设备的数量，并允许在不更换设备的情况下对工艺作相当大的改变。因此，虽然基本工厂设计是按一种方法进行的，最终选择另一种方法并未造成任何困难。

8.23. Most of the precipitation processes which have received serious consideration made use of an alternation between the (IV) and (VI) oxidation states of plutonium. Such processes involve a precipitation of plutonium (IV) with a certain compound as a carrier, then dissolution of the precipitate, oxidation of the plutonium to the (VI) state, and reprecipitation of the carrier compound while the plutonium (VI) remains in solution. Fission products which are not carried by these compounds remain in solution when plutonium (IV) is precipitated. The fission products which carry are removed from the plutonium when it is in the (VI) state. Successive oxidation-reduction cycles are carried out until the desired decontamination is achieved. The process of elimination of the fission products is called decontamination and the degree of elimination is tested by measuring the change in radioactivity of the material.

受到认真考虑的大多数沉淀工艺，都利用钚的(IV)与(VI)氧化态之间的交替。这类工艺包括：以某种化合物为载体沉淀钚(IV)，然后溶解沉淀，将钚氧化到(VI)态，再沉淀载体化合物，而钚(VI)仍留在溶液中。当钚(IV)沉淀时，不被这些化合物载带的裂变产物留在溶液中。被载带的裂变产物则在钚处于(VI)态时从钚中除去。连续进行氧化-还原循环，直至达到所期望的去污程度。消除裂变产物的过程称为去污，去污程度通过测量材料放射性的变化来检验。

### COMBINATION PROCESSES 组合工艺

8.24. It is possible to combine or couple the various types of process. Some advantages may be gained in this way since one type of process may supplement another. For example, a process which gives good decontamination might be combined advantageously with one which, while inefficient for decontamination, would be very efficient for separation from uranium.

可以把各种类型的工艺组合或结合起来。这样可能获得某些好处，因为一种工艺可以补充另一种工艺。例如，一种去污效果良好的工艺，可以有成效地与一种虽去污效率不高、但分离铀非常有效的工艺相结合。

8.25. At the time when it became necessary to decide on the process to serve as the basis for the design of the Hanford plant (June 1943), the choice, for reasons given above, was limited to precipitation processes and clearly lay between two such processes. However, the process as finally chosen actually represented a combination of the two.

在必须决定以何种工艺作为汉福德工厂设计基础之时（1943年6月），由于上述原因，选择局限于沉淀工艺，并且显然是在两种此类工艺之间。然而，最终选定的工艺实际上是两者的组合。

8.26. The success of the separation process at Hanford has exceeded all expectations. The high yields and decontamination factors and the relative ease of operation have amply demonstrated the wisdom of its choice as a process. This choice was based on a knowledge of plutonium chemistry which had been gleaned from less than a milligram of plutonium. Further developments may make the present Hanford process obsolete, but the principal goal, which was to have a workable and efficient process for use as soon as the Hanford piles were delivering plutonium, has been attained.

汉福德分离工艺的成功超出了所有预期。高收率、高去污因数以及相对容易的操作，充分证明了选择该工艺的明智。这一选择所依据的钚化学知识，是从不到一毫克的钚中获得的。进一步的发展可能使目前的汉福德工艺过时，但主要目标——即在汉福德反应堆开始产出钚时就能立即使用一种可行而高效的工艺——已经达到。

### THE ARGONNE LABORATORY 阿贡实验室

8.27. The Argonne Laboratory was constructed early in 1943 outside Chicago. The site, originally intended for a pilot plant, was later considered to be too near the city and was used for reconstructing the so-called West Stands pile which was originally built on the University of Chicago grounds and which was certainly innocuous. Under the direction of E. Fermi and his colleagues, H. L. Anderson, W. H. Zinn, G. Weil, and others, this pile has served as a prototype unit for studies of thermal stability, controls, instruments, and shielding, and as a neutron source for materials testing and neutron-physics studies. Furthermore, it has proved valuable as a training school for plant operators. More recently a heavy-water pile (see below) has been constructed there.

阿贡实验室于1943年初建于芝加哥郊外。该场地原拟用于中间工厂，后来被认为离城市太近，于是用于重建所谓的“西看台”反应堆；该堆最初建在芝加哥大学校园内，确实是安全无害的。在 E. 费米及其同事 H. L. 安德森、W. H. 津恩、G. 韦尔等人的指导下，该反应堆作为原型装置，用于研究热稳定性、控制、仪器和屏蔽，并作为材料检验和中子物理学研究的中子源。此外，它还被证明是培训工厂操作人员的宝贵培训基地。最近又在那里建造了一座重水反应堆（见下文）。

8.28. The first Argonne pile, a graphite-uranium pile, need not be described in detail. The materials and lattice structure are nearly identical to those which were used for the original West Stands pile. The pile is a cube; it is surrounded by a shield and has controls and safety devices somewhat similar to those used later at Clinton. It has no cooling system and is normally run at a power level of only a few kilowatts. It has occasionally been run at high-power levels for very brief periods. Considering that it is merely a reconstruction of the first chain-reacting unit ever built, it is amazing that it has continued in operation for more than two years without developing any major troubles.

第一座阿贡反应堆是一座石墨-铀反应堆，无需详述。其材料和栅格结构几乎与原“西看台”反应堆所用的相同。该反应堆为一个立方体，周围有屏蔽层，并具有与后来克林顿所用有些相似的控制和安全装置。它没有冷却系统，通常只在几千瓦的功率水平下运行。它偶尔在极短时间内以高功率水平运行。考虑到它只不过是有史以来建造的第一座链式反应装置的重建，它竟能连续运行两年多而未出现任何重大故障，实在令人惊叹。

8.29. One of the most valuable uses of the Argonne pile has been the measurement of neutron-absorption cross sections of a great variety of elements which might be used in piles as structural members, etc., or which might be present in pile materials as impurities. These measurements are made by observing the change in the controls necessary to make k<sub>eff</sub> equal to 1.00 when a known amount of the substance under study is inserted at a definite position in the pile. The results obtained were usually expressed in terms of ”danger coefficients.”

阿贡反应堆最有价值的用途之一，是测量各种元素的中子吸收截面，这些元素可能作为结构部件等用于反应堆，也可能作为杂质存在于反应堆材料中。这些测量是通过观察当把已知量的待研究物质插入反应堆中确定位置时，为使 k<sub>eff</sub> 等于1.00所需的控制变化来进行的。所得结果通常用“危险系数”表示。

8.30. An opening at the top of the pile lets out a very uniform beam of thermal neutrons that can be used for exponential-pile experiments, for direct measurements of absorption cross sections, for Wilson cloud chamber studies, etc.

反应堆顶部的一个开口放出一束非常均匀的热中子束，可用于指数堆实验、吸收截面的直接测量、威尔逊云室研究等。

8.31. An interesting phenomenon occurring at the top of the pile is the production of a beam or flow of ”cold” neutrons. If a sufficient amount of graphite is interposed between the upper surface of the pile and an observation point a few yards above, the neutron energy distribution is found to correspond to a temperature much lower than that of the graphite. This is presumed to be the result of a preferential transmission by the crystalline graphite of the slowest (” coldest”) neutrons, whose quantum-mechanical wave-length is great compared to the distance between successive planes in the graphite crystals.

反应堆顶部出现的一种有趣现象是产生一束或一股“冷”中子流。若在反应堆上表面与其上方几码处的观察点之间插入足够量的石墨，会发现中子能量分布对应于比石墨温度低得多的温度。这被认为是结晶石墨优先透射最慢（“最冷”）中子的结果，这些中子的量子力学波长远大于石墨晶体中相邻晶面之间的距离。

8.32. More recently a pile using heavy water as moderator was constructed in the Argonne Laboratory. The very high intensity beam of neutrons produced by this pile has been found well-suited to the study of ”neutron optics,” e.g., reflection and refraction of neutron beams as by graphite.

最近在阿贡实验室建造了一座以重水为慢化剂的反应堆。已发现该反应堆产生的极高强度中子束非常适合于“中子光学”研究，例如中子束被石墨反射和折射的研究。

8.33. A constant objective of the Argonne Laboratory has been a better understanding of nuclear processes in uranium, neptunium, and plutonium. Repeated experiments have been made to improve the accuracy of constants such as thermal-fission cross sections of U-235, U-238, and Pu-239, probabilities of non-fission neutron absorption by each of these nuclei, and number of neutrons emitted per fission.

阿贡实验室的一个始终如一的目标，是更好地理解铀、镎和钚中的核过程。为改进诸如 U-235、U-238 和 Pu-239 的热裂变截面、各核非裂变中子吸收的概率以及每次裂变发射的中子数等常数的精确度，进行了反复实验。

### THE CLINTON PLANT 克林顿工厂

8.34. In [Chapter VI](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_vi.html) we mentioned plans for a ”pilot” plant for production of plutonium to be built at the Clinton site in Tennessee. By January 1943, the plans for this project were well along; construction was started soon afterward. M. D. Whitaker was appointed director of the Clinton Laboratories. The pilot-plant plans were made cooperatively by du Pont and the Metallurgical Laboratory; construction was carried out by du Pont; plant operation was maintained by the University of Chicago as part of the Metallurgical Project.

在[第六章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_vi.html)中，我们提到计划在田纳西州克林顿场地建造一座生产钚的“中间”工厂。到1943年1月，该项目的计划已相当进展，此后不久便开始施工。M. D. 惠特克被任命为克林顿实验室主任。中间工厂计划由杜邦公司和冶金实验室合作制定；施工由杜邦公司执行；工厂运行由芝加哥大学作为冶金项目的一部分予以维持。

8.35. The main purposes of the Clinton plant were to produce some plutonium and to serve as a pilot plant for chemical separation. As regards research, the emphasis at Clinton was on chemistry and on the biological effects of radiations. A large laboratory was provided for chemical analysis, for research on purification methods, for fission-product studies, for development of intermediate-scale extraction and decontamination processes, etc. Later a ”hot laboratory,” i.e., a laboratory for remotely-controlled work on highly radioactive material, was provided. There is also an instrument shop and laboratory that has been used very actively. There are facilities for both clinical and experimental work of the health division, which has been very active. There is a small physics laboratory in which some important work was done using higher neutron intensities than were available at the Argonne Laboratory. The principal installations constructed at the Clinton Laboratory site were the pile and the separation plant; these are briefly described below.

克林顿工厂的主要目的是生产一些钚，并作为化学分离的中间工厂。就研究而言，克林顿的重点是化学和辐射的生物效应。那里设有一个大型实验室，用于化学分析、提纯方法研究、裂变产物研究、中等规模萃取和去污工艺的开发等。后来又设立了一个“热实验室”，即用于对高放射性材料进行遥控操作的实验室。还有一个一直使用得很活跃的仪器车间和实验室。卫生部门拥有临床和实验工作的设施，该部门一直非常活跃。还有一个小型物理实验室，其中利用比阿贡实验室可获得的更高的中子强度完成了一些重要工作。在克林顿实验室场地建造的主要设施是反应堆和分离工厂；下面简要描述这些设施。

#### THE CLINTON PILE 克林顿反应堆

8.36. In any steadily operating pile the effective multiplication factor k must be kept at 1, whatever the power level. The best k<sub>∞</sub> that had been observed in a uranium-graphite lattice could not be achieved in a practical pile because of neutron leakage, cooling system, cylindrical channels for the uranium, protective coating on the uranium, and other minor factors. Granted air-cooling and a maximum safe temperature for the surface of the uranium, a size of pile had to be chosen that could produce, 1,000 kw. The effective k would go down with rising temperature but not sufficiently to be a determining factor. Though a sphere was the ideal shape, practical considerations recommended a rectangular block.

在任何稳定运行的反应堆中，无论功率水平如何，有效倍增因子 k 都必须保持在1。由于中子泄漏、冷却系统、铀的圆柱形管道、铀上的保护涂层以及其他次要因素，在铀-石墨栅格中所观察到的最佳 k<sub>∞</sub> 无法在实际反应堆中实现。在采用空气冷却并给定铀表面最高安全温度的条件下，必须选择能够产生1,000千瓦的反应堆尺寸。有效 k 会随温度升高而下降，但下降幅度不足以成为决定性因素。虽然球形是理想形状，但实际考虑倾向于采用矩形块体。

8.37. The Clinton pile consists of a cube of graphite containing horizontal channels filled with uranium. The uranium is in the form of metal cylinders protected by gas-tight casings of aluminum. The uranium cylinders or slugs may be slid into the channels in the graphite; space is left to permit cooling air to flow past, and to permit pushing the slugs out at the back of the pile when they are ready for processing. Besides the channels for slugs there are various other holes through the pile for control rods, instruments, etc.

克林顿反应堆由一块石墨立方体构成，其中含有充满铀的水平管道。铀呈金属圆柱体形式，由气密的铝套管保护。铀圆柱体或铀棒可以滑入石墨中的管道；留出空间以便冷却空气流过，并便于在铀棒可以处理时将它们从反应堆后部推出。除铀棒管道外，反应堆中还有各种其他孔道，用于控制棒、仪器等。

8.38. The Clinton pile was considerably larger than the first pile at Chicago (see [Chapter VI](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_vi.html)). More important than the increased size of the Clinton pile were its cooling system, heavier shields, and means for changing the slugs. The production goal of the Clinton plant was set at a figure which meant that the pile should operate at a power level of 1,000 kw.

克林顿反应堆比芝加哥的第一座反应堆大得多（见[第六章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_vi.html)）。比克林顿反应堆尺寸增大更为重要的，是它的冷却系统、更重的屏蔽层以及更换铀棒的装置。克林顿工厂的生产目标定为意味着反应堆应在1,000千瓦功率水平下运行的水平。

8.39. The instrumentation and controls are identical in principle to those of the first pile. Neutron intensity in the pile is measured by a BF<sub>3</sub> ionization chamber and is controlled by boron steel rods that can be moved in and out of the pile, thereby varying the fraction of neutrons available to produce fission.

仪器和控制装置在原理上与第一座反应堆的相同。反应堆中的中子强度由 BF<sub>3</sub> 电离室测量，并由可移入和移出反应堆的硼钢棒控制，从而改变可用于产生裂变的中子份额。

8.40. In spite of an impressive array of instruments and safety devices, the most striking feature of the pile is the simplicity of operation. Most of the time the operators have nothing to do except record the readings of various instruments.

尽管有令人印象深刻的一系列仪器和安全装置，该反应堆最引人注目的特点却是操作简便。大多数时间，操作人员除了记录各种仪器的读数之外无事可做。

### THE SEPARATION PLANT 分离工厂

8.41. Here, as at Hanford, the plutonium processes have to be carried out by remote control and behind thick shields. The separation equipment is housed in a series of adjacent cells having heavy concrete walls. These cells form a continuous structure (canyon) which is about 100 feet long and is two-thirds buried in the ground. Adjacent to this canyon are the control rooms, analytical laboratories, and a laboratory for further purification of the plutonium after it has been decontaminated to the point of comparative safety.

这里与汉福德一样，钚工艺必须在厚屏蔽层后通过遥控进行。分离设备安置在一系列相邻的、具有厚重混凝土墙的小室中。这些小室构成一个连续结构（通道），长约100英尺，三分之二埋在地下。与这一通道相邻的是控制室、分析实验室，以及一个供钚在去污到相对安全的程度后进一步提纯的实验室。

8.42. Uranium slugs that have been exposed in the pile are transferred under water to the first of these cells and are then dissolved. Subsequent operations are performed by pumping solutions or slurries from one tank or centrifuge to another.

已在反应堆中照射过的铀棒在水下转移到这些小室中的第一个，然后被溶解。随后的操作通过将溶液或浆料从一个槽或离心机泵送到另一个槽或离心机来进行。

### PERFORMANCE OF CLINTON PILE 克林顿反应堆的性能

8.43. The Clinton pile started operating on November 4, 1943, and within a few days was brought up to a power level of 500 kw at a maximum slug surface temperature of 110<sup>o</sup> C. Improvements in the air circulation and an elevation of the maximum uranium surface temperature to 150<sup>o</sup> C. brought the power level up to about 800 kw, where it was maintained until the spring of 1944. Starting at that time, a change was made in the distribution of uranium, the change being designed to level out the power distribution in the pile by reducing the amount of metal near the center relative to that further out and thereby to increase the average power level without anywhere attaining too high a temperature. At the same time improvements were realized in the sealing of the slug jackets, making it possible to operate the pile at higher temperature. As a result, a power level of 1,800 kw was attained in May 1944; this was further increased after the installation of better fans in June 1944.

克林顿反应堆于1943年11月4日开始运行，几天之内就升到500千瓦的功率水平，铀棒表面最高温度为110<sup>o</sup> C。空气循环的改进以及铀表面最高温度提高到150<sup>o</sup> C，使功率水平提高到约800千瓦，并一直维持到1944年春季。从那时起，对铀的分布作了改变，这一改变旨在通过减少中心附近的金属量相对于外围金属量来使反应堆中的功率分布趋于平坦，从而在不超过任何地方过高温度的情况下提高平均功率水平。同时，铀棒包壳的密封得到改进，使反应堆能够在更高温度下运行。结果，1944年5月达到了1,800千瓦的功率水平；1944年6月安装更好的风扇后，这一水平进一步提高。

8.44. Thus the pile performance of June 1944 considerably exceeded expectations. In ease of control, steadiness of operation, and absence of dangerous radiation, the pile has been most satisfactory. There have been very few failures attributable to mistakes in design or construction.

因此，1944年6月的反应堆性能大大超出了预期。在控制容易、运行稳定以及没有危险辐射方面，该反应堆一直非常令人满意。归因于设计或施工错误的故障极少。

8.45. The pile itself was simple both in principle and in practice. Not so the plutonium-separation plant. The step from the first chain-reacting pile to the Clinton pile was reasonably predictable; but a much greater and more uncertain step was required in the case of the separation process, for the Clinton separation plant was designed on the basis of experiments using only microgram amounts of plutonium.

反应堆本身在原理和实践上都很简单。钚分离工厂则不然。从第一座链式反应堆到克林顿反应堆的跨越是相当可预测的；但在分离工艺方面，所需的跨越要大得多，也更具不确定性，因为克林顿分离工厂是依据仅使用微克量钚的实验设计的。

8.46. Nevertheless, the separation process worked! The first batch of slugs from the pile entered the separation plant on December 20, 1943. By the end of January 1944, metal from the pile was going to the separation plant at the rate of 1/3 ton per day. By February 1, 1944, 190 mg of plutonium had been delivered and by March 1, 1944, several grams had been delivered. Furthermore, the efficiency of recovery at the very start was about 50 per cent, and by June 1944 it was between 80 and 90 per cent.

尽管如此，分离工艺还是奏效了！第一批来自反应堆的铀棒于1943年12月20日进入分离工厂。到1944年1月底，来自反应堆的金属以每天三分之一吨的速率送往分离工厂。到1944年2月1日，已交付190毫克钚；到1944年3月1日，已交付数克。此外，一开始的回收率约为50%，到1944年6月已在80%至90%之间。

8.47. During this whole period there was a large group of chemists at Clinton working on improving the process and developing it for Hanford. The Hanford problem differed from that at Clinton in that much higher concentrations of plutonium were expected. Furthermore, though the chemists were to be congratulated on the success of the Clinton plant, the process was complicated and expensive. Any improvements in yield or decontamination or in general simplification were very much to be sought.

在整个这一时期，克林顿有一大批化学家致力于改进工艺并将其开发用于汉福德。汉福德的问题与克林顿不同，因为预期钚的浓度要高得多。此外，尽管化学家们因克林顿工厂的成功而值得祝贺，但该工艺复杂且昂贵。任何在收率或去污方面的改进，或在总体简化方面的改进，都是非常值得追求的。

8.48. Besides the proving of the pile and the separation plant and the production of several grams of plutonium for experimental use at Chicago, Clinton, and elsewhere, the Clinton Laboratories have been invaluable as a training and testing center for Hanford, for medical experiments, pile studies, purification studies, and physical and chemical studies of plutonium and fission products.

除了验证反应堆和分离工厂，以及生产数克钚供芝加哥、克林顿和其他地方实验使用之外，克林顿实验室作为汉福德的培训和试验中心，在医学实验、反应堆研究、提纯研究以及钚和裂变产物的物理和化学研究方面，一直具有极高的价值。

8.49. As typical of the kind of problems tackled there and at Chicago, the following problems-listed in a single routine report for May 1944-are pertinent:

作为那里和芝加哥所处理的那类问题的典型例子，以下问题——列于1944年5月的一份例行报告中——是相关的：

*Problems Closed Out during May 1944*: Search for New Oxidizing Agent, Effect of Radiation on Water and Aqueous Solutions, Solubility of Plutonium Peroxide, Plutonium Compounds Suitable for Shipment, Fission Product Distribution in Plant Process Solutions, Preliminary Process Design for Adsorption Extraction, Adsorption Semi-Works Assistance, Completion of Adsorption Process Design.

*1944年5月期间结清的问题*：寻找新的氧化剂、辐射对水和水溶液的影响、过氧化钚的溶解度、适合运输的钚化合物、工厂工艺溶液中的裂变产物分布、吸附萃取的初步工艺设计、吸附中间工厂协助、吸附工艺设计的完成。

*New Problems Assigned during May 1944*: New Product Analysis Method, Effect of Radiation on Graphite, Improvement in Yield, New Pile Explorations, Waste Uranium Recovery, Monitoring Stack Gases, Disposal of Active Waste Solutions, Spray Cooling of X Pile, Assay Training Program, Standardization of Assay Methods, Development of Assay Methods, Shielded Apparatus for Process Control Assays, Cloud Chamber Experiment, Alpha Particles from U-235, Radial Product Distribution, Diffraction of Neutrons.

*1944年5月期间分配的新问题*：新产品分析方法、辐射对石墨的影响、收率改进、新的反应堆探索、废铀回收、烟囱气体监测、放射性废液处置、X 堆喷淋冷却、分析培训计划、分析方法标准化、分析方法开发、用于工艺控制分析的屏蔽装置、云室实验、来自 U-235 的α粒子、径向产物分布、中子衍射。

### THE HANFORD PLANT 汉福德工厂

8.50. It is beyond the scope of this report to give any account of the construction of the Hanford Engineer Works, but it is to be hoped that the full story of this extraordinary enterprise and the companion one, the Clinton Engineer Works, will be published at some time in the future. The Hanford site was examined by representatives of General Groves and of du Pont at the end of 1942, and use of the site was approved by General Groves after he had inspected it personally. It was on the west side of the Columbia River in central Washington north of Pasco. In the early months of 1943 a 200-square-mile tract in this region was acquired by the government (by lease or purchase) through the Real Estate Division of the Office of the Chief of Engineers. Eventually an area of nearly a thousand square miles was brought under government control. At the time of acquisition of the land there were a few farms and two small villages, Hanford and Richland, on the site, which was otherwise sage-brush plains and barren hills. On the 6th of April, 1943, ground was broken for the Hanford construction camp. At the peak of activity in 1944, this camp was a city of 60,000 inhabitants, the fourth largest city in the state. Now, however, the camp is practically deserted as the operating crew is housed at Richland.

本报告的范围不包括对汉福德工程厂建设的任何叙述，但希望这一非凡事业及其伙伴克林顿工程厂的完整故事能在将来某个时候公之于众。1942年底，格罗夫斯将军和杜邦公司的代表考察了汉福德场地，格罗夫斯将军亲自视察后批准使用该场地。它位于华盛顿州中部帕斯科以北的哥伦比亚河西岸。1943年头几个月，政府通过总工程师办公室房地产司（以租赁或购买方式）获得了该地区一块200平方英里的土地。最终，近一千平方英里的区域被置于政府控制之下。获得土地时，场地上有几处农场和两个小村庄，即汉福德和里奇兰，其余地方则是山艾平原和荒芜的丘陵。1943年4月6日，汉福德建设营地破土动工。1944年活动高峰期，该营地是一座拥有60,000居民的城市，为该州第四大城市。然而现在，由于运行人员居住在里奇兰，营地实际上已空无一人。

8.51. Work was begun on the first of the Hanford production piles on June 7, 1943, and operation of the first pile began in September 1944. The site was originally laid out for five piles, but the construction of only three has been undertaken. Besides the piles, there are, of course, plutonium separation plants, pumping stations and water-treatment plants. There is also a low-power chain-reacting pile for material testing. Not only are the piles themselves widely spaced for safety several miles apart -but the separation plants are well away from the piles and from each other. All three piles were in operation by the summer of 1945.

第一座汉福德生产反应堆于1943年6月7日开工，第一座反应堆于1944年9月开始运行。场地最初规划为五座反应堆，但只着手建造了三座。除反应堆外，当然还有钚分离工厂、泵站和水处理厂。还有一座用于材料检验的低功率链式反应堆。反应堆本身不仅为了安全而相隔数英里、间距很大，而且分离工厂也远离反应堆并彼此远离。到1945年夏天，三座反应堆都已投入运行。

### CANNING AND CORROSION 封装与腐蚀

8.52. No one who lived through the period of design and construction of the Hanford plant is likely to forget the ”canning” problem, i.e., the problem of sealing the uranium slugs in protective metal jackets. On periodic visits to Chicago the writer could roughly estimate the state of the canning problem by the atmosphere of gloom or joy to be found around the laboratory. It was definitely not a simple matter to find a sheath that would protect uranium from water corrosion, would keep fission products out of the water, would transmit heat from the uranium to the water, and would not absorb too many neutrons. Yet the failure of a single can might conceivably require shut-down of an entire operating pile.

经历过汉福德工厂设计和建设时期的人，都不太可能忘记“封装”问题，即把铀棒密封在保护性金属套管中的问题。在定期访问芝加哥时，笔者可以通过实验室周围忧郁或欢欣的气氛大致判断封装问题的状况。要找到一种既能保护铀免受水腐蚀、又能把裂变产物挡在水外、还能把热量从铀传到水中、并且不会吸收过多中子的护套，绝非易事。然而，单个套管失效就可能需要使整座运行中的反应堆停堆。

8.53. Attempts to meet the stringent requirements involved experimental work on electroplating processes, hot-dipping processes, cementation-coating processes, corrosion-resistant alloys of uranium, and mechanical jacketing or canning processes. Mechanical jackets or cans of thin aluminum were feasible from the nuclear-physics point of view and were chosen early as the most likely solution of the problem. But the problem of getting a uniform, heat-conducting bond between the uranium and the surrounding aluminum, and the problem of effecting a gas-tight closure for the can both proved very troublesome. Development of alternative methods had to be carried along up to the last minute, and even up to a few weeks before it was time to load the uranium slugs into the pile there was no certainty that any of the processes under development would be satisfactory. A final minor but apparently important modification in the preferred canning process was adopted in October 1944, after the first pile had begun experimental operation. By the summer of 1945, there had been no can failure reported.

为满足这些严格要求所做的尝试，涉及电镀工艺、热浸镀工艺、渗镀涂层工艺、抗腐蚀铀合金以及机械套装或封装工艺的实验工作。从核物理学的角度看，薄铝机械护套或套管是可行的，并且很早就被选为这一问题最可能的解决办法。但要在铀与周围铝之间获得均匀导热的结合，以及实现套管气密封闭，这两个问题都被证明非常棘手。替代方法的开发不得不一直进行到最后一刻，甚至在准备把铀棒装入反应堆之前几周，仍不能确定所开发的任何一种工艺是否会令人满意。1944年10月，在第一座反应堆已开始实验运行之后，对首选封装工艺作出了最后一项次要但显然重要的修改。到1945年夏天，未报告有套管失效。

### PRESENT STATUS OF THE HANFORD PLANTS 汉福德各工厂的现状

8.54. During the fall of 1944 and the early months of 1945 the second and third Hanford piles were finished and put into operation, as were the additional chemical separation plants. There were, of course, some difficulties; however, none of the fears expressed as to canning failure, film formation in the water tubes, or radiation effects in the chemical processes, have turned out to be justified. As of early summer 1945 the piles are operating at designed power, producing plutonium, and heating the Columbia River\*. The chemical plants are separating the plutonium from the uranium and from the fission products with better efficiency than had been anticipated. The finished product is being delivered. How it can be used is the subject of [Chapter XII](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_xii-a.html).

1944年秋和1945年头几个月，第二座和第三座汉福德反应堆建成并投入运行，增设的化学分离工厂也是如此。当然，也有一些困难；然而，所表达的关于套管失效、水管中形成薄膜或化学过程中辐射效应的担忧，都没有被证明是有根据的。截至1945年初夏，各反应堆正以设计功率运行，生产钚，并加热哥伦比亚河\*。化学工厂正以高于预期的效率将钚从铀和裂变产物中分离出来。成品正在交付。如何使用它，是[第十二章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_xii-a.html)的主题。

\* The actual rise in temperature is so tiny that no effect on fish life could be expected. To make doubly sure, this expectation was confirmed by an elaborate series of experiments.

\* 实际的温升极小，因此可以预期不会对鱼类生活产生影响。为加倍稳妥，这一预期已通过一系列精心设计的实验得到证实。

### THE WORK ON HEAVY WATER 重水方面的工作

8.55. In previous chapters there have been references to the advantages of heavy water as a moderator. It is more effective than graphite in slowing down neutrons and it has a smaller neutron absorption than graphite. It is therefore possible to build a chain-reacting unit with uranium and heavy water and thereby to attain a considerably higher multiplication factor, k, and a smaller size than is possible with graphite. But one must have the heavy water.

在前几章中已提到重水作为慢化剂的优点。它在使中子减速方面比石墨更有效，而且其中子吸收比石墨小。因此，可以用铀和重水建造一座链式反应装置，从而获得比石墨可能达到的更高的倍增因子 k 和更小的尺寸。但必须拥有重水。

8.56. In the spring of 1943 the Metallurgical Laboratory decided to increase the emphasis on experiments and calculations aimed at a heavy-water pile. To this end a committee was set up under E. Wigner, a group under H. C. Vernon was transferred from Columbia to Chicago, and H. D. Smyth, who had just become associate director of the Laboratory, was asked to take general charge.

1943年春，冶金实验室决定加强针对重水反应堆的实验和计算。为此，在 E. 维格纳领导下成立了一个委员会，H. C. 弗农领导的一个小组从哥伦比亚大学转到芝加哥，并请刚刚成为该实验室副主任的 H. D. 史密斯负责全面领导。

8.57. The first function of this group was to consider in what way heavy water could best be used to insure the overall success of the Metallurgical Project, taking account of the limited production schedule for heavy water that had been already authorized.

该小组的第一项职能是考虑如何最好地利用重水来确保冶金项目的整体成功，同时考虑到已经批准的重水生产计划有限。

8.58. It became apparent that the production schedule was so low that it would take two years to produce enough heavy water to ”moderate” a fair-sized pile for plutol1ium production. On the other hand, there might be enough heavy water to moderate a small ”laboratory” pile, which could furnish information that might be valuable. In any event, during the summer of 1943 so great were the uncertainties as to the length of the war and as to the success of the other parts of the DSM project that a complete study of the possibilities of heavy-water piles seemed desirable. Either the heavy-water production schedule might be stepped up or the smaller, experimental pile might be built. An intensive study of the matter was made during the summer of 1943 but in November it was decided to curtail the program and construction was limited to a 250-kw pile located at the Argonne site.

情况变得明显：生产计划如此之低，以致需要两年时间才能生产出足够“慢化”一座中等规模钚生产反应堆的重水。另一方面，重水或许足以慢化一座小型“实验室”反应堆，而后者可能提供有价值的信息。无论如何，1943年夏天，由于对战争持续时间以及 DSM 项目其他部分能否成功存在极大的不确定性，似乎有必要对重水反应堆的各种可能性进行全面研究。或者提高重水生产计划，或者建造较小的实验反应堆。1943年夏天对这一问题进行了深入研究，但11月决定缩减该计划，建设仅限于在阿贡场地建造一座250千瓦的反应堆。

### THE ARGONNE HEAVY-WATER PILE 阿贡重水反应堆

8.59. Perhaps the most striking aspect of the uranium and heavy-water pile at the Argonne is its small size. Even with its surrounding shield of concrete it is relatively small compared to the uranium-graphite piles.

阿贡的铀-重水反应堆也许最引人注目的方面是它的尺寸之小。即使连同周围混凝土屏蔽层，与铀-石墨反应堆相比它也相对较小。

8.60. By May 15, 1944, the Argonne uranium and heavy-water pile was ready for test. With the uranium slugs in place, it was found that the chain reaction in the pile became self sustaining when only three fifths of the heavy water had been added. The reactivity of the pile was so far above expectations that it would have been beyond the capacity of the control rods to handle if the remainder of the heavy water had been added. To meet this unusual and pleasant situation some of the uranium was removed and extra control rods were added.

到1944年5月15日，阿贡铀-重水反应堆已准备好进行试验。在铀棒就位的情况下，发现当只加入五分之三的重水时，反应堆中的链式反应便达到自持。反应堆的反应性远超预期，以至于如果加入其余重水，将超出控制棒的处理能力。为应对这一不寻常而可喜的情况，移除了部分铀并增设了控制棒。

8.61. With these modifications it was possible to fill the tank to the level planned. By July 4, 1944, W. H. Zinn reported that the pile was running satisfactorily at 190 kw, and by August 8, 1944, he reported that it was operating at 300 kw.

经过这些修改，可以将水箱注满到计划水平。到1944年7月4日，W. H. 津恩报告该反应堆在190千瓦下运行令人满意；到1944年8月8日，他报告其运行功率为300千瓦。

8.62. In general the characteristics of this pile differed slightly from those of comparable graphite piles. This pile takes several hours to reach equilibrium. It shows small (less than 1 per cent) but sudden fluctuations in power level,. probably caused by bubbles in the water. It cannot be shut down as completely or as rapidly as the graphite pile because of the tendency of delayed gamma rays to produce (from the heavy water) additional neutrons. As anticipated, the neutron density at the center is high. The shields, controls, heat exchanger, etc., have operated satisfactorily.

总体而言，该反应堆的特性与相应的石墨反应堆略有不同。该反应堆需要数小时才能达到平衡。它显示出功率水平的小幅（小于1%）但突然的波动，可能是由水中的气泡引起的。它不能像石墨反应堆那样完全或迅速地停堆，因为延迟伽马射线倾向于（从重水中）产生额外中子。正如预期，中心处的中子密度很高。屏蔽层、控制装置、热交换器等均运行良好。

### THE HEALTH DIVISION 卫生部门

8.63. The major objective of the health group was in a sense a negative one, to insure that no one concerned suffered serious injury from the peculiar hazards of the enterprise. Medical case histories of persons suffering serious injury or death resulting from radiation were emphatically not wanted. The success of the health division in meeting these problems was remarkable. Even in the research group where control is most difficult, cases showing even temporary bad effects were extremely rare. Factors of safety used in plant design and operation are so great that the hazards of the home and the family car are far greater for the personnel than any arising from the plants.

卫生部门的主要目标在某种意义上是一个消极目标，即确保有关人员都不会因该事业的独特危害而遭受严重伤害。人们强烈不希望出现因辐射而遭受严重伤害或死亡的个人的医学病例史。卫生部门在应对这些问题方面的成功是显著的。即使在控制最为困难的研究小组中，显示出哪怕暂时不良影响的病例也极为罕见。工厂设计和运行中所采用的安全系数如此之大，以致对人员而言，家庭和家用汽车的危险远大于工厂所产生的任何危险。

8.64. To achieve its objective the health group worked along three major lines:

为实现其目标，卫生部门沿着三条主要路线开展工作：

(1) Adoption of pre-employment physical examinations and frequent re-examinations, particularly of those exposed to radiation.

(1) 实行就业前体格检查和频繁的复查，尤其是对接触辐射的人员。

(2) Setting of tolerance standards for radiation doses and development of instruments measuring exposure of personnel; giving advice on shielding, etc.; continually measuring radiation intensities at various locations in the plants; measuring contamination of clothes, laboratory desks, waste water, the atmosphere, etc.

(2) 制定辐射剂量的容许标准，开发测量人员受照程度的仪器；就屏蔽等提供建议；持续测量工厂各处的辐射强度；测量衣物、实验台、废水、大气等的污染。

(3) Carrying out research on the effects of direct exposure of persons and animals to various types of radiation, and on the effects of ingestion and inhalation of the various radioactive or toxic materials such as fission products, plutonium and uranium.

(3) 开展关于人员及动物直接暴露于各类辐射之效应的研究，以及关于摄入和吸入各种放射性或有毒物质（如裂变产物、钚和铀）之效应的研究。

### ROUTINE EXAMINATIONS 常规检查

8.65. The white blood-corpuscle count was used as the principal criterion as to whether a person suffered from overexposure to radiation. A number of cases of abnormally low counts were observed and correlated with the degree of overexposure. Individuals appreciably affected were shifted to other jobs or given brief vacations; none has shown permanent ill effects.

白细胞计数被用作判断一个人是否受到过度辐射照射的主要标准。观察到若干白细胞计数异常低的病例，并将其与过度照射的程度相关联。受到明显影响的人员被调往其他工作或给予短期休假；没有人显示出永久性不良影响。

8.66. At the same time it was recognized that the white blood-corpuscle count is not an entirely reliable criterion. Some work on animals indicated that serious damage might occur before the blood count gave any indication of danger. Accordingly, more elaborate blood tests were made on selected individuals and on experimental animals in the hope of finding a test that would give an earlier warning of impending injury.

同时，人们认识到白细胞计数并非完全可靠的标准。一些动物实验表明，在血计数给出任何危险迹象之前，可能已发生严重损害。因此，对选定的人员和实验动物进行了更精细的血液检验，希望找到一种能更早预警即将发生的损伤的检验方法。

### INSTRUMENTS FOR RADIATION MEASUREMENTS 辐射测量仪器

8.67. The Health Division had principal responsibility for the development of pocket meters for indicating the extent of exposure of persons. The first of these instruments was a simple electroscope about the size and shape of a fountain pen. Such instruments were electrostatically charged at the start of each day and were read at the end of the day. The degree to which they became discharged indicated the total amount of ionizing radiation to which they had been exposed. Unfortunately they were none too rugged and reliable, but the error of reading was nearly always in the right direction-i.e., in the direction of overstating the exposure. At an early date the practice was established of issuing two of these pocket meters to everyone entering a dangerous area. A record was kept of the readings at the time of issuance and also when the meters were turned in. The meters themselves were continually although gradually improved. The Health Division later introduced ”film badges,” small pieces of film worn in the identification badge, the films being periodically developed and examined for radiation blackening. These instruments for individuals such as the pocket meter and film badge were extra and probably unnecessary precautions. In permanent installations the shielding alone normally affords complete safety. Its effect is under frequent survey by either permanently installed or portable instruments.

卫生部门主要负责开发用于指示人员受照程度的袖珍计量仪。其中最早的仪器是一个大小和形状像自来水笔的简易验电器。这种仪器每天开始时刻被静电充电，并在一天结束时读数。它们放电的程度指示其所受电离辐射的总量。遗憾的是，它们不够坚固可靠，但读数误差几乎总是偏向正确的方向——即夸大受照程度的方向。早在早期就确立了这样的做法：向每个进入危险区域的人发放两个这种袖珍计量仪。发放时和交回计量仪时都记录读数。计量仪本身不断得到改进，尽管是逐渐的。卫生部门后来引入了“胶片佩章”，即佩戴在身份徽章中的小片胶片，胶片定期冲洗并检查辐射致黑程度。诸如袖珍计量仪和胶片佩章这类个人仪器属于额外且可能不必要的预防措施。在永久性装置中，仅屏蔽层通常就能提供完全的安全。其效果由固定安装或便携式仪器经常进行检查。

8.68. The Health Division cooperated with the Physics Division in the development and use of various other instruments. There was ”Sneezy” for measuring the concentration of radioactive dust in the air and ”Pluto” for measuring a-emitting contamination (usually plutonium) of laboratory desks and equipment. Counters were used to check the contamination of laboratory coats before and after the coats were laundered. At the exit gates of certain laboratories concealed counters sounded an alarm when someone passed whose clothing, skin or hair was contaminated. In addition, routine inspections of laboratory areas were made.

卫生部门与物理部门合作开发和使用各种其他仪器。有用于测量空气中放射性尘埃浓度的“Sneezy”，以及用于测量实验台和设备α发射污染（通常为钚）的“Pluto”。计数器用于检查实验室工作服在洗涤前后的污染情况。在某些实验室的出口大门处，隐蔽的计数器在衣物、皮肤或头发受到污染的人经过时发出警报。此外，还对实验室区域进行例行检查。

8.69. One of the studies made involved meteorology. It became essential to know whether the stack gases (at Clinton and at Hanford) would be likely to spread radioactive fission products in dangerous concentrations. Since the behavior of these gases is very dependent on the weather, studies were made at both sites over a period of many months, and satisfactory stack operation was specified.

所进行的研究之一涉及气象学。必须了解烟囱气体（在克林顿和汉福德）是否可能以危险浓度扩散放射性裂变产物。由于这些气体的行为非常依赖于天气，在两个场地都进行了历时数月的研究，并规定了令人满意的烟囱运行方式。

### RESEARCH 研究

8.70. Since both the scale and the variety of the radiation hazards in this enterprise were unprecedented, all reasonable precautions were taken; but no sure means were at hand for determining the adequacy of the precautions. It was essential to supplement previous knowledge as completely as possible. For this purpose, an extensive program of animal experimentation was carried out along three main lines: (1) exposure to neutron, alpha, beta and gamma radiation; (2) ingestion of uranium, plutonium and fission products; (3) inhalation of uranium, plutonium and fission products. Under the general direction of Dr. Stone these experiments were carried out at Chicago, Clinton and the University of California principally by Dr. Cole and Dr. Hamilton. Extensive and valuable results were obtained.

由于这项事业中辐射危害的规模和多样性都是前所未有的，因此采取了一切合理的预防措施；但当时没有可靠手段来判断这些预防措施是否充分。必须尽可能完整地补充原有知识。为此，沿三条主要路线开展了广泛的动物实验计划：(1) 暴露于中子、α、β和伽马辐射；(2) 摄入铀、钚和裂变产物；(3) 吸入铀、钚和裂变产物。在斯通博士的总体指导下，这些实验主要由科尔博士和汉密尔顿博士在芝加哥、克林顿和加利福尼亚大学进行。获得了广泛而有价值的结果。

### SUMMARY 小结

8.71. Both space and security restrictions prevent a detailed report on the work of the laboratories and plants concerned with plutonium production.

篇幅和安全限制都使得无法详细报告与钚生产有关的实验室和工厂的工作。

8.72. Two types of neutron absorption are fundamental to the operation of the plant: one, neutron absorption in U-235 resulting in fission, maintains the chain reaction as a source of neutrons; the other, neutron absorption in U-238 leads to the formation of plutonium, the desired product.

两类中子吸收对工厂的运行至关重要：一类是在 U-235 中被吸收导致裂变，作为中子源维持链式反应；另一类是在 U-238 中被吸收导致生成所需产物钚。

8.73. The course of a nuclear chain reaction in a graphite- moderated heterogeneous pile can be described by following a single generation of neutrons. The original fast neutrons are slightly increased in number by fast fission, reduced by resonance absorption in U-238 and further reduced by absorption at thermal energies in graphite and other materials and by escape; the remaining neutrons, which have been slowed in the graphite, cause fission in U-235, producing a new generation of fast neutrons similar to the previous generation.

石墨慢化非均匀反应堆中核链式反应的过程，可以通过追踪单代中子来描述。最初快中子的数量因快裂变而略有增加，因 U-238 中的共振吸收而减少，又因在石墨和其他材料中于热能处被吸收以及逃逸而进一步减少；剩余的中子已在石墨中减速，它们在 U-235 中引起裂变，产生与上一代类似的新一代快中子。

8.74. The product, plutonium, must be separated by chemical processes from a comparable quantity of fission products and a much larger quantity of uranium. Of several possible separation processes the one chosen consists of a series of reactions including precipitating with carriers, dissolving, oxidizing and reducing.

产物钚必须通过化学过程与数量相当的裂变产物以及数量大得多的铀分离。在若干可能的分离工艺中，所选定的工艺由一系列反应组成，包括用载体沉淀、溶解、氧化和还原。

8.75. The chain reaction was studied at low power at the Argonne Laboratory beginning early in 1943. Both chain reaction and chemical separation processes were investigated at the Clinton Laboratories beginning in November 1943, and an appreciable amount of plutonium was produced there.

从1943年初开始，在阿贡实验室以低功率研究了链式反应。从1943年11月起，在克林顿实验室研究了链式反应和化学分离工艺，并在那里生产了可观数量的钚。

8.76. Construction of the main production plant at Hanford, Washington, was begun in 1943 and the first large pile went into operation in September 1944. The entire plant was in operation by the summer of 1945 with all chain-reacting piles and chemical-separation plants performing better than had been anticipated.

华盛顿州汉福德的主要生产工厂于1943年开始建设，第一座大型反应堆于1944年9月投入运行。到1945年夏天，整个工厂投入运行，所有链式反应堆和化学分离工厂的性能都优于预期。

8.77. Extensive studies were made on the use of heavy water as a moderator and an experimental pile containing heavy water was built at the Argonne Laboratory. Plans for a production plant using heavy water were given up.

对重水作为慢化剂的使用进行了广泛研究，并在阿贡实验室建造了一座含重水的实验反应堆。使用重水的生产工厂计划被放弃。

8.78. The Health Division was active along three main lines: (1) medical examination of personnel; (2) advice on radiation hazards and constant check on working conditions; (3) research on the effects of radiation. The careful planning and exhaustive research work of this division have resulted in an outstanding health record at Hanford and elsewhere in the project.

卫生部门沿着三条主要路线积极开展工作：(1) 对人员进行医学检查；(2) 就辐射危害提供建议并不断检查工作条件；(3) 研究辐射的效应。该部门周密的规划和详尽的研究工作，使汉福德及项目其他地方取得了出色的健康记录。
