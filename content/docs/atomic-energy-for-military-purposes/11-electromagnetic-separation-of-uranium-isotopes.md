### INTRODUCTION 引言

11.1. In [Chapter IV](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_iv.html) we said that the possibility of large-scale separation of the uranium isotopes by electromagnetic means was suggested in the fall of 1941 by E. O. Lawrence of the University of California and H. D. Smyth of Princeton University. In [Chapter IX](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_ix-a.html) we described the principles of one method of electromagnetic separation and listed the three limitations of that method: difficulty of producing ions, limited fraction of ions actually used, and space charge effects.
在[第四章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_iv.html)中我们说过，1941年秋，加利福尼亚大学的E. O.劳伦斯（E. O. Lawrence）和普林斯顿大学的H. D.斯迈思（H. D. Smyth）提出了以电磁方法大规模分离铀同位素的可能性。在[第九章](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_ix-a.html)中，我们描述了电磁分离一种方法的原理，并列出了该方法的三个局限：难以产生离子、实际利用的离子比例有限，以及空间电荷效应。

11.2. By the end of December 1941, when the reorganization of the whole uranium project was effected, Lawrence had already obtained some samples of separated isotopes of uranium and in the reorganization he was officially placed in charge of the preparation of further samples and the making of various associated physical measurements. However, just as the Metallurgical Laboratory very soon shifted its objective from the physics of the chain reaction to the large-scale production of plutonium, the objective of Lawrence’s division immediately shifted to the effecting of large-scale separation of uranium isotopes by electromagnetic methods. This change was prompted by the success of the initial experiments at California and by the development at California and at Princeton of ideas on other possible methods. Of the many electromagnetic schemes suggested, three soon were recognized as being the most promising: the ”calutron” mass separator, the magnetron-type separator later developed into the ”ionic centrifuge,” and the ”isotron” method of ”bunching” a beam of ions. The first two of these approaches were followed at California and the third at Princeton. After the first few months, by far the greatest effort was put on the calutron, but some work on the ionic centrifuge was continued at California during the summer of 1942 and was further continued by J. Slepian at the Westinghouse laboratories in Pittsburgh on a small scale through the winter of 1944-1945. Work on the isotron was continued at Princeton until February 1943, when most of the group was transferred to other work. Most of this chapter will be devoted to the calutron since that is the method that has resulted in large-scale production of U-235. A brief description will also be given of the thermal diffusion plant built to provide enriched feed material for the electromagnetic plant.
至1941年12月底，整个铀项目的重组完成时，劳伦斯已经获得了若干分离铀同位素的样品；在重组中，他被正式任命负责制备更多样品并进行各种相关的物理测量。然而，正如冶金实验室很快就将其目标从链式反应的物理学转向钚的大规模生产一样，劳伦斯所辖部门的目标也立即转向以电磁方法实现铀同位素的大规模分离。这一转变是由加利福尼亚初步实验的成功以及加利福尼亚和普林斯顿在其它可能方法上的构想的发展所促成的。在提出的众多电磁方案中，有三种很快被认为最有前途：“卡卢特隆”（calutron）质量分离器、后来发展为“离子离心机”的磁控管型分离器，以及将离子束“聚束”的“同位素分离器”（isotron）方法。前两种方案在加利福尼亚跟进，第三种在普林斯顿跟进。最初几个月之后，绝大部分力量投入到卡卢特隆上，但1942年夏在加利福尼亚仍继续进行了若干离子离心机的工作，并由J.斯莱皮恩（J. Slepian）在匹兹堡西屋电气实验室以较小规模继续到1944—1945年冬。同位素分离器的工作在普林斯顿持续到1943年2月，此后该组大部分人被调往其他工作。本章大部分篇幅将用于讨论卡卢特隆，因为正是这种方法实现了铀-235的大规模生产。此外还将简要描述为电磁分离厂提供浓缩原料而建造的热扩散厂。

11.3. Security requirements make it impossible here - as for other parts of the project - to present many of the most interesting technical details. The importance of the development is considerably greater than is indicated by the mount of space which is given it here.
出于保密要求，此处无法像项目其他部分一样呈现许多最有趣的技术细节。这一发展的重要性远大于此处所给篇幅所显示的程度。

### ELECTROMAGNETIC MASS SEPARATORS 电磁质量分离器

### PRELIMINARY WORK 前期工作

11.4. A. O. Nier’s mass spectrograph was set up primarily to measure relative abundances of isotopes, not to separate large samples. Using vapour from uranium bromide Nier had prepared several small samples of separated isotopes of uranium, but his rate of production was very low indeed, since his ion current amounted to less than one micro-ampere. (A mass spectrograph in which one micro-ampere of normal uranium ions passes through the separating fields to the collectors will collect about one microgram of U-235 per 16-hour day.) The great need of samples of enriched U-235 for nuclear study was recognized early by Lawrence, who decided to see what could be done with the help of the 37-inch (cyclotron) magnet at Berkeley. The initial stages of this work were assisted by a grant from the Research Corporation of New York, which was later repaid. Beginning January 1, 1942, the entire support came from the OSRD through the S-1 Committee. Later, as in other parts of the uranium project, the contracts were taken over by the Manhattan District.
A. O.尼尔的质谱仪最初是为测量同位素的相对丰度而设置的，而非用于分离大量样品。尼尔利用溴化铀蒸气制备了若干分离铀同位素的小样品，但其产率极低，因为他的离子流不足一微安。（在一台质谱仪中，若有一微安的天然铀离子通过分离场到达收集器，则每个16小时工作日约可收集一微克铀-235。）劳伦斯很早就认识到核研究迫切需要浓缩铀-235样品，于是决定看看借助伯克利37英寸（回旋加速器）磁铁能做些什么。这项工作初期得到了纽约研究公司的资助，该款项后来已偿还。自1942年1月1日起，全部支持通过S-1委员会来自科学研究与发展局（OSRD）。后来，如同铀项目的其他部分一样，合同由曼哈顿工程区接管。

11.5. At Berkeley, after some weeks of planning, the 37-inch cyclotron was dismantled on November 24, 1941, and its magnet was used to produce the magnetic field required in what came to be called a ”calutron” (a name representing a contraction of ”California University cyclotron”). An ion source consisting of an electron beam traversing the vapour of a uranium salt was set up corresponding to the ion source shown in the drawing in [Chap. IX, p. 164](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_ix-a.html). Ions were then accelerated to the slit S2 through which they passed into the separating region where the magnetic field bent their paths into semicircles terminating at the collector data. By December 1, 1941, molecular ion beams from the residual gas were obtained, and shortly thereafter the beam consisting of singly charged uranium ions (U+) was brought up to an appreciable strength. It was found that a considerable proportion of the ions leaving the source were U+ ions. For the purpose of testing the collection of separated samples, a collector with two pockets was installed, the two pockets being separated by a distance appropriate to the mass numbers 235 and 238. Two nail collection runs using U+ beams of low strength were made in December, but subsequent analyses of the samples showed only a small separation factor. By the middle of January 1942, a run had been made with a reasonable beam strength and an aggregate flow or through-put of appreciable amount which odd a much improved separation factor. By early February 1942, beams of much greater strength were obtained, and Lawrence reported that good separation factors were obtainable with such beams. By early March 1942, the ion current had been raised still further. These results tended to bear out Lawrence’s hopes that space charge could be neutralized by ionisation of the residual gas in the magnet chamber.
在伯克利，经过数周规划，37英寸回旋加速器于1941年11月24日被拆解，其磁铁被用来产生后来被称为“卡卢特隆”的装置所需的磁场（该名称是“加利福尼亚大学回旋加速器”的缩写）。设置了一个由穿过铀盐蒸气的电子束构成的离子源，其结构与[第九章第164页](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/smyth_ix-a.html)图中所示的离子源相对应。随后离子被加速到狭缝S2，通过该狭缝进入分离区，在那里磁场将它们的路径弯成半圆，终止于收集器处。到1941年12月1日，已获得来自残余气体的分子离子束；此后不久，由单电荷铀离子（U+）组成的离子束增强到可观强度。结果发现，离开离子源的离子中有相当比例是U+离子。为了检验分离样品的收集，安装了一个带两个收集槽的收集器，两槽之间的距离与质量数235和238相适应。12月进行了两次使用低强度U+束的初步收集运行，但随后对样品的分析只显示出很小的分离因子。到1942年1月中旬，已用合理的束流强度和可观的总流量（通过量）进行了一次运行，其分离因子大为改善。到1942年2月初，获得了强度大得多的束流，劳伦斯报告说用这样的束流可以获得良好的分离因子。到1942年3月初，离子流进一步提高。这些结果倾向于证实劳伦斯的希望，即空间电荷可以通过磁铁室内残余气体的电离而中和。

### INITIATION OF A LARGE PROGRAM 大规模计划的启动

11.6. By this time it was clear that the calutron was potentially able to effect much larger scale separations than had ever before been approached by an electromagnetic method. It was evidently desirable to explore the whole field of electromagnetic separation. With this end in view, Lawrence mobilized his group at the Radiation Laboratory of the University of California at Berkeley and began to call in others to help. Among those initially at Berkeley were D. Kicks, P. C. Aebersold, W. M. Brobeck, F. A. Jenkins, K. R. MacKenzie, W. B. Reynolds, D. H. Sloan, F. Oppenheimer, J. G. Backs, B. Peters, A. C. Helmholz, T. Finkelstein, and W. E. Perkins, Jr. Lawrence called back some of his former students, including R. L. Thornton, J. R. Richardson, and others. Among those working at Berkeley for various periods were L. P. Smith from Cornell, E. U. Condon and J. Slepian from Westinghouse, and I. Langmuir and K. H. Kingdom from General Electric. During this early period J. R. Oppenheimer was still at Berkeley and contributed some important ideas. In the fall of 1943 the group was further strengthened by the arrival of a number of English physicists under the leadership of M. L. Oliphant of the University of Birmingham.
此时已经清楚，卡卢特隆有潜力实现比以往任何电磁方法都大得多的规模分离。显然，探索电磁分离的整个领域是可取的。为此目的，劳伦斯动员了他在伯克利加利福尼亚大学辐射实验室的团队，并开始召集其他人前来协助。最初在伯克利的人员包括D.基克斯（D. Kicks）、P. C.艾伯索尔德（P. C. Aebersold）、W. M.布罗贝克（W. M. Brobeck）、F. A.詹金斯（F. A. Jenkins）、K. R.麦肯齐（K. R. MacKenzie）、W. B.雷诺兹（W. B. Reynolds）、D. H.斯隆（D. H. Sloan）、F.奥本海默（F. Oppenheimer）、J. G.巴克斯（J. G. Backs）、B.彼得斯（B. Peters）、A. C.赫尔姆霍尔茨（A. C. Helmholz）、T.芬克尔斯坦（T. Finkelstein）和小W. E.珀金斯（W. E. Perkins, Jr.）。劳伦斯召回了他的一些前学生，包括R. L.桑顿（R. L. Thornton）、J. R.理查森（J. R. Richardson）等人。在不同时期于伯克利工作的人员中，有来自康奈尔的L. P.史密斯（L. P. Smith）、来自西屋的E. U.康登（E. U. Condon）和J.斯莱皮恩，以及来自通用电气的I.朗缪尔（I. Langmuir）和K. H.金登（K. H. Kingdom）。在这一早期阶段，J. R.奥本海默仍在伯克利，并贡献了一些重要想法。1943年秋，在伯明翰大学M. L.奥利芬特（M. L. Oliphant）的带领下，一批英国物理学家到来，使该团队进一步得到加强。

11.7. Initially a large number of different methods were considered and many exploratory experiments were performed. The main effort, however, soon became directed towards the development of the calutron, the objective being a high separation factor and a large current in the positive ion beam.
最初考虑了大量不同的方法，并进行了许多探索性实验。然而，主要力量很快就转向卡卢特隆的研制，目标是获得高分离因子和正离子束中的大电流。

### IMMEDIATE OBJECTIVES 近期目标

11.8. Of the three apparent limitations listed in the first paragraph - difficulty of producing ions, limited fraction of ions actually used, and space charge effects - only the last had yielded to the preliminary attack. Apparently space charge in the neighborhood of the positive ion beam could be nullified to a very great extent. There remained as the immediate objectives a more productive ion source and more complete utilization of the ions.
在第一段所列的三个明显局限——难以产生离子、实际利用的离子比例有限以及空间电荷效应——中，只有最后一个在初步攻关中取得了进展。显然，正离子束附近的空间电荷可以在很大程度上被抵消。于是近期目标仍然是更有产率的离子源和对离子更充分的利用。

11.9. The factors that control the effectiveness of an ion source are many. Both the design of the source proper and the method of drawing ions from it are involved. The problems to be solved cannot be formulated simply and must be attacked by methods that are largely empirical. Even if security restrictions permitted an exposition of the innumerable forms of ion source and accelerating system that were tried, such exposition would be too technical to present here.
控制离子源效能的因素很多。既涉及离子源本身的设计，也涉及从中引出离子的方法。需要解决的问题无法简单表述，必须主要依靠经验方法来攻克。即使保密限制允许阐述曾尝试过的无数种离子源和加速系统，这样的阐述也过于专业，不宜在此呈现。

11.10. Turning to the problem of effecting more complete utilization of the ions, we must consider in some detail the principle of operation of the calutron. The calutron depends on the fact that singly charged ions moving in a uniform magnetic field perpendicular to their direction of ion are bent into circular paths of radius proportional to their momenta. Considering now just a single isotope, it is apparent that the ions passing through the two slits (and thus passing into the large evacuated region in which the magnetic field is present) do not initially follow a single direction, but have many initial directions lying within a small angle, whose size depends on the width of the slits.
转到如何更充分地利用离子这一问题，我们必须较为详细地考察卡卢特隆的工作原理。卡卢特隆依赖于这样一个事实：在垂直于其运动方向的均匀磁场中运动的单电荷离子，会被弯成半径与其动量成正比的圆形路径。现在只考虑单一同位素，显然，通过两个狭缝（从而进入存在磁场的大真空区）的离子最初并不沿单一方向运动，而是具有位于一个小角度范围内的许多初始方向，该角度的大小取决于狭缝的宽度。

Fortunately, however, since all the ions of the isotope in question follow curved paths of the sam diameter, ions starting out in slightly different directions tend to meet again - or almost meet again - after completing a semicircle. It is, of course, at this position of reconvergence that the collector is placed. Naturally, the ions of another isotope (for example, ions of mass 238 instead of 235) behave similarly, except that they follow circles of slightly different diameter. Samples of the two isotopes lawyer caught in collectors at the two different positions of reconvergence. Now the utilization of a greater fraction of the ions originally produced may be accomplished readily enough by widening the two slits referred to. But to widen the slits to any great extent without sacrificing sharpness of focus at the reconvergence positions is not easy. Indeed it can be accomplished only by use of carefully proportioned space variations in the magnetic field strength. Fortunately, such variations were worked out successfully.
然而幸运的是，由于该同位素的所有离子都沿相同直径的弯曲路径运动，从略有不同的方向出发的离子在完成半圆之后会再次会聚——或几乎再次会聚。收集器自然就放置在这一再会聚的位置。当然，另一种同位素的离子（例如质量数为238而非235的离子）行为类似，只是它们沿直径略有不同的圆运动。两种同位素的样品分别在两个不同的再会聚位置被收集器捕获。如今，若要利用最初产生的离子中更大的比例，只需加宽前面提到的两个狭缝即可相当容易地实现。但要在不牺牲再会聚位置聚焦锐度的前提下大幅加宽狭缝并不容易。事实上，只有通过在磁场强度中精心设置的空间变化才能做到。幸运的是，这种变化被成功地设计出来了。

11.11. Another problem, not so immediate but nevertheless recognized as important to any production plant, was that of more efficient use of the magnetic field. Since large electromagnets are expensive both to build and to operate, it was natural to consider using the same magnetic field for several ion beams. The experimental realisation of such an economical scheme became a major task of the laboratory.
另一个问题虽然不那么紧迫，但被认为对任何生产厂都很重要，那就是更有效地利用磁场。由于大型电磁铁无论建造还是运行都很昂贵，自然要考虑让同一磁场服务于多个离子束。这种经济方案的实验实现成为实验室的一项主要任务。

### THE GIANT MAGNET 巨型磁铁

11.12. Although the scale of separation reached by March 1942 was much greater than anything that had previously been done with an electromagnetic mass separator, it was still very far from that required to produce amounts of material that would be of military significance. The problems that have been outlined not only had to be solved, but they had to be solved on a grand scale. The 37-inch cyclotron magnet that had been used was still capable of furnishing useful information, but larger equipment was desirable. Fortunately a very much larger magnet, intended for a giant cyclotron, had been under construction at Berkeley This magnet, with a pole diameter of 184 inches and a pole gap of 72 inches, was to be the largest in existence. Work on it had been interrupted because of the war, but it was already sufficiently advanced so that it could be finished within a few months if adequate priorities were granted. Aside from the magnet itself, the associated building, laboratories, shops, etc., were almost ideal for the development of the calutron. Needless to say, work was resumed on the giant magnet and by the end of May 1942 it was ready for use. [\*](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/#note)
尽管到1942年3月所达到的分离规模已远大于以往任何电磁质量分离器所做的工作，但距离生产具有军事意义的物质量仍然很远。所概述的问题不仅必须解决，而且必须大规模地解决。所使用的37英寸回旋加速器磁铁仍能提供有用的信息，但人们希望有更大的设备。幸运的是，伯克利已经在建造一台用于巨型回旋加速器的极大磁铁。这台磁铁极面直径为184英寸，极隙为72英寸，将是当时世界上最大的磁铁。由于战争，它的建造曾被中断，但工程进度已经相当靠前，只要给予足够的优先权，几个月内就能完成。除磁铁本身外，相关的建筑、实验室、车间等几乎非常适合卡卢特隆的研制。不用说，巨型磁铁的建造工作恢复了，到1942年5月底它已可供使用。[\*](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/smyth-report/#note)

11.13. The first experiments using the 37-inch magnet have been described in a previous paragraph. Later developments proceeded principally along these two lines: construction and installation of a properly engineered separation unit for the 37-inch magnet, and design and construction of experimental separation units to go into the big magnet.
使用37英寸磁铁的最初实验已在上一段中描述。此后的研制主要沿两条路线进行：为37英寸磁铁建造和安装一套经过适当工程设计的分离装置，以及设计和建造用于大磁铁的实验性分离装置。

11.14. Besides the gradual increase in ion beam strength and separation factor that resulted from a series of developments in the ion source and in the accelerating system, the hoped-for improvement in utilization of ions was achieved during the summer of 1942, using the giant magnet. Further, it was possible to maintain more than one ion beam in the same magnetic separating region. Experiments on this latter problem did run into some difficulties, however, and it appeared that there might be limitations on the number of sources and receivers that could be put in a single unit as well as on the current that could be used in each beam without spoiling the separation.
除了离子源和加速系统的一系列改进使离子束强度和分离因子逐步提高之外，1942年夏利用巨型磁铁还实现了人们所期望的离子利用率改进。此外，还能够在同一磁分离区中维持一个以上的离子束。然而，关于后一问题的实验确实遇到了一些困难，看来单个装置中可放置的离子源和接收器数量以及每个束流在不破坏分离的前提下可使用的电流都可能存在限制。

11.15. It was evident that many separator units would be needed to get an amount of production of military significance. Therefore, consideration was given to various systems of combining groups of units in economical arrangements. A scheme was worked out which was later used in the production plants and which has proved satisfactory.
显然，要获得具有军事意义的产量，将需要许多分离装置。因此，人们考虑了将各组装置以经济方式组合起来的各种系统。一套方案被设计出来，后来用于生产厂，并被证明令人满意。

### ADVANTAGEOUS OF THE ELECTROMAGNETIC SYSTEM 电磁系统的优势

11.16. In September 1942, both the gaseous diffusion and the centrifugal methods of uranium isotope separation had been under intensive study - and for a longer period than in the case of the electromagnetic method. Both of these methods - gaseous diffusion and centrifuge - looked feasible for large-scale production of U-235, but both would require hundreds of stages to achieve large-scale separation. Neither had actually produced any appreciable amounts of separated U-235. No large-scale plant for plutonium production was under way, and the self sustaining chain reaction which was to produce plutonium had not yet been proved attainable. But in the case of the electromagnetic method, after the successful separation of milligram amounts, there was no question as to the scientific feasibility. If one unit could separate 10 mg a day, 100,000,000 units could separate one ton a day. The questions were of cost and time. Each unit was to be a complicated electromagnetic device requiring high vacuum, high voltages, and intense magnetic fields: and a great deal of research and development work would be required before complete, large-scale, units could be constructed. Many skilled operators would probably be needed. Altogether, at that time it looked very expensive, but it also looked certain and relatively quick. Moreover, the smallness of the units had the advantage that development could continue, modifications could be made in the course of construction or, within limits, after construction, and capacity could always be expanded by building new units.
1942年9月，气体扩散法和离心法这两种铀同位素分离方法都已处于深入研究之中，而且研究时间比电磁法更长。气体扩散和离心这两种方法看来都可用于铀-235的大规模生产，但两者都需要数百级才能实现大规模分离。两者实际上都尚未生产出任何可观数量的分离铀-235。当时尚无大规模钚生产厂在建，而本应用于生产钚的自持链式反应也尚未被证明可以实现。但在电磁法方面，在成功分离毫克量之后，其科学可行性已毫无疑问。如果一个装置一天能分离10毫克，那么一亿个装置一天就能分离一吨。问题在于成本和时间。每个装置都将是一台复杂的电磁设备，需要高真空、高电压和强磁场；在能够建造完整的大型装置之前，还需要大量的研究和发展工作。很可能需要许多熟练的操作人员。总之，当时它看起来非常昂贵，但也显得确定且相对迅速。此外，装置体积小有一个好处，即研制可以持续进行，修改可以在建造过程中进行，或在限定范围内在建造之后进行，而生产能力总能通过增建新装置来扩大。

### POLICY QUESTION 政策问题

11.17. On the basis of rather incomplete scientific and engineering information on all the methods and on the basis of equally dubious cost estimates, decisions had to be made on three issues: (1) whether to build an electromagnetic plant; (2) how big such a plant should be; (3) at what point of development the design should be frozen.
在关于所有方法的科学和工程资料都相当不完整、成本估算同样可疑的情况下，必须就三个问题作出决定：（1）是否建造电磁分离厂；（2）这样的工厂应有多大；（3）在设计发展到什么阶段时应将其冻结。

### APPROVAL OF PLANT CONSTRUCTION 工厂建设方案的批准

11.18. On the strength of the results reported on experiments at Berkeley in the summer of 1942, the S-1 Executive Committee at a meeting at Berkeley on September 13-14, 1942, recommended that commitments be made by the Army for an electromagnetic separation plant to be built at the Tennessee Valley site (Clinton Engineer Works). It was recommended that it should be agreed that commitments for this plant might be cancelled on the basis of later information. It was recommended that a pilot plant should be erected at the Tennessee Valley site as soon as possible. (However, this recommendation was subsequently withdrawn and such a pilot plant was never built.) The construction of a production plant was authorized by General Groves on November 5, 1942, with the understanding that the design for the first units was to be frozen immediately.
根据1942年夏伯克利实验所报告的结果，S-1执行委员会于1942年9月13日至14日在伯克利举行会议，建议由军方承诺在田纳西河谷厂址（克林顿工程厂）建造一座电磁分离厂。建议同意该厂的承诺可依据后来获得的信息予以取消。建议尽快在田纳西河谷厂址建立一座中间试验厂。（然而，这一建议后来被撤回，这样的中间试验厂从未建成。）格罗夫斯将军于1942年11月5日批准建造生产厂，但有一项谅解，即首批装置的设计应立即冻结。

### ORGANIZATION FOR PLANNING AND CONSTRUCTION 规划与建设的组织

11.19. In describing the production of plutonium, we discussed the division of responsibility between the Metallurgical Project and the duPont Company. The electromagnetic separation plant was planned and built under a somewhat different scheme of organization. The responsibility was divided between six major groups. The Radiation Laboratory at the University of California was responsible for research and development; the Westinghouse Electric and Manufacturing Company for making the mechanical parts, i.e., sources, receivers, pumps, tanks, etc..; the General Electric Company for the electrical equipment and controls the Allis-Chalmers Company for the magnets; the Stone and Webster Engineering Company for the construction and assembly; and the Tennessee Eastman Company for operation. All five industrial concerns kept groups of their engineers at Berkeley so that a system of frequent informal conference and cross- checking was achieved. Thus the major part of the planning was one cooperatively in a single group, even though the details might be left to the home offices of the various companies.
在描述钚的生产时，我们讨论了冶金项目与杜邦公司之间的责任分工。电磁分离厂则是在一种略有不同的组织方案下规划和建造的。责任被划分为六个主要组。加利福尼亚大学辐射实验室负责研究与发展；西屋电气与制造公司负责制造机械部件，即离子源、接收器、泵、真空箱等；通用电气公司负责电气设备与控制；阿利斯-查默斯公司负责磁铁；斯通-韦伯斯特工程公司负责建造与装配；田纳西伊士曼公司负责运行。五家工业公司都在伯克利派驻了工程师小组，从而形成了一种频繁举行非正式会议和相互核对的制度。因此，规划的主要部分是在一个单一团体中合作完成的，尽管细节可能留给各公司的总部处理。

### THE BASIS OF THE TECHNICAL DECISIONS 技术决策的依据

11.20. Strangely enough, although the theory of the self sustaining chain-reacting pile is already well worked out, the theory of gaseous discharge, after fifty years of intensive study, is still inadequate for the prediction of the exact behavior of the ions in a calutron. The amount of U-235 collected per day, and the purity of the material collected, are affected by many factors, including: (1) the width, spacing, and shape of the collector, (2) the pressure in the magnet space, (3) the strength and uniformity of the magnetic field, (4) the shape and spacing of the defining slits and accelerating system, (5) the accelerating voltage (6) the size and shape of the slit in the arc source from which the ions come, (7) the current in the arc, (8) the position of the arc within the arc chamber, (9) the pressure of vapor in the arc chamber, (10) the chemical nature of the vapor. Evidently there was not time for a systematic study of all possible combinations of variables. The development had to be largely intuitive. A variety of conditions had to be studied and a number of partial interpretations had to be made. Then the accumulated experience of the group, the ”feel” of the problem, had to be translated into specific plans and recommendations.
说来奇怪，尽管自持链式反应堆的理论已经相当完善，但气体放电的理论经过五十年的深入研究，仍不足以预测卡卢特隆中离子的确切行为。每天收集的铀-235数量以及所收集材料的纯度受许多因素影响，包括：（1）收集器的宽度、间距和形状；（2）磁铁空间中的压力；（3）磁场强度和均匀性；（4）限定狭缝和加速系统的形状与间距；（5）加速电压；（6）离子所来自的弧光源中狭缝的大小和形状；（7）弧电流；（8）弧在弧室内的位置；（9）弧室内蒸气压力；（10）蒸气的化学性质。显然，没有时间对所有可能的变量组合进行系统研究。研制工作在很大程度上只能凭直觉进行。必须研究各种条件，作出若干部分性的解释。然后，该团体积累的经验、对问题的“感觉”，必须转化为具体的计划和建议。

### TECHNICAL DECISIONS REQUIRED 需要作出的技术决策

11.21. (a) The Number of Stages. As in all methods, a compromise must be made between yield and separation factor. In the electromagnetic system, the separation factor is much higher than in other systems so that the number of stages required is small. There was a possibility that a single stage might be sufficient. Early studies indicated that attempts to push the separation factor so high as to make single-stage operation feasible cut the yield to an impracticably small figure.
（a）级数。与所有方法一样，必须在产率和分离因子之间作出折中。在电磁系统中，分离因子远高于其他系统，因此所需级数很少。有可能单级就足够。早期研究表明，若试图把分离因子推高到使单级运行可行的程度，产率会降到不切实际的低水平。

11.22. (b) Specifications. The information and experience that had been acquired on the variables such as those mentioned above had to be translated into decisions on the following principal points before design could actually begin: (1) the size of a unit as determined by the radius of curvature of the ion path the length of the source slit, and the arrangement of source and receivers; (2) the maximum intensity of magnetic field required; (3) whether or not to use large divergence of ion beams: (4) the number of ion sources and receivers per unit; (5) whether the source should be at high potential or at ground potential; (6) the number of accelerating electrodes and the maximum potentials to be applied to them; (7) the power requirements tar arcs, accelerating voltages, pumps, etc.; (8) pumping requirement, (9) number of units per pole gap; (10) number of units per building.
（b）规格。在能够真正开始设计之前，必须把在所提到的这类变量上获得的信息和经验转化为对以下主要问题的决定：（1）由离子路径曲率半径、离子源狭缝长度以及离子源与接收器的布置所决定的装置尺寸；（2）所需的最大磁场强度；（3）是否采用大的离子束发散角；（4）每台装置的离子源和接收器数量；（5）离子源应处于高电位还是接地电位；（6）加速电极的数量及施加于其上的最大电位；（7）弧、加速电压、泵等所需的功率；（8）抽气要求；（9）每个极隙中的装置数量；（10）每座建筑中的装置数量。

### EXPERIMENTAL UNITS AT BERKELEY 伯克利的实验装置

11.23. Most of the design features for the first plant had to be frozen in the fall of 1942 on the basis of results obtained with runs made using the giant magnet at Berkeley. The plant design, however, called for units of a somewhat different type. While there was no reason to suppose that these changes would introduce any difference in performance, it was obviously desirable to build a prototype unit at Berkeley. The construction of this unit was approved at about the same time that the first plant units were ordered so that experience with it had no influence on fundamental design, but it was finished and operating by April 1943, that is, six months before the first plant unit. Consequently, it was invaluable for testing and training purposes. Later, a third magnet was built in the big magnet building at Berkeley. All told, there have been six separator units available simultaneously for experimental or pilot plant purposes at Berkeley. Much auxiliary work has also been done outside the complete units.
第一座工厂的大部分设计特征必须在1942年秋根据伯克利巨型磁铁运行所得结果予以冻结。然而，工厂设计所要求的是类型略有不同的装置。虽然没有理由认为这些变化会带来性能上的差异，但在伯克利建造一台原型装置显然是可取的。这台装置的建造获得批准的时间与首批工厂装置订购的时间大致相同，因此使用它的经验对基本设计没有影响，但它于1943年4月完成并投入运行，即比第一台工厂装置早六个月。因此，它对于测试和培训目的极为宝贵。后来，在伯克利的大磁铁建筑中又建造了第三台磁铁。总计起来，伯克利曾同时有六套分离装置可用于实验或中间试验厂目的。在完整装置之外还进行了大量辅助工作。

### ISOTRON SEPARATOR 同位素分离器

11.24. A we have already said, H. D. Smyth of Princeton became interested in electromagnetic methods of separation in the late summer and fall of 1941. He was particularly interested in devising some method of using an extended ion source and beam instead of one limited essentially to one dimension by a system as in the calutron mass separator. A method of actually achieving separation using an extended ion source was suggested by R. R. Wilson of Princeton. The device which resulted from Wilson’s ideas was given the deliberately meaningless name ”isotron.”
正如我们已经说过的，普林斯顿的H. D.斯迈思在1941年夏末和秋季对电磁分离方法产生了兴趣。他尤其感兴趣的是设计某种方法，使用扩展的离子源和离子束，而不是像卡卢特隆质量分离器那样，基本上被系统限制在一个维度上的离子源。普林斯顿的R. R.威尔逊（R. R. Wilson）提出了一种利用扩展离子源实际实现分离的方法。由威尔逊的想法产生的装置被赋予了一个故意毫无意义的名称——“同位素分离器”（isotron）。

11.25. The isotron is an electromagnetic mass separator using extended source of ions, in contrast to the slit sources used in ordinary mass spectrographs. The ions from the extended source are first accelerated by a constant, high-intensity, electric field and are then further accelerated by a low-intensity electric field varying at radio frequency and in ”saw tooth” manner. The effect of the constant electric field is to project a strong beam of ions down a tube with uniform kinetic energy and therefore with velocities inversely proportional to the square root of the masses of ions. The varying electric field, on the other hand, introduces small, periodic variations in ion velocity, and has the effect of causing the ions to ”bunch” at a certain distance down the tube. (This same principle is used in the klystron high- frequency oscillator, where the electrons are ”bunched” or ”velocity-modulated.”) The bunches of ions of different mass travel with different velocities and therefore become separated. At the position (actually on area perpendicular to the beam where this occurs, an analyzer applies a transverse focussing electric field with a radio frequency component synchronized with the arrival of the bunches. The synchronization is such that the varying component of the transverse field strength is zero when the U-235 ion bunches come through and a maximum when the U-238 ion bunches come through. The U-235 beams are focussed on a collector, but the U-238 bunches are deflected Thus the separation is accomplished.
同位素分离器是一种使用扩展离子源的电磁质量分离器，与普通质谱仪所用的狭缝源形成对照。来自扩展源的离子首先被恒定高强度电场加速，然后被一个以射频并按“锯齿”方式变化的低强度电场进一步加速。恒定电场的作用是把强离子束沿一根管子射出，使其具有均匀的动能，因而速度与离子质量的平方根成反比。另一方面，交变电场引入小的周期性离子速度变化，其效果是使离子在管内一定距离处“聚束”。（同样的原理也用于速调管高频振荡器，其中电子被“聚束”或“速度调制”。）不同质量的离子束团以不同速度行进，因而彼此分离。在发生这种情况的位置（实际上是垂直于束流的一个面上），一个分析器施加横向聚焦电场，该电场具有与束团到达同步的射频分量。同步方式使得当铀-235离子束团通过时横向场强的变化分量为零，而当铀-238离子束团通过时为最大值。铀-235束被聚焦到收集器上，而铀-238束团则被偏转。分离便由此实现。

11.26. This scheme was described at the December 18, 1941 meeting of the Uranium Committee and immediately thereafter was discussed more fully with Lawrence, who paid a visit to Princeton. The promise of the method seemed sufficient to justify experimental work, which was begun immediately under an OSRD contract and continued until February 1943. Since the idea involved was a novel one, there were two outstanding issues: (1) whether the method would work at all; (2) whether it could be developed for large-scale production promptly enough to compete with the more orthodox methods already under development.
这一方案在1941年12月18日铀委员会会议上作了描述，此后不久又与劳伦斯进行了更充分的讨论，劳伦斯访问了普林斯顿。该方法的希望似乎足以证明进行实验工作是合理的，实验工作在OSRD合同下立即开始，一直持续到1943年2月。由于所涉及的想法是新颖的，有两个突出问题：（1）该方法是否根本可行；（2）它能否迅速发展到大规模生产，以与已在研制中的更为正统的方法竞争。

11.27. An experimental isotron was constructed and put into operation by the end of January 1942. Preliminary experiments at that time indicated that the isotopes of lithium could be separated by the method. The first successful collection of partially separated uranium isotopes was made in the spring
建造了一台实验性同位素分离器，并于1942年1月底投入运行。当时的初步实验表明，用该方法可以分离锂的同位素。首次成功收集到部分分离的铀同位素是在1942年春天

11.28. Unfortunately, progress during the summer and fall of 1942 was not as rapid as had been hoped. Consequently, it was decided to close down the Princeton project in order to permit sending the personnel to the site where the atomic-bomb laboratory was about to get under way. Before the group left Princeton a small experimental isotron collected several samples of partly separated uranium. Thus, the method worked; but its large-scale applicability was not fully investigated.
遗憾的是，1942年夏秋的进展不如人们所希望的那样快。因此，决定关闭普林斯顿项目，以便将人员派往原子弹实验室即将开始工作的场址。在该组离开普林斯顿之前，一台小型实验性同位素分离器收集了若干部分分离的铀样品。因此，该方法确实可行；但其大规模适用性未得到充分研究。

### THE MAGNETRON AND THE IONIC CENTRIFUGE 磁控管与离子离心机

11.29. In December 1941, when the whole subject of isotope separation was under discussion at Berkeley, the magnetron was suggested as a possible mass separator. In the meantime, Smyth of Princeton had been in contact with L. P. Smith of Cornell and had discovered that Smith and his students had done a considerable amount of work - and with evidence of success - on the separation of the isotopes of lithium by just such a method. This was reported to Lawrence in Washington at one of the December, 1941, meetings of the Uranium Committee. Lawrence immediately got in touch with Smith, with the result that Smith worked on the method at Berkeley from February 1942 to June 1942. J. Slepian of the Westinghouse Research Laboratory in East Pittsburgh came to Berkeley in the winter of 1941-1942 at Lawrence’s invitation and became interested in a modification of the magnetron which he called an ionic centrifuge. Slepian stayed at Berkeley most of the time until the fall of 1942, after which he returned to East Pittsburgh where he continued the work.
1941年12月，当整个同位素分离课题在伯克利讨论时，有人提出磁控管可作为一种可能的质量分离器。与此同时，普林斯顿的斯迈思与康奈尔的L. P.史密斯取得了联系，并发现史密斯及其学生已经用正是这样一种方法在锂同位素分离方面做了大量工作，并有成功的证据。这一情况在1941年12月于华盛顿举行的铀委员会会议上报告给了劳伦斯。劳伦斯立即与史密斯取得联系，结果是史密斯从1942年2月到1942年6月在伯克利从事该方法的工作。东匹兹堡西屋研究实验室的J.斯莱皮恩应劳伦斯之邀于1941—1942年冬来到伯克利，并对磁控管的一种改型产生了兴趣，他称之为离子离心机。斯莱皮恩在伯克利待到1942年秋的大部分时间，之后返回东匹兹堡继续工作。

11.30. No separation of uranium was actually attempted in the magnetron. Experiments with lithium with low ion currents showed some separation, but no consistent results were obtained with high ion currents. In the case of the ionic centrifuge, uranium samples have been collected showing appreciable separation, but the results have not been clear-cut or consistent.
在磁控管中实际上没有尝试分离铀。用低离子流进行的锂实验显示出一些分离，但用高离子流未获得一致的结果。在离子离心机方面，收集到的铀样品显示出可观的分离，但结果并不明确，也不一致。

### THE SITUATION AS OF EARLY 1943 1943年初的形势

11.31. With the virtual elimination of the isotron and the ionic centrifuge from the development program, the calutron separator became the only electromagnetic method worked on intensively. Construction of initial units of a plant had been authorized and designs had been frozen for such units, but the whole electromagnetic program had been in existence for only a little more than a year and it was obvious that available designs were based on shrewd guesses rather than on adequate research. A similar situation might have occurred with the chain reacting pile if unlimited amounts of uranium and graphite had been available before the theory had been worked out or before the nuclear constants had been well determined. Fortunately the nature of the two projects was very different, making it a less speculative venture to build an electromagnetic plant unit hastily than would have been the case for the pile. Further research and development could proceed advantageously even while initial units of the plant were being built and operated.
随着同位素分离器和离子离心机实际上被从研制计划中剔除，卡卢特隆分离器成为唯一被集中研究的电磁方法。工厂首批装置的建造已获批准，这些装置的设计也已冻结，但整个电磁计划存在的时间仅略多于一年，显然现有设计依据的是精明的猜测而非充分的研究。如果在该理论被建立或核常数被很好确定之前就有无限的铀和石墨可用，链式反应堆可能会出现类似的情况。幸运的是，这两个项目的性质非常不同，使得匆忙建造电磁分离厂装置比建造反应堆的投机性要小。即使在工厂首批装置正在建造和运行的同时，进一步的研究与发展仍可有利地进行。

### CONSTRUCTION AND OPERATION; MARCH 1943 TO JUNE 1945 建设与运行；1943年3月至1945年6月

#### COMPARISON WITH DIFFUSION AND PLUTONIUM PLANTS 与扩散厂和钚厂的比较

11.32. The preceding chapters show that the end of 1942 was a time of decision throughout the uranium project. For it was at that time that a self-sustaining chain reaction was first produced, that construction was authorized for the Hanford plutonium plant, the diffusion plant at Clinton, and the electromagnetic plant at Clinton. The diffusion plant was more flexible than the plutonium plant, since the diffusion plant could be broken down into sections and stages, built in whole or in part, to produce varying amounts of U-235 of varying degrees of enrichment. The electromagnetic plant was even more flexible, since each separator unit was practically independent of the other units. The separation process consisted of loading a charge into a unit, running the unit for a while, then stopping it and removing the product. To be sure, the units were built in groups, but most of the controls were separate for each unit. This feature made it possible to build the plant in steps and to start operating the first part even before the second was begun. It was also possible to change the design of subsequent units as construction proceeded; within limits it was possible even to replace obsolescent units in the early groups with new improved units.
前面几章表明，1942年末是整个铀项目的决策时期。因为正是在那时首次产生了自持链式反应，也正是那时批准建造汉福德钚厂、克林顿扩散厂和克林顿电磁分离厂。扩散厂比钚厂更灵活，因为扩散厂可以分解为若干区段和级，可整体或部分建造，以生产不同数量和不同浓缩度的铀-235。电磁分离厂甚至更为灵活，因为每台分离装置实际上独立于其他装置。分离过程包括向装置中装入一份物料，运行一段时间，然后停止并取出产品。诚然，装置是成组建造的，但大多数控制装置对各装置是独立的。这一特点使得工厂可以分阶段建造，甚至在第二部分开始之前就启动第一部分运行。随着建造的推进，还可以改变后续装置的设计；在限定范围内，甚至可以用改进的新装置替换早期组中的过时装置。

### NATURE AND ORGANIZATION OF DEVELOPMENT WORK 开发工作的性质与组织

11.33. Construction of the first series of electromagnetic units at Clinton began in March of 1943 and this part of the plant was ready for operation in November 1943. The group at Berkeley continued to improve the ion sources, the receivers, and the auxiliary equipment, aiming always at greater ion currents. In fact, Berkeley reports describe no less than seventy-one different types of source and one hundred and fifteen different types of receiver, all of which reached the design stage and most of which were constructed and tested. As soon as the value of a given design change was proved, every effort was made to incorporate it in the designs of new units.
克林顿第一批电磁装置的建造始于1943年3月，这部分工厂于1943年11月具备运行条件。伯克利的团队继续改进离子源、接收器和辅助设备，始终以更大的离子流为目标。事实上，伯克利的报告描述了不少于七十一种不同类型的离子源和一百一十五种不同类型的接收器，所有这些都达到了设计阶段，其中大多数被制造并测试。一旦某项设计改动的价值得到证实，就尽一切努力将其纳入新装置的设计中。

11.34. Such developments as these required constant interchange of information among laboratory, engineering, construction, and operating groups. Fortunately the liaison was excellent. The companies stationed representatives at Berkeley, and members of the research group at Berkeley paid frequent and prolonged visits to the plant at Clinton. In fact, some of the research men were transferred to the payroll of the Tennessee Eastman Company operating the plant at Clinton, and a group of over one hundred physicists and research engineers still kept on the Berkeley payroll were assigned to Clinton. Particularly in the early stages of operation the Berkeley men stationed at Clinton were invaluable as ”trouble shooters” and in instructing operators. A section of the plant continued to be maintained as a pilot unit for testing modified equipment and revised operating procedures, and was run jointly by the Berkeley group and by Tennessee Eastman. In addition to the British group under Oliphant already mentioned, there was a British group of chemists at Clinton under J. W. Baxter.
这类研制工作需要实验室、工程、建造和运行各组之间不断交流信息。幸运的是，联络非常出色。各公司在伯克利派驻了代表，伯克利研究组的成员也频繁且长期地访问克林顿的工厂。事实上，一些研究人员被转到运营克林顿工厂的田纳西伊士曼公司的工资名册上，另有一百多名仍由伯克利支付薪金的物理学家和研究工程师被派往克林顿。特别是在运行的早期阶段，驻扎在克林顿的伯克利人员作为“故障排除者”和操作人员的指导者极为宝贵。工厂的一个区段一直作为中间试验装置保留，用于测试改进的设备和修订的操作程序，由伯克利组和田纳西伊士曼共同运行。除了已经提到的奥利芬特领导的英国组之外，克林顿还有一个由J. W.巴克斯特（J. W. Baxter）领导的英国化学家组。

### CHEMICAL PROBLEMS 化学问题

11.35. Originally, the uranium salts used as sources of vapor for the ion-producing arcs had not been investigated with any very great thoroughness at Berkeley, but as the process developed, a good deal of work was done on these salts, and a search was made for a uranium compound that would be better than that originally used. Some valuable studies were also made on methods of producing the compound chosen.
最初，用作产生离子弧的蒸气来源的铀盐在伯克利并未得到非常彻底的研究，但随着工艺的发展，对这些盐做了大量工作，并寻找一种比最初所用更好的铀化合物。还对生产所选化合物的方法进行了一些有价值的研究。

11.36. By far the most important chemical problem was the recovery of the processed uranium compounds from the separation units. This recovery problem had two phases. In units of the first stage it was essential to recover the separated uranium from the receivers with maximum efficiency; whereas recovery of the scattered unseparated uranium from other parts of the unit was less important. But if higher stage units are used even the starting material contains a high concentration of U-235, and it is essential to recover all the material in the unit at the end of each run, i.e., material remaining in the ion source and material deposited on the accelerating electrodes, on the walls of the magnet chamber, and on the receiver walls.
最重要的化学问题无疑是从分离装置中回收加工过的铀化合物。这一回收问题有两个方面。在第一级装置中，必须最高效率地从接收器中回收已分离的铀；而从装置其他部分回收散落的未分离铀则不那么重要。但如果使用更高级的装置，即使起始材料也含有高浓度的铀-235，那么在每次运行结束时必须回收装置中的所有材料，即留在离子源中的材料以及沉积在加速电极上、磁铁室壁上和接收器壁上的材料。

### THE THERMAL DIFFUSION PLANT 热扩散厂

11.37. For nearly a year the electromagnetic plant was the only one in operation. Therefore the urge to increase its production rate was tremendous. It was realized that any method of enriching - even slightly enriching - the material to be fed into the plant would increase the production rate appreciably. For example, an electromagnetic unit that could produce a gram a day of 40 per cent pure U-235 from natural uranium could produce two grams a day of 80 per cent U-235 if the concentration of U-235 in the feed material was twice the natural concentration (1.4 per cent instead of 0.7 per cent).
在大约一年的时间里，电磁分离厂是唯一运行的工厂。因此，提高其生产率的迫切性极大。人们认识到，任何能浓缩——哪怕只是轻微浓缩——送入工厂材料的办法，都会明显提高生产率。例如，一台电磁装置若能从天然铀每天生产一克纯度为40%的铀-235，那么当原料中铀-235的浓度为天然浓度的两倍（1.4%而非0.7%）时，它每天就能生产两克纯度为80%的铀-235。

11.38. We have already referred to the work done by P. H. Abelson of the Naval Research Laboratory on the separation of the uranium isotopes by thermal diffusion in a liquid compound of uranium. By the spring of 1943 Abelson had set up a pilot plant that accomplished appreciable separation of a considerable quantity of uranium compound. It was therefore proposed that a large-scale thermal diffusion plant should be constructed. Such a plant would be cheaper than any of the other large-scale plants, and it could be built more quickly. Its principal drawback was its enormous consumption of steam, which made it appear impracticable for the whole job of separation.
我们已经提到过海军研究实验室的P. H.阿贝尔森（P. H. Abelson）在通过液态铀化合物中的热扩散分离铀同位素方面所做的工作。到1943年春天，阿贝尔森已建立了一座中间试验厂，对相当数量的铀化合物实现了可观的分离。因此有人提议建造一座大规模热扩散厂。这样的工厂将比其他任何大型工厂都便宜，而且可以更快建成。其主要缺点是蒸汽消耗量巨大，这使其在整个分离任务中显得不切实际。

11.39. Not only was a pilot plant already in operation at the Naval Research Laboratory, but a second, somewhat larger plant was under construction at the Philadelphia Navy Yard. Through the cooperation of the Navy both the services of Abelson and the plans for a large-scale plant were made available to the Manhattan District. It was decided to erect the large-scale thermal diffusion plant at Clinton (using steam from the power plant constructed for the gaseous diffusion plant) and to use the thermal-diffusion-plant product as feed material for the electromagnetic plant.
不仅海军研究实验室已有一座中间试验厂在运行，而且在费城海军船厂还有一座稍大的第二座工厂正在建造。通过海军的合作，阿贝尔森的服务和大型工厂的计划都提供给了曼哈顿工程区。决定在克林顿建造大型热扩散厂（使用为气体扩散厂建造的动力厂提供的蒸汽），并将热扩散厂的产品用作电磁分离厂的原料。

11.40. This new thermal diffusion plant was erected in amazingly short time during the late summer of 1944. In spite of some disappointments, operation of this plant has succeeded in its purpose of considerably increasing the production rate of the electromagnetic plant. It has also stimulated work on the uranium recovery problem. The future of this plant is uncertain. Operation of the gaseous-diffusion plant makes it difficult to get enough steam to operate the thermal diffusion plant, but also furnishes another user for its product.
这座新的热扩散厂于1944年夏末在惊人的短时间内建成。尽管有一些令人失望之处，该厂的运行成功地实现了其目的，即大幅提高电磁分离厂的生产率。它还推动了对铀回收问题的研究。该厂的未来尚不确定。气体扩散厂的运行使得难以获得足够的蒸汽来运行热扩散厂，但也为其产品提供了另一个用户。

### MISCELLANEOUS PROBLEMS 其他问题

11.41. Although the scientific and technical problems which confronted the Berkeley groups were probably not as varied or numerous as the problems encountered at Chicago and Columbia, they were nevertheless numerous. Thus many problems arose in the designing of the electric power and control circuits, magnetic fields, insulators, vacuum pumps, tanks, collectors, and sources. Many equipment items had to be designed from scratch and the mass-produced under high priority.
尽管伯克利各组所面临的科学和技术问题可能不如芝加哥和哥伦比亚遇到的问题那样多样或众多，但它们仍然很多。因此，在电力与控制回路、磁场、绝缘子、真空泵、真空箱、收集器和离子源的设计中出现了许多问题。许多设备项目必须从头设计，并以高优先级批量生产。

### PRESENT STATUS 现状

11.42. The electromagnetic separation plant was in large-scale operation during the winter of 1944-1945, and produced U-235 of sufficient purity for use in atomic bombs. Its operating efficiency is being continually improved. Research work is continuing although on a reduced scale
电磁分离厂在1944—1945年冬处于大规模运行状态，生产出了纯度足以用于原子弹的铀-235。其运行效率正在不断提高。研究工作仍在继续，尽管规模有所缩小

### SUMMARY 小结

11.43. In the early days of the uranium project, electromagnetic methods of isotope separation were rejected primarily because of the expected effects of space-charge. In the fall of 1941 the question was reopened; experiments at Berkeley showed that space-charge effects could be largely overcome. Consequently a large-scale program for the development of electromagnetic methods was undertaken.
在铀项目的早期，电磁同位素分离方法主要由于预期的空间电荷效应而被否定。1941年秋，这一问题被重新提起；伯克利的实验表明，空间电荷效应可以在很大程度上被克服。因此，一项发展电磁方法的大规模计划得以开展。

11.44. Of the various types of electromagnetic methods proposed, the calutron (developed at Berkeley) received principal attention. Two other novel methods were studied, one at Berkeley and one at Princeton. The calutron mass separator consists of an ion source from which a beam of uranium ions is drawn by an electric field, an accelerating system in which the ions are accelerated to high velocities, a magnetic field in which the ions travel in semicircles of radia, depending on ion mass, and a receiving system. The principal problems of this method involved the ion source, accelerating system, divergence of the ion beam, space charge, and utilization of the magnetic field. The chief advantages of the calutron were large separation factor, small hold-up, short start-up time, and flexibility of operation. By the fall of 1942 sufficient progress had been made to justify authorization of plant construction, and a year later the first plant units were ready for trial at the Clinton Engineer Works in Tennessee
在所提出的各种电磁方法中，卡卢特隆（在伯克利研制）受到主要关注。另外两种新颖方法得到研究，一种在伯克利，一种在普林斯顿。卡卢特隆质量分离器由一个离子源（由电场从中引出铀离子束）、一个将离子加速到高速度的加速系统、一个使离子沿半径取决于离子质量的半圆路径运动的磁场，以及一个接收系统组成。这种方法的主要问题涉及离子源、加速系统、离子束发散、空间电荷和磁场利用。卡卢特隆的主要优点是大分离因子、滞留量小、启动时间短和运行灵活。到1942年秋，已取得足够进展，足以证明批准建厂是合理的；一年后，首批工厂装置已在田纳西克林顿工程厂准备试运行

11.45. Research and development work on the calutron were carried out principally at the Radiation Laboratory of the University of California, under the direction of Lawrence. Westinghouse, General Electric, and Allis Chalmers constructed a majority of the parts; Stone and Webster built the plant, and Tennessee Eastman operated it.
卡卢特隆的研究与发展工作主要在加利福尼亚大学辐射实验室在劳伦斯的指导下进行。西屋、通用电气和阿利斯-查默斯制造了大部分部件；斯通-韦伯斯特建造了工厂，田纳西伊士曼负责运营。

11.46. Since the calutron separation method was one of batch operations in a large number of largely independent units, it was possible to introduce important improvements even after plant operation had begun.
由于卡卢特隆分离方法是在大量基本独立的装置中进行批式操作，因此即使在工厂开始运行之后也有可能引入重要改进。

11.47. In the summer of 1944 a thermal-diffusion separation plant was built at the Clinton Engineer Works to furnish enriched feed material for the electromagnetic plant and thereby increase the production rate of this latter plant. The design of the thermal diffusion plant was based on the results of research carried out at the Naval Research Laboratory and on the pilot plant built by the Navy Department at the Philadelphia Navy Yard.
1944年夏，在克林顿工程厂建造了一座热扩散分离厂，为电磁分离厂提供浓缩原料，从而提高后者的生产率。热扩散厂的设计基于海军研究实验室所进行的研究成果以及海军部在费城海军船厂建造的中间试验厂。

11.48. Although research work on the calutron was started later than on the centrifuge and diffusion systems, the calutron plant was the first to produce large amounts of the separated isotopes of uranium.
尽管卡卢特隆的研究工作开始得比离心法和扩散法晚，但卡卢特隆工厂最先生产出大量分离的铀同位素。
